线光明） 。
6. An apple a day keeps the doctor away.
一天一苹果，医生远离我。
7. A stitch in time saves nine. 小洞不补，
大洞吃苦（及时处理，事半功倍） 。
8. The grass is always greener on the
other side. 这山望着那山高。
9. Beauty is in the eye of the beholder.
情人眼里出西施。
10. A penny saved is a penny earned. 省一
分就是赚一分。
11. Rome wasn't built in a day. 罗马不是一
天建成的（冰冻三尺非一日之寒） 。
12. Out of sight, out of mind. 眼不见，心不
烦。
13. Barking dogs seldom bite. 吠犬不咬人。
14. You can lead a horse to water, but you
can't make it drink. 引马河边易，逼马饮
水难（师傅领进门，修行靠个人） 。
15. One man's meat is another man's
poison. 甲之蜜糖，乙之砒霜。
16. It's never too old to learn. 活到老，学
到老。
17. There's no smoke without fire. 无风不
起浪。
18. Practice what you preach. 言行一致。
19. Don't put all your eggs in one basket.
别把所有的鸡蛋放在一个篮子里（不要孤注
一掷） 。
20. Where there's a will, there's a way. 有
志者事竟成。
21. A fall into a pit, a gain in your wit. 吃
一堑，长一智。
22. As you sow, so shall you reap. 种瓜得
瓜，种豆得豆。
23. Easy come, easy go. 来得容易去得快。
24. First come, first served. 先到先得。
25. Kill two birds with one stone. 一石二鸟；
一箭双雕。
26. Let the cat out of the bag. 泄露秘密。
27. Burn the midnight oil. 开夜车。
28. Make hay while the sun shines. 趁热打
铁（把握时机） 。
29. A good beginning is half done. 良好的
开端是成功的一半。
30. All's well that ends well. 结果好，一切
都好。
31. All work and no play makes Jack a dull
boy. 只工作不玩耍，聪明孩子也变傻。
32. The early bird catches the worm. 早起
的鸟儿有虫吃。
33. Honesty is the best policy. 诚实为上策。
34. Strike while the iron is hot. 趁热打铁。
35. All good things must come to an end.
天下无不散之筵席（好事总有尽头） 。
36. A picture is worth a thousand words.
一图胜千言。
37. Don't cry wolf. 别喊狼来了 （别发假警报） 。
38. That's food for thought. 这值得深思。
39. It's a storm in a teacup. 小题大做。
40. You are what you eat. 人如其食 （饮食可
反映一个人的性格与生活环境） 。
41. Many hands make light work. 众人拾
柴火焰高。
42. Don't put the cart before the horse. 不
要本末倒置。
43. Look before you leap. 三思而后行。
44. Once bitten, twice shy. 一朝被蛇咬，十
年怕井绳。
45. Don't put off until tomorrow what
you can do today. 今日事，今日毕。
46. A miss is as good as a mile. 失之毫厘，
谬以千里。
47. Two heads are better than one. 三个臭
皮匠，顶个诸葛亮。
48. The more, the merrier. 人越多越热闹
（多多益善） 。
49. When the going gets tough, the tough
get going. 艰难之路，唯勇者行。
50. Great minds think alike. 英雄所见略同。
51. It takes two to tango. 一个巴掌拍不响。
52. The proof of the pudding is in the
eating. 实践出真知。
53. Variety is the spice of life. 变化是生活
的调味品（丰富多样使生活更精彩） 。
54. A rolling stone gathers no moss. 滚石
不生苔，转业不聚财（比喻经常变换工作或
生活方式的人不会有所积累） 。
55. You can't judge a tree by its bark. 不
能以貌取人 （与 “don't judge a book by
its cover” 类似表达） 。
56. The pen is mightier than the sword. 笔
诛胜于剑伐（文字的力量胜过武力） 。
57. All that glitters is not gold. 闪光的未必
都是金子。
58. A chain is no stronger than its
weakest link. 一着不慎，满盘皆输（链条
的坚固程度取决于它最薄弱的环节） 。
59. Don't bite the hand that feeds you. 不
要恩将仇报。
60. Clothes make the man. 人靠衣装。
61. Give a dog a bad name and hang him.
欲加之罪，何患无辞。
62. He who laughs last laughs best. 谁笑
到最后，谁笑得最好。
63. If you can't beat them, join them. 打不
过，就加入他们。
64. In for a penny, in for a pound. 一不做，
二不休。
65. It's no use crying over spilt milk. 覆水
难收，哭也没用。
66. Justice has long arms. 天网恢恢， 疏而不
漏。
67. Knowledge is power. 知识就是力量。
68. Laugh and the world laughs with you,
weep and you weep alone. 笑，世界与
你同笑；哭，你独自去哭。
69. Least said, soonest mended. 少说为佳，
言多必失。
70. Life is not all roses. 人生并非处处鸟语花
香（人生并非一帆风顺） 。
71. Like father, like son. 有其父必有其子。
72. Little strokes fell great oaks. 水滴石穿。
73. Live and let live. 自己活， 也让别人活 （待
人宽容如待己） 。
74. Lookers-on see most of the game. 旁
观者清。
75. Man proposes, God disposes. 谋事在
人，成事在天。
76. Many a little makes a mickle. 积少成多。
77. Necessity is the mother of invention.
需求是发明之母。
78. Never do things by halves. 做事不要半
途而废。
79. Never hit a man when he's down. 不要
落井下石。
80. Never judge from appearances. 不可
以貌取人。
81. No news is good news. 没有消息就是好
消息。
82. No pains, no gains. 一分耕耘， 一分收获。
83. No rose without a thorn. 没有不带刺的
玫瑰（世上没有十全十美的事） 。
84. Nothing ventured, nothing gained. 不
入虎穴，焉得虎子。
85. One good turn deserves another. 善有
善报。
86. One swallow does not make a summer.
一燕不成夏（不可仅凭个别现象而下判断） 。
87. Out of the frying pan into the fire. 刚
出虎口，又入狼窝。
88. Practice makes perfect. 熟能生巧。
89. Prevention is better than cure. 预防胜
于治疗（防患于未然） 。
90. Pride goes before a fall. 骄兵必败。
91. Put the cart before the horse. 本末倒
置。
92. Rome was not built in a day. 罗马非一
日建成（伟业非一日之功） 。
93. Seeing is believing. 眼见为实。
94. Share and share alike. 有福同享， 有难同
当。
95. Slow and steady wins the race. 稳扎稳
打，无往不胜（慢工出细活） 。
96. Strike while the iron is hot. 趁热打铁。
97. The die is cast. 木已成舟（事已决定，无
法更改） 。
98. The end justifies the means. 只要目的
正当，可以不择手段（此观点具有一定争议
性 ）。
99. The eye is bigger than the belly. 眼馋
肚饱 （形容人贪心， 想要的多但实际吃不下） 。
100. The grass is always greener on the
other side of the fence. 这山望着那山高。
101. The leopard cannot change its spots.
江山易改，本性难移。
102. The more haste, the less speed. 欲速
则不达。
103. The pot calls the kettle black. 五十步
笑百步。
104. There's no place like home. 金窝银窝，
不如自家草窝。
105. There's no smoke without fire. 无风
不起浪。
106. Time and tide wait for no man. 时不
我待。
107. Time flies. 时光飞逝。
108. To kill two birds with one stone. 一箭
双雕；一举两得。
109. Too many cooks spoil the broth. 厨子
太多煮坏汤（人多手杂反坏事） 。
110. Two wrongs do not make a right. 别
人错了，不等于你对了（不能以错纠错） 。
111. Walls have ears. 隔墙有耳。
112. Waste not, want not. 不浪费， 不愁缺。
113. When in Rome, do as the Romans do.
入乡随俗。
114. Where there's a will, there's a way. 有
志者事竟成。
115. While the cat's away, the mice will
play. 猫儿不在，老鼠翻天。
116. You can lead a horse to water, but
you can't make it drink. 牵马到河易，强
马饮水
117. You can't have your cake and eat it
too. 鱼与熊掌不可兼得。
118. You reap what you sow. 种瓜得瓜，种
豆得豆。
119. A bad workman always blames his
tools. 拙匠总怪工具差。
120. A bird in the hand is worth two in the
bush. 一鸟在手胜过双鸟在林。
121. A burnt child dreads the fire. 一朝被
蛇咬，十年怕井绳。
122. A constant guest is never welcome.
常客招人嫌。
123. A fair face may hide a foul heart. 笑
里藏刀；人面兽心。
124. A friend is easier lost than found. 得
朋友难，失朋友易。
125. A good medicine tastes bitter. 良药苦
口。
126. A house divided against itself cannot
stand. 家和万事兴；内讧则败。
127. A little knowledge is a dangerous
thing. 一知半解，自欺欺人。
128. A man is known by his friends. 什么
人交什么朋友。
129. A new broom sweeps clean. 新官上任
三把火。
130. A stitch in time saves nine. 小洞不补，
大洞吃苦。
131. A word spoken is past recalling. 一言
既出，驷马难追。
132. Absence makes the heart grow
fonder. 小别胜新婚；久别情更深。
133. Accidents will happen. 天有不测风云。
134. Actions speak louder than words. 行
动胜于言辞。
135. Advice when most needed is least
heeded. 忠言逆耳。
136. After a storm comes a calm. 雨过天晴。
137. All is fair in love and war. 情场战场，
不择手段。
138. All is well that ends well. 结果好，一切
都好。
139. All roads lead to Rome. 条条大路通罗
马。
140. All that glitters is not gold. 闪光的未
必都是金子。
141. All things are difficult before they
are easy. 万事开头难。
142. All work and no play makes Jack a
dull boy. 只工作，不玩耍，聪明孩子也变
傻。
143. An idle youth, a needy age. 少壮不努
力，老大徒伤悲。
144. As the tree, so the fruit. 种瓜得瓜，种
豆得豆。
145. As you sow, so shall you reap. 一分耕
耘，一分收获。
146. Bad news travels fast. 坏事传千里。
147. Beauty is in the eye of the beholder.
情人眼里出西施。
148. Beggars can't be choosers. 饥不择食；
行乞者不得有选择。
149. Better be the head of a dog than the
tail of a lion. 宁为鸡头，不为凤尾。
150. Better late than never. 迟做总比不做
好。
151. Better safe than sorry. 宁可事先谨慎
有余，不要事后追悔莫及。
152. Between two stools one falls to the
ground. 脚踏两条船， 迟早要落水 （两者皆
选，一无所得） 。
153. Birds of a feather flock together. 物
以类聚，人以群分。
154. Blood is thicker than water. 血浓于水。
155. Brevity is the soul of wit. 言以简洁为
贵。
156. Business is business. 公事公办。
157. Business is the salt of life. 事业是人生
的第一需要。
158. Call a spade a spade. 直言不讳； 实事求
是。
159. Cannot see the wood for the trees.
一叶障目，不见泰山。
160. Care and diligence bring luck. 谨慎和
勤奋才能抓住机遇。
161. Caution is the parent of safety. 小心
驶得万年船。
162. Cheats never prosper. 骗人发不了财。
163. Children are what the mothers are.
耳濡目染，身教言传。
164. Choose an author as you choose a
friend. 择书如择友。
165. Christmas comes but once a year. 佳
节良辰，机会难得。
166. Circumstances alter cases. 具体情况具
体分析。
167. Cleanliness is next to godliness. 清洁
近乎美德。
168. Clumsy birds have to start flying
early. 笨鸟先飞。
169. Constant dripping wears away the
stone. 水滴石穿，绳锯木断。
170. Content is better than riches. 知足者
常乐。
171. Count one's chickens before they are

172. Courtesy on one side only lasts not
long. 来而不往非礼也。
173. Creep before you walk. 循序渐进。
174. Cry for the moon. 海底捞月；想做做不

175. Custom is a second nature. 习惯是后
天养成的。
176. Custom makes all things easy. 有个好
习惯，事事皆不难。
177. Cut the coat according to the cloth.
量体裁衣。
178. Danger is next neighbor to security.
塞翁失马，焉知非福。
179. Dead men tell no tales. 死人不会告密。
180. Death is the great leveller. 死亡面前，
人人平等。
