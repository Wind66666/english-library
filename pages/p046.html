接待国外客户
无非就这800句
一、行程确认与规划
How about your schedule for this business trip?（您这次商务出行的日程安排是怎样的？）
Where are the key places you plan to visit during your stay?（您停留期间计划前往的重点地点有哪些？）
Do you need me to help organize your daily itinerary?（需要我帮您整理每日行程吗？）
Can you share your approximate arrival and departure times?（您能告知大致的抵达和离开时间吗？）
Will your schedule include any free time for sightseeing?（您的行程里会有空闲时间用于游览吗？）
How many days do you plan to stay in our city?（您计划在我们这座城市停留几天？）
Are there specific time slots you prefer for meetings?（您对会议时间有特定偏好的时段吗？）
Do you have any prior commitments that might affect the itinerary?（您有其他可能影响行程的既定安排吗？）
Would you like to adjust the meeting time to fit your schedule better?（您想调整会议时间以更契合您的日程吗？）
Can you confirm if the proposed itinerary works for you?（您能确认拟定的行程是否符合您的需求吗？）
How many people will be part of your visiting group?（您的来访团队共有多少人？）
Are there any special requirements for the group members' arrangements?（团队成员的安排有特殊需求吗？）
Do we need to prepare separate accommodations for some members?（需要为部分成员准备单独住宿吗？）
Will there be any VIPs in the visiting group who need extra care?（来访团队中有需要特殊关照的贵宾吗？）
Can you tell us the job roles of the group members?（您能告知团队成员的工作职责吗？）
Are there any language preferences among the visiting team?（来访团队成员有语言偏好吗？）
Do we need to arrange translators for certain members?（需要为部分成员安排翻译吗？）
Will the group travel together or split up for some activities?（团队会集体行动，还是部分活动分开进行？）
Can you confirm the final number of visiting personnel?（您能确认来访人员的最终人数吗？）
The itinerary I drafted is here—does it meet your expectations?（我拟定的行程表在这儿，符合您的预期吗？）
I can revise the itinerary according to your feedback.（我可以根据您的反馈修改行程。）
Does the order of activities in the itinerary make sense to you?（行程中活动的顺序您觉得合理吗？）
Are there any activities in the itinerary you want to add or remove?（行程里有您想增加或删除的活动吗？）
Will the proposed meeting duration work for your team?（拟定的会议时长对您的团队合适吗？）
Do you need us to leave more buffer time between activities?（需要我们在活动之间预留更多缓冲时间吗？）
Can we adjust the itinerary to avoid rush-hour traffic?（我们能调整行程以避开交通高峰期吗？）
Is there any place you want to add to the itinerary last minute?（您临时有想添加到行程里的地点吗？）
Do you prefer morning or afternoon for factory visits?（您更喜欢上午还是下午去工厂参观？）
Will you have any business meetings with other companies during your stay?（您停留期间会和其他公司有商务会议吗？）
Can you inform us if your travel plans change unexpectedly?（若行程突发变动，您能告知我们吗？）
Do you need help booking local transportation for your itinerary?（需要我们帮您预订行程中的本地交通吗？）
Are there any time-sensitive tasks that need to be included in the itinerary?（有需要纳入行程的时效性任务吗？）
Will you need to prepare any materials before specific activities in the itinerary?（行程中特定活动前，您需要准备材料吗？）
Can we schedule a brief call to finalize the itinerary?（我们能安排简短通话敲定行程吗？）
Does the itinerary allow enough time for rest during your trip?（行程给您预留了足够的休息时间吗？）
Are there any cultural activities you want to experience in the itinerary?（您想在行程中体验哪些文化活动？）
Will the itinerary be shared with other members of your team?（行程会分享给您团队的其他成员吗？）
Do you need a printed copy of the itinerary for each group member?（需要为每位团队成员准备行程打印件吗？）
Can you confirm if the itinerary aligns with your overall business goals?（您能确认行程是否符合您的整体业务目标吗？）
二、饮食偏好与餐厅选择
Are there any types of food you don't eat due to dietary restrictions?（因饮食限制，您有忌口的食物吗？）
Do you have any allergies to specific ingredients?（您对特定食材过敏吗？）
Are you a vegetarian or have any special eating habits?（您是素食者，或有特殊饮食习惯吗？）
Do you prefer spicy, mild, or sweet flavors?（您偏好辣、清淡还是甜的口味？）
Are there any cuisines you particularly enjoy?（您有特别喜欢的菜系吗？）
Do you avoid any food for religious reasons?（因宗教信仰，您有避免食用的食物吗？）
Are you okay with seafood in the restaurant menu?（餐厅菜单中的海鲜您能接受吗？）
Do you have a preference for local or international restaurants?（您偏好本地餐厅还是国际餐厅？）
Are you looking for a casual or formal dining environment?（您想要休闲还是正式的用餐环境？）
Do you have any requirements for the restaurant's location?（您对餐厅位置有要求吗？）
Are there any restaurants you've heard of that you want to try?（您有听说过想尝试的餐厅吗？）
Do you prefer buffets, set meals, or a la carte options?（您偏好自助餐、套餐还是单点？）
Are you okay with eating at a restaurant with a busy atmosphere?（您能接受在热闹的餐厅用餐吗？）
Do you have any preferences for the restaurant's price range?（您对餐厅的价位有偏好吗？）
Are there any local specialties you want to try during your meal?（用餐时您想尝试哪些本地特色菜？）
Do you avoid food that's too greasy or fried?（您会避免过于油腻或油炸的食物吗？）
Are you okay with restaurants that serve alcohol?（您能接受提供酒精饮品的餐厅吗？）
Do you have a preference for indoor or outdoor seating?（您偏好室内还是室外座位？）
Are there any specific times you prefer to have meals?（您有偏好的用餐时间吗？）
Do you need the restaurant to accommodate a large group?（需要餐厅能容纳多人团队吗？）
Are you interested in trying traditional local breakfast?（您有兴趣尝试传统本地早餐吗？）
Do you prefer light or hearty lunches during workdays?（工作日午餐您偏好清淡还是丰盛的？）
Are you looking for a quiet restaurant for business dinners?（商务晚餐您想要安静的餐厅吗？）
Do you have any requirements for the restaurant's hygiene standards?（您对餐厅的卫生标准有要求吗？）
Are you okay with trying new and unique food items?（您愿意尝试新奇的食物吗？）
Do you prefer restaurants with private dining rooms?（您偏好有包间的餐厅吗？）
Are there any food ingredients you dislike that we should note?（有您不喜欢、需我们留意的食材吗？）
Do you need the restaurant to provide vegetarian options?（需要餐厅提供素食选择吗？）
Are you interested in pairing local drinks with your meal?（您有兴趣搭配本地饮品用餐吗？）
Do you have a preference for family-style or individual servings?（您偏好分餐制还是合餐制？）
Are you okay with restaurants that have a waiting time?（您能接受需要等位的餐厅吗？）
Do you need the restaurant to be wheelchair-accessible?（需要餐厅有无障碍设施吗？）
Are there any specific cooking methods you prefer for your food?（您对食物的烹饪方式有偏好吗？）
Do you avoid food with strong odors?（您会避免有刺激性气味的食物吗？）
Are you interested in trying a themed restaurant?（您有兴趣尝试主题餐厅吗？）
Do you have any preferences for the restaurant's ambiance?（您对餐厅的氛围有偏好吗？）
Are you okay with restaurants that use organic ingredients?（您能接受使用有机食材的餐厅吗？）
Do you need the restaurant to offer takeout options if needed?（若有需要，您需要餐厅提供外带服务吗？）
Are there any local snacks you want to try besides main meals?（除正餐外，您想尝试哪些本地小吃？）
三、会议室布置准备
Have we prepared enough snacks for the meeting attendees?（我们为参会人员准备了足够的零食吗？）
Are the gifts for clients placed neatly on the conference table?（给客户的礼品有整齐摆放在会议桌上吗？）
Have all the meeting documents been printed and organized?（所有会议文件都已打印整理好吗？）
Is the voice recorder fully charged and ready to use?（录音笔已充满电并准备好使用了吗？）
Has the projector been tested to ensure it works properly?（投影仪已测试，确保能正常使用吗？）
Are there enough pens for each client to use during the meeting?（有足够的笔供每位客户会议中使用吗？）
Has a product catalog been prepared for every client?（为每位客户都准备了产品目录吗？）
Is the conference room's air conditioning set to a comfortable temperature?（会议室空调已调至舒适温度吗？）
Have the chairs around the conference table been arranged neatly?（会议桌周围的椅子已摆放整齐吗？）
Is the whiteboard or flipchart ready with markers and erasers?（白板或活动挂图已备好马克笔和板擦吗？）
Have we prepared enough drinking water for the meeting?（我们为会议准备了足够的饮用水吗？）
Is the conference room's lighting bright enough for the presentation?（会议室灯光亮度足够支持演示吗？）
Has the laptop connected to the projector been checked?（连接投影仪的笔记本电脑已检查过吗？）
Are there any extension cords prepared in case of power needs?（已准备好延长线以防用电需求吗？）
Has the meeting agenda been printed and distributed to each seat?（会议议程已打印并分发到每个座位吗？）
Is the conference room's Wi-Fi connection stable and accessible?（会议室 Wi-Fi 连接稳定且可使用吗？）
Have we prepared name tags for all meeting participants?（我们为所有参会者准备了姓名牌吗？）
Is the conference room door labeled clearly for clients to find?（会议室门有清晰标识，方便客户找到吗？）
Has the trash bin in the conference room been emptied?（会议室垃圾桶已清理干净吗？）
Are there any tissues placed on the conference table for use?（会议桌上有摆放纸巾供使用吗？）
Has the sound system in the conference room been tested?（会议室音响系统已测试过吗？）
Is there a backup projector bulb prepared in case of failure?（已准备备用投影仪灯泡以防故障吗？）
Have we prepared a power bank for emergency device charging?（已准备充电宝供设备应急充电吗？）
Is the conference table clean and free of any unnecessary items?（会议桌干净且无多余物品吗？）
Are there any plants placed in the conference room for a better ambiance?（会议室有摆放绿植以提升氛围吗？）
Has the meeting start time been displayed in the conference room?（会议室有显示会议开始时间吗？）
Is there a first-aid kit prepared near the conference room?（会议室附近有准备急救箱吗？）
Have we confirmed the number of attendees to adjust the setup?（已确认参会人数以调整布置吗？）
Is the conference room's background music set to a low volume?（会议室背景音乐已调至低音量吗？）
Has the window in the conference room been cleaned for a clear view?（会议室窗户已清洁，视野清晰吗？）
Are there any blankets prepared in case clients feel cold?（已准备毯子以防客户觉得冷吗？）
Has the coffee or tea been prepared and kept warm?（咖啡或茶已准备好并保温吗？）
Is the remote control for the projector and TV easily accessible?（投影仪和电视遥控器容易获取吗？）
Have we prepared a list of emergency contacts for the meeting?（已准备会议应急联系人名单吗？）
Is the conference room's ventilation system working well?（会议室通风系统运行良好吗？）
Are there any promotional materials placed on the conference table?（会议桌上有摆放宣传资料吗？）
Has the seating arrangement been planned according to client roles?（已根据客户身份规划座位安排吗？）
Is there a clock in the conference room for time management?（会议室有时钟方便掌控时间吗？）
Have we checked the conference room for any potential safety hazards?（已检查会议室是否有安全隐患吗？）
