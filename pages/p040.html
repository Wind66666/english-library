Could you say that last part again? I want to make sure I get it right.
（您能把最后那部分再说一遍吗？我想确认自己听明白了。）
Sorry, I didn’t catch what you said about the project schedule. Can you explain a little more?
（抱歉，我没听清您说的项目进度相关内容。您能再多解释一下吗？）
When you talk about the new rules, could you slow down a bit? I need to take notes.
（您讲新规定的时候，能慢一点吗？我得记笔记。）
The data you showed just now is a bit confusing. How did you get these numbers?
（您刚才展示的数据有点让人困惑。这些数字是怎么来的呀？）
Sorry, I forgot what the main goal of this stage is. Could you remind me?
（抱歉，我忘了这个阶段的主要目标是什么了。您能提醒我一下吗？）
When you mentioned working with the marketing team, what specific jobs do we need to do?
（您提到和市场部合作，我们需要做哪些具体的工作呢？）
I don’t really get the logic behind this solution. Can you walk me through it step by step?
（我不太明白这个解决方案背后的逻辑。您能一步步给我讲清楚吗？）
Can you rephrase that sentence? I’m not familiar with the words you used.
（您能换种说法吗？您用的那些词我不太熟悉。）
Sorry, the internet is unstable, and I didn’t hear the end of your speech. Could you repeat it?
（抱歉，网络不太稳定，我没听到您发言的最后部分。您能再讲一遍吗？）
When you talked about adjusting the budget, I didn’t hear how much we can give to the R&D department. Can you confirm that?
（您讲调整预算的时候，我没听清能给研发部门多少钱。您能确认一下吗？）
I need a minute to process the info you just shared. Can we pause for 30 seconds before moving on?
（我需要点时间消化您刚分享的信息。我们能暂停 30 秒再继续吗？）
Sorry, a sudden message distracted me. Did you talk about the client meeting next week?
（抱歉，一条突然的消息让我分神了。您提到下周的客户会议了吗？）
Can you highlight the key points of our discussion so far? I don’t want to miss anything important.
（您能强调一下到目前为止我们讨论的重点吗？我不想错过重要内容。）
I’m not sure when the report is due. Did you say it’s the end of this week or next week?
（我不确定报告什么时候要交。您说是这周末还是下周末来着？）
When you said “priority tasks”, which ones are the most important?
（您说 “优先任务” 的时候，哪些是最要紧的呀？）
Sorry, I’m still trying to understand the new workflow. Can you give an example?
（抱歉，我还在努力理解这个新的工作流程。您能举个例子吗？）
Can you speak a little louder? There’s background noise, and I can’t hear you well.
（您能说大声一点吗？这里有背景噪音，我听不太清。）
I didn’t hear who’s in charge of the follow-up work. Can you tell me?
（我没听清谁负责后续工作。您能告诉我吗？）
Sorry, I’m a bit behind on this topic. Can you briefly recap what was discussed before I joined?
（抱歉，我对这个话题有点跟不上。您能简要回顾一下我来之前讨论的内容吗？）
When you talked about risk factors, I didn’t get how we can reduce them. Can you explain the steps?
（您讲风险因素的时候，我没明白我们该怎么降低风险。您能解释一下步骤吗？）
I’m stuck on how to respond to the client’s request mentioned in the meeting. Does anyone have suggestions?
（我不知道该怎么回应会议上提到的客户需求。有人有建议吗？）
Sorry, my mind went blank for a second. Can you repeat the question you asked me?
（抱歉，我刚才脑子突然一片空白。您能重复一下问我的问题吗？）
I can’t express my thoughts clearly right now. Can I have a minute to organize them?
（我现在没法清楚地表达我的想法。能给我一分钟整理一下吗？）
When I try to talk about my task progress, I don’t know which details to focus on. Can you give me a hint?
（我想讲自己的任务进度，却不知道该重点说哪些细节。您能给我点提示吗？）
I’m stuck because I don’t have the latest data to support my point. Can someone give it to me quickly?
（我卡壳了，因为没有最新数据支撑我的观点。有人能快点给我吗？）
Sorry, I can’t find the right words to describe the problem we’re facing. Can you help me rephrase it?
（抱歉，我找不到合适的词描述我们遇到的问题。您能帮我换种说法吗？）
I can’t decide whether to propose Option A or Option B. Can we talk about their pros and cons again?
（我拿不定主意该提议方案 A 还是方案 B。我们能再聊聊它们的优缺点吗？）
When I share feedback, I’m worried I’ll sound too negative. Can you tell me how to say it more constructively?
（分享反馈的时候，我担心自己听起来太消极。您能告诉我怎么说更有建设性吗？）
I’m stuck because I need to check some old records first. Can I come back to this after the meeting?
（我卡壳了，因为得先查一些旧记录。我能会后再谈这个问题吗？）
Sorry, I can’t connect my point to the overall agenda. Can you help me link them?
（抱歉，我没法把我的观点和整体议程联系起来。您能帮我串联一下吗？）
Oh, I didn’t expect to be called on. Let me quickly get my thoughts together.
（哦，我没料到会被点名。让我快点整理一下思路。）
Thanks for asking. In my opinion, the main challenge we’re facing is…
（谢谢提问。在我看来，我们面临的主要挑战是……）
I need to check my notes to give a correct answer. Can you give me 20 seconds?
（我得查下笔记才能给出准确答案。您能给我 20 秒吗？）
About this issue, I’ve been working on it with my team, and so far we’ve achieved…

I’m glad you brought this up. I think we should deal with it by…
（很高兴您提到这个问题。我觉得我们应该通过…… 来处理。）
I haven’t fully analyzed this part yet, but based on what I know now, I think…
（我还没完全分析这部分，但根据我现在了解的情况，我觉得……）
Let me start by sharing the progress of my recent work related to this topic.

This is a very important question. From what I’ve seen, the root cause might be…
（这是个很重要的问题。根据我的观察，根本原因可能是……）
I need to check with a colleague to confirm some details. Can I give a more complete response later?
（我得和同事确认些细节。我能之后再给出更完整的答复吗？）
Thanks for giving me the chance to share. I think the key to solving this problem is…
（谢谢给我分享的机会。我觉得解决这个问题的关键是……）
I’m still learning about this area, but I can share what I understand so far.
（我还在学习这个领域，但可以分享一下目前的理解。）
Let me think carefully. From a practical point of view, we can take these steps…
（让我仔细想想。从实际角度出发，我们可以采取这些步骤……）
I have some basic data related to this question, and I want to show it first.
（我有一些和这个问题相关的基础数据，想先展示一下。）
As someone who worked on the initial phase of the project, I can say that…
（作为参与过项目初始阶段的人，我可以说……）
Before I answer, I need to make sure. Do you mean…
（回答之前，我得确认一下。您是指…… 吗？）
This question reminds me of a similar situation we had before. Back then, we solved it by…

I’m not 100% sure about the answer, but I can look into it and get back to everyone by the end of the day.

Let me split my answer into two parts. First, about the current situation…

I’m sorry for the confusion in the last email. The correct info is as follows…
（很抱歉上一封邮件造成了混乱。正确的信息如下……）

（请忽略我 10 分钟前发给您的邮件。我在项目时间线上犯了错，修订版已经附上了。）
I’m really sorry for sending the incomplete report in the last email. Here’s the full and correct version.
（很抱歉上一封邮件里发了不完整的报告。这是完整且正确的版本。）
There was a typo in the contact info in the last email. The correct phone number is +86-XXX-XXXXXXX.
（上一封邮件的联系方式里有个拼写错误。正确的电话号码是 + 86-XXX-XXXXXXX。）
I apologize for the wrong data in the email I sent earlier. I’ve checked and updated it, and you can find the new file in the attachment.
（我为之前邮件里的错误数据道歉。我已经核对并更新了，您可以在附件里找到新文件。）
Please note that the email I sent at 9 a.m. has the wrong meeting venue. The correct place is Conference Room 3.
（请注意，我上午 9 点发的邮件里会议地点错了。正确的地点是 3 号会议室。）
I’m sorry for the confusion caused by the last email. The task deadline is this Friday, not next Friday.
（很抱歉上一封邮件造成了混乱。任务截止日期是这周五，不是下周五。）
I made a mistake in the recipient list of the last email. This one is only for you, and I’m sorry for the trouble.
（我在上一封邮件的收件人列表里犯了错。这封只给您一个人，给您带来麻烦很抱歉。）
I’m writing to correct the mistake in my earlier email. The project leader is Ms. Johnson, not Mr. Smith.
（我写这封邮件是为了更正之前的错误。项目负责人是约翰逊女士，不是史密斯先生。）
Please ignore the draft email I sent you by accident just now. The final version will be sent to you within an hour.
（请忽略我刚才不小心发给您的草稿邮件。最终版本会在一小时内发给您。）
I apologize for the wrong attachment in the last email. The correct report is attached now, and I’ve checked all the content.
