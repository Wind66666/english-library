职场幽默笑话·办公室篇（网络精选 中英对照） Funny Office Jokes with Chinese Translations 一、会议与视频篇 1. Why did the meeting go on forever? Because nobody wanted to be the one to end "one more thing." 会议为什么开个没完？因为谁都不想当那个终结"我再补充一点"的人。 2. Our meetings could have been emails, and our emails could have been silence. 我们的会议本可以是一封邮件，而那封邮件本可以是一段沉默。 3. I love meetings the way I love traffic jams — full of people going nowhere together. 我爱开会，就像我爱堵车——一大群人待在一起，哪儿也去不了。 4. Some meetings are like GPS with no signal — lots of talking, zero direction. 有些会议就像没信号的导航——说了无数句话，方向为零。 5. I've muted myself so many times, my microphone thinks it's on vacation. 我静音的次数太多，麦克风都以为自己休假去了。 6. "You're on mute" should be the official company motto by now. "你还在静音"这句话，到现在都够格当公司官方口号了。 7. My webcam angle makes me look like I'm reporting live from the ceiling. 我的摄像头角度，让人觉得我在天花板上做现场直播。 8. Half of every meeting is just people asking, "Can you see my screen?" 每场会议的一半时间，大家都在问同一个问题："能看见我的屏幕吗？" 二、咖啡续命篇 9. I like my coffee how I like my Mondays — strong and over quickly. 我对咖啡的要求和对周一一样——够浓，而且快点过去。 10. Decaf is just coffee that gave up on its dreams. 低因咖啡，就是梦想破灭了的咖啡。 11. Espresso yourself before someone else does it for you. 趁别人还没替你开口，先自己"浓缩"表达一番。 （espresso 谐音 express，双关） 12. My blood type is coffee-positive. 我的血型是"咖啡阳性"。 13. Without coffee, I'm just a very tired decision-maker. 没有咖啡，我只是一台非常疲惫的决策机器。 14. Somebody labeled their lunch "do not touch," which is corporate for "I don't trust any of you." 有人给自己的午餐贴上"勿动"标签，翻译成职场话就是："你们一个我都信不过。" 15. Break time is the only meeting I never want to end. 茶歇，是唯一一场我永远不想让它结束的会议。 三、邮件篇 16. My inbox has more unread messages than a mystery novel has plot twists. 我收件箱里的未读邮件，比悬疑小说的情节反转还多。 17. "Per my last email" is corporate for "read it this time." "如我上一封邮件所述"，职场翻译是："这次麻烦真的看一下。" 18. I marked 200 emails as read just to feel something. 我把两百封邮件标成已读，纯粹为了找点感觉。 19. Replying "noted" to an email is the professional version of a shrug emoji. 回复"收到"，就是专业版的耸肩表情。 20. Someone hit "reply all" and now the whole company knows about the printer jam. 有人手滑点了"回复全部"，于是全公司都知道打印机卡纸了。 21. CC'ing the boss is corporate for "I'm covering myself." 抄送老板，职场含义是："我先给自己留个证据。" 四、老板与同事篇 22. My boss told me to have a good day, so I went home. 老板让我"好好过一天"，于是我就回家了。 23. Why did the boss bring a ladder to work? To reach the next level. 老板为什么带梯子来上班？为了"更上一层楼"。 24. My coworker's desk is so messy, archaeologists want to study it. 我同事的桌子乱到考古学家都想来实地研究。 25. Nothing bonds coworkers faster than mutually complaining about the Wi-Fi. 没有什么比一起吐槽公司 Wi-Fi，更能快速拉近同事感情。 五、周末与日常篇 26. My weekend went by so fast, I think it filed for early retirement. 我的周末过得太快，我怀疑它申请了提前退休。 27. My alarm clock and I have a strictly professional relationship — we don't get along. 我和闹钟是纯粹的"工作关系"——我们处不来。 28. Deadlines are just really pushy calendar reminders. 截止日期，不过是态度特别强硬的日历提醒。 29. I changed my password to "incorrect" so the computer tells me when I'm wrong. 我把密码设成了 incorrect（错误），这样输错的时候，电脑就会提醒我：你的密码"错误"。 30. Why don't office chairs ever get tired? They're always well-rested. 办公椅为什么从来不累？因为它们永远在"歇着"。（well-rested 双关：休息充分 / 一直坐着） 六、冷笑话彩蛋篇 31. Why did the stapler get promoted? It really held things together. 订书机为什么升职了？因为它真的能把一切"凝聚起来"。（hold together 双关） 32. What do you call a printer that sings? A jam session. 会唱歌的打印机叫什么？——"卡纸演奏会"。（jam 双关：卡纸 / 即兴合奏）
