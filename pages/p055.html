700 essential English for foreign companies 外企必备500句职场英语 一、项目进度管理类​ （一）跟进项目​ Could you please update me on the current status of the X project?（你能跟我更新一下 X 项目的当前进展吗？）​ I’d like to follow up on the tasks we discussed in last week’s project meeting.（我想跟进一下上周项目会议中我们讨论的任务。）​ Let’s touch base on the project progress every Friday afternoon.（我们每周五下午碰一下项目进度吧。）​ Has there been any progress on the client approval for the project plan?（项目计划的客户审批方面有进展吗？）​ I need to check in on the development team’s progress with the new module.（我需要了解一下开发团队在新模块上的进展。）​ Could you share the latest project timeline with me?（你能把最新的项目时间表分享给我吗？） Let’s review the project milestones to see if we’re on track.（我们回顾一下项目里程碑，看看是否按计划进行。）​ I’m following up to see if the materials for the project have been want to follow up on the action items assigned to you in the project kickoff meeting.（我想跟进一下项目启动会议上分配给你的行动项。）​ Let’s discuss any obstacles that might be affecting the project progress.（我们讨论一下可能影响项目进度的任何障碍。）​ Has the project team resolved the issues identified in the last progress review?（项目团队是否解决了上次进度审查中发现的问题？）​ Could you confirm if the project deliverables for this month latest progress data.（我们把最新的进度数据更新到项目仪表板上吧。）​ Has there been any change in the project scope that might impact the timeline?（项目范围是否有任何变化，可能会影响时间表？）​ Could you walk me through the current progress of the key project tasks?（你能带我过一遍关键项目任务的当前进展吗？）​ I want to follow up on the feedback from the stakeholders regarding the project.（我想跟进一下利益相关者对项目的反馈。）​ Let’s assess whether we need to adjust the project plan based on the current progress.（我们根据当前进展评估一下是否需要调整项目计划。）​ （二）催同事进度​ Could you prioritize the X task and let me know when it will be completed?（你能优先处理 X 任务，并告诉我什么时候能完成吗？）​ The deadline for this Could you give it higher priority and update me soon?（这个任务已经搁置一段时间了，你能把它列为更高优先级，并尽快跟我更新吗？）​ We’re up against a tight deadline. Could you focus on completing this task first?（我们面临紧迫的截止日期，你能先专注完成这个任务吗？）​ I need to coordinate with other departments based on your progress. Could you provide an update today?（我需要根据你的进度与其他部门协调，你今天能给我一个更新吗？）​ The delay in your task might affect the entire project timeline. Could you take action immediately?（你任务的延迟可能会影响整个项目时间表，你能立即采取行动吗？）​ Could you share a detailed plan of how you’ll catch up on the delayed progress?（你能分享一个详细的计划，说明如何赶上延迟的进度吗？）​ We have a meeting with the client tomorrow to discuss the project. Could you finish your work before then?（我们明天要和客户开会讨论这个项目，你能在那之前完成工作吗？）​ （三）催问进度​ How is the progress coming along on the task you’re working on?（你正在做的任务进度怎么样了？）​ Could you let me know how far you’ve gotten with the X project?（你能告诉我你在 X 项目上进展到什么程度了吗？）​ What’s the current status of the work we assigned to you last week?（上周我们分配给你的工作目前是什么状态？）​ Have you made any headway on the client proposal?（你在客户提案方面有什么进展吗？）​ How much of the task has been completed so far?（到目前为止，这个任务完成了多少？）​ Could you give me a quick update on how the project is progressing?（你能快速跟我更新一下项目进展情况吗？）​ Are we on track to finish the task by the scheduled deadline?（我们能按预定截止日期完成任务吗？）​ What’s the latest with the work you’re responsible for?（你负责的工作最新情况如何？）​ Have you encountered any difficulties that are slowing down the progress?（你遇到什么困难导致进度变慢了吗？）​ Could you estimate how much more time you’ll need to complete the task?（你能估计一下还需要多少时间完成这个任务吗？）​ How is the work going on the new marketing campaign?（新营销活动的工作进展如何？）​ Are there any updates on the progress of the product development?（产品开发的进度有什么更新吗？）​ What percentage of the project has been accomplished up to now?（到目前为止，项目完成了百分之多少？）​ Could you inform me of the current progress of the data analysis task?（你能告知我数据分析任务的当前进度吗？）​ How close are you to finishing the report we need for the meeting?（你离完成我们会议所需的报告还有多远？）​ （四）催问项目进度并汇报​ I’ve checked with the team and wanted to ask about the project progress; so far, 60% of the tasks have been completed, and I’ll report this to the manager later.（我已经和团队沟通过，想了解一下项目进度，目前 60% 的任务已完成，之后我会向经理汇报。）​ Could you update me on the project progress first? After that, I’ll draft a report and share it with the stakeholders.（我们一起过一下项目进度，之后我会起草一份报告，分享给利益相关者。）​ Could you provide the detailed project progress data? I need to integrate it into the weekly report and submit it to the department head.（你能提供详细的项目进度数据吗？我需要把它整合到周报中，提交给部门主管。）​ I’m checking the project progress to report at the upcoming team meeting; please highlight any key achievements and challenges.（我正在了解项目进度，以便在即将到来的团队会议上汇报，请重点说明关键成果和挑战。）​ First, could you let me know how the project is going? I have to report the progress to the project steering committee next week.（首先，你能告诉我项目进展如何吗？我下周要向项目指导委员会汇报进度。）​ I need to ask about the project progress first, then summarize it and report to the CEO during the quarterly review.（我需要先了解项目进度，然后进行总结，在季度审查时向首席执行官汇报。）​ Could you share the project progress update? I’ll incorporate it into the progress report and send it to all team members.（你能分享项目进度更新吗？我会把它纳入进度报告，发送给所有团队成员。）​ I’m following up on the project progress to report to the client’s project manager; please make sure the information is accurate and up-to-date.（我正在跟进项目进度，以便向客户的项目经理汇报，请确保信息准确且最新。）​ （五）项目需要延迟​ Due to unexpected supply chain issues, we need to delay the project delivery by two two months while we find a replacement.（由于一名关键团队成员离职，在我们寻找替代者期间，项目需要延迟两个月。）​ We need to postpone the project deadline by one week to complete the final revisions requested by the stakeholders.（我们需要将项目截止日期推迟一周，以完成利益相关者要求的最终修订。）​ After assessing the impact of the recent policy changes, we’ve decided to delay the project until the situation of the project, so we need to prioritize it above all other tasks.（客户要求紧急交付项目，所以我们需要把它列为所有任务中的优先事项。）​ This is a high-priority urgent project, and all team members need to focus on it urgent due to a sudden change in the market, and we need to accelerate the process immediately.（由于市场突然变化，这个项目变得紧急，我们需要立即加快进程。）​ This is an urgent project that requires us to mobilize all available resources to complete it on time.（这是一个紧急项目，需要我们调动所有可用资源按时完成。）​ The senior management has labeled this project as urgent, so we need to adjust our work plans accordingly.（高层管理已将这个项目定为紧急项目，所以我们需要相应调整工作计划。）​ We have an urgent project deadline approaching, and every minute counts in ensuring its completion.（我们有一个紧急的项目截止日期即将到来，要确保完成，分秒必争。）​ This project is urgent, and we need to hold an emergency meeting right now to discuss the action plan.（这个项目很紧急，我们需要立即召开紧急会议讨论行动计划。）​ Due to the urgent nature of this project, we need to reallocate staff from other teams to support it.（由于这个项目的紧急性，我们需要从其他团队重新调配人员来支持它。）​ （七）项目没了​ I just received the news that the X project has been canceled due to budget cuts.（我刚收到消息，由于预算削减，X 项目已经取消了。）​ The company has decided to terminate the project as it no longer aligns with our strategic goals.（公司决定终止这个项目，因为它不再符合我们的战略目标。）​ We’ve been informed that the client has pulled the plug on the project, so we need to stop all project we’ve been working on has been terminated unexpectedly.（我很遗憾地通知你，我们一直在做的这个项目意外终止了。）​ The project has been abandoned after the initial feasibility study showed it wasn’t economically facing difficulties with the integration of the new software system.（这个项目在新软件系统的集成方面遇到了困难。）​ We’re encountering challenges in securing the necessary permits for the project.（我们在为项目获取必要许可方面面临挑战。）​ The replaced with an email.（我听说有些团队成员觉得我们的会议没必要，发一封邮件就能替代） Team members have been saying that the meeting agendas are always unclear or sent too late.（团队成员一直在说，会议议程总是不清晰，或者发得太晚。）​ There’s feedback that Let’s table the non-urgent discussions and follow up later.（我们已经超出会议时间了。非紧急的讨论先搁置，之后再跟进。）​ I’m afraid we’re running over. Let’s summarize the key takeaways and end the meeting now.（恐怕我们已经超时了。让我们总结一下关键要点，现在就结束会议吧。）​ We’ve used up all the allocated time. Let’s schedule a follow-up meeting to cover the remaining items.（我们已经用完了所有分配的时间。我们安排一次后续会议来讨论剩下的内容吧。） I see we’re running behind schedule. Could we limit each person’s update to two minutes?（我发现我们进度落后了。每个人的更新发言能限制在两分钟内吗？）​ We’re over time, so let’s skip the detailed explanations and just share the key results.（我们已经超时了，所以我们跳过详细解释，只分享关键结果吧。）​ I’m sorry to cut this short, but we’re running over. Let’s finalize the action items and end here.（抱歉要打断大家，但我们已经超时了。让我们确定好行动项，就此结束吧。）​ We’ve gone past the meeting end time. Let’s agree to discuss the remaining topics via email.（我们已经过了会议结束时间。我们同意通过邮件讨论剩下的话题吧。）​ （三）会议提前结束​ We’ve covered all the items on the agenda earlier than expected, so we can end the meeting now.（我们比预期更早地覆盖了议程上的所有内容，所以现在可以结束会议了。）​ Since we’ve resolved all the key issues and finalized the action items, let’s wrap up the meeting early today.（既然我们已经解决了所有关键问题，确定了行动项，今天就提前结束会议吧。） We’ve made all the necessary decisions and there’s nothing left to discuss, so let’s end the meeting ahead of schedule.（我们已经做出了所有必要的决定，没有剩下的内容要讨论了，所以提前结束会议吧。）​ The main topics have been thoroughly discussed and no further input is needed, so we can finish the meeting early.（主要话题已经讨论得很透彻，不需要更多意见了，所以我们可以提前结束会议。​ We’ve completed the meeting objectives faster than planned, so let’s adjourn the meeting now.（我们比计划更快地完成了会议目标，现在就散会吧。）​ Since everyone is in agreement on the next steps and there are no outstanding questions, let’s end the meeting a bit early.（既然大家都同意下一步计划，也没有未解决的问题，就稍微提前一点结束会议吧。）​ We’ve covered all the urgent matters and the non-urgent ones can be handled later, so let’s wrap up the meeting early.（我们已经讨论了所有紧急事项，非紧急的可以之后处理，所以提前结束会议吧。） The meeting has been productive and we’ve achieved our goals, so let’s end it ahead of the scheduled time.（这次会议很有成效，我们已经达成了目标，所以提前结束吧。）​ There’s no need to continue the meeting since all agenda items have been addressed, so let’s finish early today.（既然所有议程项目都已经处理完毕，没必要继续开会了，今天就提前结束吧。） We’ve finished all the planned discussions and there’s no new business to add, so let’s adjourn the meeting early.（我们已经完成了所有计划好的讨论，也没有新的事项要补充，所以提前散会吧。）​ （四）会议开场白​ Good morning/afternoon everyone. Thank you for joining today’s meeting. Let’s start by going over the agenda.（大家上午 / 下午好。感谢参加今天的会议。我们先过一下议程。）​ Welcome everyone. Today’s meeting is to discuss the progress of the X project and finalize the next steps.（欢迎大家。今天的会议是为了讨论 X 项目的进展，并确定下一步计划。） Hi everyone. Let’s kick off today’s meeting. Before we start, let’s take a moment to welcome our new team member, [Name], who will be joining us for the first time.（大家好。开始之前，我们花点时间欢迎我们的新团队成员 [姓名]，他今天是第一次参加我们的会议。）​ Welcome to today’s meeting. Let’s begin by confirming that everyone has received the meeting materials we sent earlier.（欢迎参加今天的会议。我们先确认一下，大家是否都收到了我们之前发的会议材料。）​ Hi everyone. Thanks for being here.
