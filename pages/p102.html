英语表达800句
一、日常寒暄场景
（一）电梯 / 走廊速聊（30 秒内）
Finally Friday! Any fun plans to escape this heat?
（终于周五了！这么热的天有什么有趣的出行计划吗？）
Love your sneakers! Are they as comfy as they look?
（这双运动鞋真好看！穿起来和看起来一样舒服吗？）
That meeting ran so long. I need coffee to reboot!
（会议开太久了，我急需咖啡 “续命”！）
Hey! I think we've passed each other a few times - I'm [Your Name], by the way.
（嘿！我们好像碰到过几次 —— 对了，我是 [你的名字]。）
Ah, cool. I'm over in [Your Department]. Small world in a big building!
（哇，挺好的。我在 [你的部门]，这么大的办公楼里，世界真小！）
Did you catch the rain this morning? It was pouring!
（你今早赶上那场雨了吗？下得好大！）
The new cafeteria sandwiches are pretty good - have you tried them?
（食堂新出的三明治挺好吃的，你尝过吗？）
That elevator always takes forever at this time of day.
（每天这个点，这电梯总像要等一辈子。）
Heading to a meeting? Hope it goes smoothly!
（要去开会吗？祝一切顺利！）
I heard the office is getting new chairs next week - excited?
（我听说下周办公室要换新椅子了，期待吗？）
Your laptop sticker is so cool! Where did you get it?
（你笔记本电脑的贴纸好酷！在哪儿买的？）
This morning's traffic was crazy - I almost missed the start time.
（今早交通太堵了，我差点迟到。）
Do you know if the 3rd-floor printer is working again?
（你知道三楼的打印机修好了吗？）
The team lunch yesterday was fun - too bad you couldn't make it.
（昨天的团队午餐很开心，可惜你没去成。）
I need to grab a snack from the pantry later - want me to get you something?
（我等下去茶水间拿零食，要帮你带点吗？）
That presentation you gave yesterday was really clear - great job!
（你昨天的演示特别清晰，做得好！）
The AC in my office is too cold - do you have an extra sweater?
（我办公室的空调太冷了，你有多余的毛衣吗？）
Are you attending the company workshop next Friday?
（你要参加下周五的公司研讨会吗？）
I just finished a tough task - finally can take a breath!
（我刚做完一项棘手的任务，终于能喘口气了！）
Did you see the new notice about remote work days?
（你看到关于远程工作日的新通知了吗？）
This elevator music is actually not that bad today.
（今天的电梯音乐居然还挺好听的。）
I forgot my umbrella this morning - hope it doesn't rain again.
（我今早忘带伞了，希望别再下雨。）
Your new haircut looks nice! Fresh change.
（你新剪的头发真好看！很清爽的改变。）
Heading down to the lobby? Can you hold the door for me?
（要去大堂吗？能帮我扶下门吗？）
I tried the new bubble tea shop nearby - their taro flavor is great.
（我去了附近那家新奶茶店，芋头味超赞。）
That deadline got pushed back - what a relief!
（截止日期延后了，太让人安心了！）
Do you know who's giving the keynote speech next month?
（你知道下个月谁来做主旨演讲吗？）
My phone died in the meeting - did I miss anything important?
（我手机在会上没电了，有错过重要内容吗？）
The parking lot was full this morning - I had to park two blocks away.
（今早停车场满了，我只好停在两个街区外。）
Weekend plans? I'm thinking of hiking if the weather is good.
（周末有计划吗？天气好的话我想去徒步。）
（二）茶水间交流
Trying the new matcha powder? It's my secret focus booster!
（在试新抹茶粉？这是我的 “专注神器”！）
The water cooler's moody today.. fridge water is colder though!
（饮水机又闹脾气了…… 不过冰箱里的水更冰哦！）
Quick poll: Sugar in coffee - yes or no?
（紧急调研：咖啡加糖派还是无糖派？）
That smells amazing - homemade or takeout?
（这味道也太香了吧 —— 是自己做的还是外卖呀？）
Leftovers from last night - Thai curry.
（昨晚的剩菜 —— 泰式咖喱。）
Delicious. I keep saying I'll learn to cook more but end up rotating three dishes every week.
（太香了。我总说要多学几道家常菜，结果每周还是翻来覆去吃那三样。）
Did you try the new fruit platter in the pantry? The strawberries are so sweet!
（你尝过茶水间新的水果拼盘吗？草莓超甜！）
This microwave is acting up again - my lunch is still cold.
（这微波炉又出问题了 —— 我的午饭还是凉的。）
Do you have a favorite snack to keep you going in the afternoon?
（你有什么下午提神的本命零食吗？）
The pantry ran out of paper cups - I had to use a mug from my desk.
（茶水间没有纸杯了，我只好用自己桌上的马克杯。）
（三）商务社交活动
Hi, I'm [Your Name]. Mind if I join you?
（嗨，我是 [你的名字]。我能加入你们吗？）
Not at all! I'm [Their Name].
（当然可以！我是 [对方名字]。）
Nice to meet you. What brings you to the event - are you in the same industry?
（很高兴认识你。你是为什么来参加这个活动呀 —— 咱们是同一个行业的吗？）
Yeah, I work in [Your Industry]! What about you - do you specialize in a specific area here?
（对呀，我在 [你的行业] 工作！你呢，在这一行有专门深耕的领域吗？）
Oh, [Their Industry] sounds interesting! Have you been part of this field for a long time?
（哇，[对方的行业] 听起来好有意思！你在这个领域已经做了很久了吗？）
I just started last year, still learning a lot. Did you come to this event for networking, or more for the talks?
（我去年才刚入行，还在学很多东西。你过来参加这个活动，主要是为了拓展人脉，还是更想听听讲座呀？）
Networking was my main goal too - so far, everyone here has been really friendly. Have you met anyone cool before me?
（拓展人脉也是我的主要目的 —— 目前来看，这儿的人都特别友好。在我之前，你有遇到什么有趣的人吗？）
That's awesome! I haven't run into many people from [Target Field] yet - do you know if there are more folks like us here?
（太厉害了！我到现在还没碰到几个 [目标领域] 的人 —— 你知道这儿还有更多同领域的人吗？）
I heard the keynote later is about [Keynote Topic] - are you planning to stick around for that?
（我听说待会儿的主旨演讲是关于 [演讲主题] 的 —— 你打算留下来听吗？）
Me too! Maybe we can grab a coffee before it starts, and chat more about what we've been working on lately?
（我也打算听！说不定咱们可以在开始前先去买杯咖啡，多聊聊最近在忙的项目呀？）
二、客户服务场景
（一）投诉接收与共情
I'm sorry to hear you're having an issue with our product.
（很遗憾听到您在使用我们产品时遇到了问题。）
Can you tell me more about the issue you're experiencing?
（您能再详细说说遇到的问题吗？）
I understand your frustration, and I will do my best to resolve this for you.
（我理解您的不满，我会尽全力为您解决这个问题。）
Please rest assured that we will take your complaint seriously and work to rectify the situation.
（请放心，我们会认真对待您的投诉，并努力纠正问题。）
We apologize for any inconvenience this has caused you.
（对于给您带来的不便，我们深表歉意。）
I’m sorry this has fallen short of your expectations—we want to make it right.
（很抱歉让您失望了，我们会尽力弥补。）
It’s totally reasonable for you to feel this way, and we won’t ignore your concerns.
（您有这样的感受完全合理，我们不会忽视您的顾虑。）
I can imagine how frustrating this must be for you—let’s fix it together.
（我能想象这对您来说有多困扰，咱们一起解决它。）
Thank you for bringing this to our attention—your feedback helps us improve.
（感谢您向我们反馈，您的意见能帮助我们进步。）
I’m sorry the product didn’t work as promised—we’ll take immediate action.
（很抱歉产品未达到承诺的效果，我们会立即采取行动。）
Can you walk me through what happened step by step? That will help us find the root cause.
（您能一步步跟我说说是怎么回事吗？这能帮我们找到问题根源。）
I’m truly sorry for the trouble this has caused—let’s get this resolved quickly.
（为给您带来的麻烦深表歉意，咱们尽快解决这个问题。）
Your satisfaction matters to us, and we’re committed to addressing this issue fully.
（您的满意对我们很重要，我们会全力解决这个问题。）
I’m sorry you had to reach out about this—we should have caught this earlier.
（很抱歉您不得不为此联系我们，我们本应更早发现这个问题。）
I understand how important this is for you, so I’ll prioritize your case.
（我知道这对您很重要，所以我会优先处理您的问题。）
We’re sorry for the mix-up, and we’ll make sure this doesn’t happen again.
（很抱歉出现了混乱，我们会确保此类情况不再发生。）
Can you tell me when you first noticed the problem? That’ll help our investigation.
（您能说说第一次发现这个问题的时间吗？这对我们的调查有帮助。）
I’m sorry the service didn’t meet your needs—let’s figure out a solution that works for you.
（很抱歉服务未满足您的需求，咱们一起找到适合您的解决方案。）
Thank you for your patience as we work through this—we appreciate it.
（感谢您在我们处理问题期间的耐心，我们很感激。）
I’m sorry this has taken up your time—we’ll resolve this as soon as possible.
（很抱歉占用了您的时间，我们会尽快解决。）
（二）问题调查与信息收集
We will investigate this matter and get back to you with a resolution as soon as possible.
（我们会调查此事，并尽快给您回复解决方案。）
Can you provide us with photos or a video of the issue?
（您能给我们提供问题的照片或视频吗？）
We will arrange for our quality control team to inspect the product and determine the cause of the issue.
（我们会安排质检团队检查产品，找出问题原因。）
Could you please confirm your order number so we can look up your purchase details?
（您能确认一下订单号吗？这样我们就能查询您的购买信息了。）
The issue you mentioned is unusual—has this happened before?
（您提到的问题比较少见，之前出现过这种情况吗？）
We've tested similar products and haven't encountered this problem—we'll need to check your specific case.
（我们测试过同类产品，未发现此问题，需要单独核查您的情况。）
Can you tell me when and how you first noticed the issue?
（您能说说第一次发现这个问题的时间和场景吗？）
Could you provide the serial number of the product? It's usually on the bottom or back.
（您能提供产品的序列号吗？通常在底部或背面。）
Did you follow the user manual when setting up the product? Maybe we can walk through the steps together.
（您在安装产品时参考说明书了吗？或许我们可以一起过一遍步骤。）
Can you tell me what devices or software you're using with our product? That might be related.
（您能说说使用我们产品时搭配的设备或软件吗？这可能与此相关。）
Have you tried restarting the product or checking for updates?
（您尝试过重启产品或检查更新吗？）
Could you describe what happens when the issue occurs—any error messages?
（您能描述一下问题出现时的情况吗？有没有错误提示？）
We’ll need to know where and when you purchased the product to assist you better.
（我们需要了解您购买产品的时间和地点，以便更好地为您提供帮助。）
Can you let us know how often the issue happens? Is it consistent or occasional?
（您能告诉我们这个问题出现的频率吗？是一直这样还是偶尔发生？）
Have you contacted any other support teams about this, or is this your first time reaching out?
（您之前就这个问题联系过其他客服团队吗？还是这是第一次反馈？）
Could you share the details of any previous interactions you’ve had about this issue?
（您能分享一下之前就这个问题沟通的细节吗？）
We may need to connect your case to our technical team—would you be okay with that?
（我们可能需要将您的问题转交技术团队，您没问题吧？）
Can you confirm the version of the product you’re using? It’s often listed in the settings.
（您能确认使用的产品版本吗？通常在设置里能找到。）
Have you made any changes to the product or its setup before the issue started?
（问题出现前，您对产品或其设置做过什么改动吗？）
We’ll send you a form to fill out with more details—could you return it within 24 hours?
（我们会给您发一份表格，需要您填写更多细节，您能在 24 小时内回复吗？）
（三）解决方案与补偿
We can offer you a full refund or a replacement product—which would you prefer?
（我们可以为您提供全额退款或更换产品，您更倾向于哪种方案？）
Once we confirm the cause, we'll send you a detailed solution and a compensation plan.
（一旦确认原因，我们会给您发送详细的解决方案和补偿方案。）
I'm sorry the first solution didn't work—let's try a different approach.
（很抱歉第一个方案没解决问题，我们换种方式试试。）
We'll cover all shipping costs if you need to return the product for inspection.
（如果您需要退回产品检测，所有运费由我们承担。）
We can offer you a 20% discount on your next purchase as a token of apology.
（作为歉意，我们可以为您的下次购买提供 20% 的折扣。）
We'll send you a replacement product via express shipping—it should arrive in 2 days.
（我们会用快递给您寄替换产品，预计 2 天内送达。）
The issue was caused by a software bug—we've released an update that fixes it.
（问题是软件漏洞导致的，我们已发布修复更新。）
We apologize for the miscommunication about the delivery time—we'll send you a free gift to make up for it.
（对于配送时间的沟通失误，我们深表歉意，会给您寄一份赠品作为补偿。）
To make up for the inconvenience, we'll extend your subscription by 3 months for free.
（为弥补给您带来的不便，我们会免费为您延长 3 个月的订阅期。）
We can arrange for a technician to visit your location and fix the issue in person—at no cost to you.
（我们可以安排技术人员上门为您解决问题，全程免费。）
If you'd like to keep the product, we can offer a partial refund to cover the inconvenience.
（如果您想保留产品，我们可以提供部分退款以弥补不便。）
We'll prioritize your replacement order to ensure it ships out today.
（我们会优先处理您的换货订单，确保今天发出。）
As a gesture of goodwill, we'll add 500 reward points to your account.
（作为友好表示，我们会给您的账户添加 500 积分。）
We can reschedule the service for a time that's more convenient for you—no extra charges.
（我们可以为您重新安排更方便的服务时间，不额外收费。）
The solution involves updating the firmware—we'll send you a step-by-step guide.
（解决方案需要更新固件，我们会给您发一份分步指南。）
We'll cover the cost of any repairs needed to fix the issue caused by our product.
（因我们产品导致的问题，所有维修费用由我们承担。）
If you prefer, we can transfer the refund to your original payment method within 3-5 business days.
（如果您愿意，我们可以在 3-5 个工作日内将退款退到您原支付方式中。）
We'll provide you with a complimentary extended warranty for 1 year to ensure future peace of mind.
（我们会为您免费提供 1 年的延长保修，让您日后使用更放心。）
The replacement part will arrive next week—we'll call you to schedule installation once it's here.
（替换零件下周会到，到货后我们会打电话给您安排安装。）
To resolve this, we'll adjust your bill for this month and credit the overcharged amount back to you.
（为解决此问题，我们会调整您本月的账单，并将多收的费用退还给您。）
（四）后续跟进与保障
I've escalated your complaint to our senior team, and they will review it within 24 hours.
（我已将您的投诉上报给高级团队，他们会在 24 小时内审核。）
I'll follow up with you personally tomorrow to update you on the progress.
（我明天会亲自跟进，向您同步进展情况。）
The delay in your order was due to a supply chain issue—we're working to ship it out today.
（您订单的延迟是供应链问题导致的，我们今天会加急发货。）
We've updated our system to prevent this issue from happening again—thank you for bringing it to our attention.
（我们已更新系统，避免此类问题再次发生，感谢您的提醒。）
The technical team is currently troubleshooting the issue—they expect to finish in 3 hours.
（技术团队正在排查问题，预计 3 小时内完成。）
Your complaint has been assigned a case number: [Case Number]—you can use it to check progress anytime.
（您的投诉已分配案例号：[案例号]，您可随时用它查询进展。）
Is there a specific time when we can contact you to discuss the solution?
（我们什么时候联系您讨论解决方案比较方便？）
We've found the root cause of the issue and will implement a fix by the end of the day.
（我们已找到问题根源，会在今天内实施修复。）
If you'd like to speak to a manager, I can connect you right away.
（如果您想和经理沟通，我可以立即为您转接。）
We'll conduct a follow-up call next week to ensure the issue doesn't reappear.
（我们下周会打回访电话，确认问题没有再次出现。）
I'll send you an email tomorrow with a detailed update on how we're addressing your issue.
（明天我会给您发邮件，详细同步我们处理您问题的进展。）
You can check the status of your case online using the link we sent to your email.
（您可以通过我们发至您邮箱的链接，在线查询问题处理状态。）
Our team will send you a confirmation once the solution has been implemented.
（解决方案实施后，我们团队会给您发确认通知。）
We'll keep you informed of any delays or changes to the proposed solution.
（如果解决方案有任何延迟或变动，我们会及时告知您。）
I'll make a note in your case to ensure we follow up with you even if there are no updates yet.
（我会在您的问题记录中备注，即使暂无更新，也会主动跟进您。）
Once the issue is resolved, we'll send you a short survey to get your feedback on our service.
（问题解决后，我们会给您发一份简短的调查问卷，了解您对我们服务的反馈。）
We'll prioritize your case to ensure it's resolved within 48 hours.
（我们会优先处理您的问题，确保 48 小时内解决。）
If you have any questions before our follow-up, feel free to call our support line at [Phone Number].
（在我们跟进前，如有任何问题，可随时拨打我们的客服热线：[电话号码]。）
We've shared your feedback with the product team to prevent similar issues in future versions.
（我们已将您的反馈分享给产品团队，避免未来版本出现类似问题。）
I'll personally oversee your case to make sure everything goes smoothly until it's fully resolved.
（我会亲自监督您的问题处理过程，确保在完全解决前一切顺利。）
三、绩效沟通场景
（一）绩效肯定与优势认可
I wanted to start by saying I appreciate your hard work on the recent client projects—your attention to detail really stood out.
（首先，我很感谢你在近期客户项目中的辛勤付出，你的细致入微非常突出。）
Looking at your performance this year, you’ve exceeded the sales target by 15%—great job.
（看你今年的绩效，销售额超出目标 15%，做得很好。）
Your project management skills have improved a lot—you’re now able to handle multiple tasks without missing deadlines.
（你的项目管理能力进步很大，现在能同时处理多项任务且不延误截止日期。）
We’ve received positive feedback from clients about your communication—they say you’re very responsive.
（我们收到了客户对你沟通的正面反馈，他们说你回复很及时。）
Your innovation in process optimization saved the team 20 hours a week—we want to recognize that.
（你在流程优化中的创新每周为团队节省了 20 小时，我们想对此给予认可。）
Your ability to handle high-pressure projects is impressive—you stay calm and deliver quality work even under stress.
（你处理高压项目的能力很出色，即使在压力下也能保持冷静并交付高质量工作。）
You’ve brought in 3 new big clients this year—your client acquisition skills are a huge asset to the team.
（你今年带来了 3 个新的大客户，你的客户获取能力是团队的重要财富。）
Your report on market trends was used by the leadership team—they praised its depth and insight.
（你的市场趋势报告被领导团队采用，他们称赞报告内容深入且有洞察力。）
We’ve noticed you’re good at resolving team conflicts—you listen to everyone and find fair solutions.
（我们发现你很擅长解决团队冲突，你会倾听每个人的意见并找到公平的解决方案。）
Your cross-department projects have been successful—you build strong relationships with other teams to get things done.
（你的跨部门项目很成功，你能与其他团队建立良好关系以推进工作。）
Your presentation skills have improved—you now make complex information easy to understand for the audience.
