纪要结构
The standard structure of a meeting minute usually starts with Attendees. （会议纪要的标准结构通常以参会人员开始。）
Listing Attendees clearly helps everyone know who was present at the meeting. （清晰列出参会人员有助于所有人了解谁出席了会议。）
Attendees should include both in - person participants and those joining remotely. （参会人员应包括现场参会者和远程参会者。）
After Attendees, the next part of the meeting minute is Agenda Items. （参会人员之后，会议纪要的下一部分是议程项目。）
Agenda Items need to be listed in the order they were discussed during the meeting. （议程项目需按照会议中讨论的顺序列出。）
Each Agenda Item should have a brief description to show its main content. （每个议程项目都应有简要说明，以表明其主要内容。）
Following Agenda Items, Action Items are an essential part of the meeting minute structure. （在议程项目之后，行动项是会议纪要结构中的重要组成部分。）
Action Items should be closely related to the Agenda Items discussed. （行动项应与讨论过的议程项目密切相关。）
The “Attendees - Agenda Items - Action Items” structure makes the meeting minute organized. （“参会人员 - 议程项目 - 行动项” 这一结构使会议纪要条理清晰。）
Using this structure can help readers quickly find the key information they need. （采用这种结构能帮助读者快速找到所需的关键信息。）
When writing Attendees, it's better to include their job titles if necessary. （撰写参会人员时，如有必要，最好注明他们的职位。）
Attendees list should be checked carefully to avoid missing anyone. （参会人员名单应仔细核对，避免遗漏。）
Agenda Items can be numbered to make the meeting minute more systematic. （议程项目可以编号，使会议纪要更具系统性。）
Each numbered Agenda Item can be followed by a short note on the discussion process. （每个编号的议程项目后可附上关于讨论过程的简短说明。）
Action Items in the structure should be specific and not vague. （该结构中的行动项应具体明确，不能模糊不清。）
The order of Attendees, Agenda Items and Action Items should not be randomly changed. （参会人员、议程项目和行动项的顺序不应随意更改。）
Attendees part can also include the time when each person arrived if it's important. （如重要，参会人员部分还可包括每个人的到达时间。）
Agenda Items should be prepared before the meeting and listed in the minute accordingly. （议程项目应在会议前准备好，并据此列入纪要。）
Action Items are the core part that drives the follow - up work after the meeting. （行动项是推动会议后后续工作的核心部分。）
The clear division of the three parts in the structure improves the readability of the minute. （该结构中三个部分的明确划分提高了纪要的可读性。）
When there are substitute attendees, it should be indicated in the Attendees part. （如有代参会者，应在参会人员部分注明。）
Agenda Items that were not discussed in the meeting should also be noted in the minute. （会议中未讨论的议程项目也应在纪要中注明。）
Action Items need to be linked to the corresponding Agenda Items for easy tracking. （行动项需与对应的议程项目关联，方便跟踪。）
The Attendees part can be divided into “Present” and “Absent” if needed. （如有需要，参会人员部分可分为 “出席” 和 “缺席” 两类。）
Agenda Items should be concise and not too lengthy in the meeting minute. （会议纪要中的议程项目应简洁，不宜过长。）
Action Items in the structure should be assigned to specific individuals. （该结构中的行动项应分配给具体个人。）
The “Attendees - Agenda Items - Action Items” structure is widely used in foreign - funded enterprises. （“参会人员 - 议程项目 - 行动项” 结构在外企中被广泛使用。）
Attendees information helps to clarify the responsibility scope of each participant. （参会人员信息有助于明确每个参会者的责任范围。）
Agenda Items serve as a guide for the meeting discussion and the minute content. （议程项目是会议讨论和纪要内容的指引。）
Action Items in the minute should be feasible and achievable within a certain period. （纪要中的行动项应具有可行性，能在一定期限内完成。）
When writing the Attendees part, the names should be written in a unified format. （撰写参会人员部分时，姓名应采用统一格式。）
Agenda Items can be grouped by different topics to make the minute clearer. （议程项目可按不同主题分组，使纪要更清晰。）
Action Items need to have clear time limits to ensure timely completion. （行动项需有明确的时间限制，确保按时完成。）
The Attendees part can include the contact information of key participants if required. （如需要，参会人员部分可包括关键参会者的联系方式。）
Agenda Items that are put forward temporarily during the meeting should be added to the minute. （会议中临时提出的议程项目应补充到纪要中。）
Action Items should be reviewed regularly to check their progress. （行动项应定期审查，检查其进展情况。）
The structure of the meeting minute starting with Attendees is in line with international conventions. （以参会人员开头的会议纪要结构符合国际惯例。）
Agenda Items in the minute should reflect the key points of the meeting discussion. （纪要中的议程项目应反映会议讨论的重点。）
Action Items are the basis for evaluating the effectiveness of the meeting. （行动项是评估会议成效的依据。）
The Attendees, Agenda Items and Action Items should be consistent with the actual situation of the meeting. （参会人员、议程项目和行动项应与会议实际情况相符。）
When there are multiple meeting rooms, it can be indicated in the Attendees part where each participant is. （如有多个会议室，可在参会人员部分注明每个参会者所在的地点。）
Agenda Items can be marked with priorities to guide the follow - up work. （议程项目可标注优先级，以指导后续工作。）
Action Items in the minute should be specific to avoid ambiguity in implementation. （纪要中的行动项应具体，避免执行时产生歧义。）
The Attendees part should be updated in a timely manner if there are personnel changes during the meeting. （如会议期间有人员变动，参会人员部分应及时更新。）
Agenda Items that require further discussion in the next meeting should be noted in the current minute. （需在下次会议中进一步讨论的议程项目应在本次纪要中注明。）
Action Items should be assigned based on the responsibilities and capabilities of the staff. （行动项应根据员工的职责和能力进行分配。）
The “Attendees - Agenda Items - Action Items” structure helps to improve work efficiency. （“参会人员 - 议程项目 - 行动项” 结构有助于提高工作效率。）
Attendees list can be attached as an appendix if the number of participants is large. （如参会人数较多，参会人员名单可作为附件附上。）
Agenda Items in the minute should be written in a formal and professional language. （纪要中的议程项目应使用正式、专业的语言撰写。）
Action Items in the minute should be tracked until they are completed. （纪要中的行动项应跟踪至完成。）

Check if all attendees （including remote participants） are correctly listed in the meeting minutes. （检查会议纪要中是否正确列出所有参会者（包括远程参会者）。）
Verify that the meeting time （start time and end time） in the minutes matches the actual meeting duration. （核实纪要中的会议时间（开始时间和结束时间）与实际会议时长是否一致。）
Confirm that each agenda item discussed in the meeting is recorded in the minutes without omission. （确认会议中讨论的每个议程项目都已记录在纪要中，无遗漏。）
Check if all action items in the minutes have clear assignees （[Name]） and deadlines （[Date]）. （检查纪要中的所有行动项是否都有明确的负责人（[姓名]）和截止日期（[日期]）。）
Verify that the key decisions made in the meeting are accurately recorded in the minutes, without ambiguity. （核实会议中做出的关键决策是否准确记录在纪要中，无歧义。）
Check if the controversial points or undecided matters in the meeting are clearly noted in the minutes. （检查会议中的争议点或未决事项是否在纪要中明确注明。）
Confirm that the language of the meeting minutes is formal, professional, and free of grammar or spelling errors （especially in English parts）. （确认会议纪要语言正式、专业，无语法或拼写错误（尤其是英文部分）。）

Check if the minutes avoid overly lengthy descriptions and focus on key information （decisions, action items, risks）. （检查纪要避免过长描述，重点聚焦关键信息（决策、行动项、风险）。）

“RACI 矩阵”跟踪会议行动项的英文表达
RACI Matrix Definition: RACI is an acronym for Responsible, Accountable, Consulted, and Informed, which is used to clarify roles and responsibilities for each action item. （RACI 矩阵定义：RACI 是 Responsible（执行责任人）、Accountable（最终责任人）、Consulted（咨询对象）和 Informed（知情对象）的缩写，用于明确每个行动项的角色与职责。）
"Responsible （R）": The person who actually completes the action item. Example: [Name] is Responsible for drafting the marketing plan. （“执行责任人（R）”：实际完成行动项的人。示例：[姓名] 是起草营销计划的执行责任人。）
"Accountable （A）": The person who approves the completion of the action item and takes overall responsibility. Example: [Name] is Accountable for the final approval of the marketing plan. （“最终责任人（A）”：批准行动项完成情况并承担整体责任的人。示例：[姓名] 是营销计划最终审批的最终责任人。）
"Consulted （C）": The person who provides input or advice for the action item before it is completed. Example: [Name] （Design Team Lead） is Consulted on the visual design of the marketing plan. （“咨询对象（C）”：行动项完成前提供意见或建议的人。示例：[姓名]（设计团队负责人）是营销计划视觉设计的咨询对象。）
"Informed （I）": The person who is notified of the progress and final result of the action item but does not provide input. Example: [Name] （Sales Manager） is Informed of the marketing plan's approval status. （“知情对象（I）”：被告知行动项进展及最终结果但不提供意见的人。示例：[姓名]（销售经理）是营销计划审批状态的知情对象。）
RACI Matrix Application Principle 1: Each action item should have only one Accountable （A） to avoid responsibility ambiguity. （RACI 矩阵应用原则 1：每个行动项应仅有一名最终责任人（A），避免责任模糊。）
RACI Matrix Application Principle 2: There can be multiple Responsible （R） persons for complex action items （e.g., cross-departmental tasks）, but they need to clarify division of labor. （RACI 矩阵应用原则 2：复杂行动项（如跨部门任务）可有多名执行责任人（R），但需明确分工。）
RACI Matrix Application Principle 3: Consulted （C） persons should be limited to those with direct expertise to avoid inefficient discussions. （RACI 矩阵应用原则 3：咨询对象（C）应限于具备直接专业知识的人，避免低效讨论。）

RACI Role Description for Meeting Action Items: When recording action items in minutes, adding RACI roles helps track who is doing what, who approves, and who needs to be updated. （会议行动项 RACI 角色描述：在纪要中记录行动项时，添加 RACI 角色有助于跟踪 “谁执行、谁审批、谁需知情”。）
How to Assign RACI Roles: First confirm the Accountable （A） （usually the person who proposed the action item or the department head）, then assign the Responsible （R）, and finally identify Consulted （C） and Informed （I） based on task relevance. （RACI 角色分配方法：先确定最终责任人（A）（通常是提出行动项的人或部门负责人），再分配执行责任人（R），最后根据任务关联性确定咨询对象（C）和知情对象（I）。）
RACI Matrix Benefit 1: Reduces duplication of work by clarifying which team members are Responsible （R） for specific tasks. （RACI 矩阵优势 1：通过明确哪些团队成员是特定任务的执行责任人（R），减少重复工作。）
RACI Matrix Benefit 2: Improves accountability as each action item has a clear Accountable （A） who cannot shift responsibility. （RACI 矩阵优势 2：每个行动项有明确的最终责任人（A），无法推卸责任，提升问责效率。）
RACI Matrix Benefit 3: Enhances communication efficiency by ensuring only relevant persons are Consulted （C） or Informed （I）. （RACI 矩阵优势 3：仅让相关人员成为咨询对象（C）或知情对象（I），提升沟通效率。）
RACI Matrix Update Requirement: If the action item scope or team members change, the RACI roles should be updated in the meeting minutes in a timely manner. （RACI 矩阵更新要求：若行动项范围或团队成员发生变化，需在会议纪要中及时更新 RACI 角色。）
RACI Role Conflict Resolution: If a person is both Responsible （R） and Accountable （A） （common for simple tasks）, it should be clearly noted in the minutes to avoid confusion. （RACI 角色冲突解决：若一人同时担任执行责任人（R）和最终责任人（A）（简单任务中常见），需在纪要中明确注明，避免混淆。）
RACI Matrix in Meeting Follow-up: During the next meeting's action item review, refer to the RACI matrix to check the Responsible （R） person's progress and the Accountable （A） person's supervision status. （会议跟进中的 RACI 矩阵：在下次会议行动项评审时，参考 RACI 矩阵检查执行责任人（R）的进展及最终责任人（A）的监督情况。）

RACI Role Language Consistency: When writing RACI roles in English minutes, use the full terms （Responsible, Accountable, Consulted, Informed） for the first mention, and abbreviations （R, A, C, I） for subsequent mentions. （RACI 角色语言一致性：在英文纪要中撰写 RACI 角色时，首次提及使用完整术语（Responsible、Accountable、Consulted、Informed），后续提及使用缩写（R、A、C、I）。）
RACI Matrix for Cross-departmental Action Items: For tasks involving multiple departments, the Accountable （A） should be a senior manager who can coordinate cross-departmental resources. （跨部门行动项的 RACI 矩阵：对于涉及多个部门的任务，最终责任人（A）应是能够协调跨部门资源的高级管理人员。）
RACI Matrix for Urgent Action Items: For time-sensitive tasks, clearly mark the Responsible （R） person's daily progress update frequency （e.g., Twice a day） in the RACI matrix to ensure timely completion. （紧急行动项的 RACI 矩阵：对于时间敏感的任务，在 RACI 矩阵中明确标注执行责任人（R）的每日进度更新频率（如每日两次），确保及时完成。）
RACI Role Documentation Requirement: All RACI role assignments for action items should be documented in the meeting minutes, not just verbally communicated, to avoid post-task disputes. （RACI 角色文档记录要求：行动项的所有 RACI 角色分配都应记录在会议纪要中，而非仅口头沟通，避免任务后产生争议。）
RACI Matrix Review Frequency: For long-term action items （e.g., 3-month projects）, review and adjust the RACI matrix every month during the project progress meeting to adapt to task changes. （RACI 矩阵评审频率：对于长期行动项（如 3 个月的项目），在项目进度会议中每月评审并调整 RACI 矩阵，以适应任务变化。）
RACI Matrix and Remote Teams: When working with remote team members, the RACI matrix should clearly specify the communication channels （e.g., Slack for daily updates, Zoom for weekly reviews） for the Responsible （R） and Informed （I） roles. （RACI 矩阵与远程团队：与远程团队成员协作时，RACI 矩阵应明确规定执行责任人（R）和知情对象（I）的沟通渠道（如日常更新用 Slack，每周评审用 Zoom）。）
