in a fireproof and moisture-proof file cabinet, with a clear label indicating the contract name and validity period.（行政部门负责将合同原件存放在防火防潮的文件柜中，并贴上清晰的标签，注明合同名称和有效期。）​ We will scan the original contract into a digital version and store it in the company's encrypted cloud drive, so that authorized personnel can access it when needed.（我们会将合同原件扫描成电子版，存放在公司加密的云盘中，方便授权人员在需要时查阅。）​ The project manager will set a reminder three months before the contract expires, to start the discussion on contract renewal or termination with the client in advance.（项目经理会在合同到期前三个月设置提醒，提前与客户启动合同续约或终止的讨论。）​ We will establish a contract management spreadsheet, recording the contract number, parties involved, validity period, key clauses, and follow-up tasks.（我们会建立合同管理表格，记录合同编号、涉及方、有效期、关键条款和跟进任务。）​ If there is any change in the contract during the cooperation period (such as adjusting the delivery quantity), we need to sign a supplementary agreement and attach it to the original contract.（合作期间若合同有任何变更（如调整交货数量），我们需要签署补充协议，并将其附在原合同后。）​ The legal department will conduct a quarterly review of all active contracts to check whether the implementation of the contract complies with the terms and identify potential risks.（法务部门会每季度对所有生效合同进行审查，检查合同履行是否符合条款，识别潜在风险。）​ When the contract is terminated (either upon expiration or early termination), we will archive the original contract, supplementary agreements, and related communication records for at least 5 years in accordance with the company's regulations.（当合同终止时（无论是到期还是提前终止），我们会按照公司规定，将原合同、补充协议和相关沟通记录归档至少 5 年。）​ The finance department will refer to the payment terms in the contract when collecting payments from the client, and promptly notify the project manager if there is any delay in payment.（财务部门向客户收款时，会参考合同中的付款条款，若出现付款延迟，及时通知项目经理。） We will restrict access to the contract to only authorized personnel (such as the project team, legal department, and finance department) to protect the company's confidential information.（我们会限制合同的查阅权限，仅授权人员（如项目团队、法务部门、财务部门）可查看，以保护公司机密信息。）​ If the client fails to fulfill the contract terms (such as delayed payment), we will first send a reminder a project kick-off meeting with the client next Wednesday to confirm the project team members, communication mechanism, and initial timeline.（下周三我们会与客户召开项目启动会，确认项目团队成员、沟通机制和初步时间线。）​ The project manager will send a project kick-off document to all team members (including the client's team) one day before the meeting, which outlines the project goals and scope.（项目经理会在会议前一天向所有团队成员（包括客户团队）发送项目启动文件，其中概述了项目目标和范围。） During the kick-off meeting, we will assign a dedicated communication coordinator for both parties, who will be responsible for daily information exchange and problem reporting.（启动会上，我们会为双方各指定一名沟通协调员，负责日常信息交换和问题上报。）​ We will set up a shared project management platform (such as Asana) for both teams, where we can update task progress, upload documents, and leave comments in real time.（我们会为双方团队搭建共享的项目管理平台（如 Asana），在平台上实时更新任务进度、上传文件和留言。）​ The technical team will hold a separate meeting with the client's technical representatives to discuss the technical requirements and compatibility of the project.（技术团队会与客户的技术代表单独召开会议，讨论项目的技术需求和兼容性问题。）​ We will confirm the project's key milestones with the client during the kick-off phase, such as the completion date of the prototype and the final delivery date.（启动阶段，我们会与客户确认项目的关键里程碑，如原型完成日期和最终交付日期。）​ The finance department will send the project's payment schedule to the client, which specifies the payment amount and time nodes according to the contract terms.（财务部门会向客户发送项目付款时间表，根据合同条款明确付款金额和时间节点。）​ We will collect the contact information of all key personnel in the client's team (such as phone numbers and email addresses) and compile it into a contact list for emergency communication.（我们会收集客户团队所有关键人员的联系方式（如电话、邮箱），整理成联系表，用于紧急沟通。）
