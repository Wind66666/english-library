The HR team organizes “career panels” where employees from different departments and career stages share their career paths and advice.（人力资源团队组织 “职业座谈会”，邀请不同部门和职业阶段的员工分享他们的职业道路和建议。）
I want to strengthen my strategic planning skills, so I’m attending a workshop on long-term business planning and asking to be part of the company’s annual planning process.（我想提升战略规划能力，因此参加了长期业务规划研讨会，并请求参与公司的年度规划流程。）
The company’s training program includes a mentorship component, where each participant is paired with a senior employee who provides guidance and support.（公司的培训计划包含导师环节，每位参与者都会与一名高级员工配对，获取指导和支持。）
I’ve set a goal to master one new skill every quarter—this quarter, it’s data visualization, and next quarter, it will be public speaking.（我设定了每季度掌握一项新技能的目标 —— 本季度是数据可视化，下季度是公开演讲。）
The company hosts “lunch and learn” webinars with external experts—last month, we had a session on trends in sustainable business practices.（公司与外部专家合作举办 “午餐学习” 网络研讨会 —— 上个月，我们举办了关于可持续商业实践趋势的会议。）
I’m working on improving my cross-cultural communication skills by taking a course and learning about the cultural norms of our international clients.（我通过参加课程和了解国际客户的文化规范，提升跨文化沟通能力。）
The HR department provides resources for employees to create a personal development plan, including templates and examples of successful plans.（人力资源部门为员工提供制定个人发展计划的资源，包括模板和成功计划案例。）
I want to learn more about product development, so I’ve asked to be part of the product team’s weekly meetings and am reading books on product management.（我想了解更多产品开发知识，因此请求参加产品团队的周会，并阅读产品管理相关书籍。）
The company’s leadership development program includes a project where participants work on a real business challenge—my team is focused on reducing operational costs.（公司的领导力发展计划包含一个项目，参与者需解决实际业务挑战 —— 我的团队专注于降低运营成本。）
I’m practicing my negotiation skills by role-playing with a colleague, simulating scenarios like negotiating with suppliers or resolving customer complaints.（我通过与同事进行角色扮演练习谈判技巧，模拟与供应商谈判或解决客户投诉等场景。）
The company’s internal job board includes “developmental roles”—positions that are designed to help employees build new skills while contributing to the team.（公司内部招聘板包含 “发展型岗位”—— 这类岗位旨在帮助员工在为团队做贡献的同时培养新技能。）
I want to improve my ability to manage tight deadlines, so I’m using time-management tools and asking my manager for tips on prioritizing tasks.（我想提升应对紧张截止日期的能力，因此使用时间管理工具，并向经理请教任务优先级排序技巧。）
The HR team conducts follow-up surveys after training programs to gather feedback and ensure that employees are applying what they’ve learned to their work.（人力资源部门在培训计划结束后进行跟进调查，收集反馈并确保员工将所学应用到工作中。）
