2. Can you tell us about your weaknesses?
Sample Answers
Sample 1
“I am rather impatient when working in a team (self - awareness). I'm fond of working independently, so it's challenging for me to rely on others to complete a task. That's why I've pursued a position that enables me to work independently (being truthful). Nevertheless, I've also been working on improving this weakness by enrolling in team - building workshops. Even when I work independently, it's crucial that I learn to trust my co - workers better (self - improvement/recovery).”
Sample 2
“Public speaking makes me extremely nervous (self - awareness). Even though I don't have to do a great deal of public speaking in my current role, I still consider it an important skill to enhance. To overcome this fear, I spoke to my manager and expressed my desire to give the introductory speech during our team meetings. This has helped me become less nervous when speaking to a group of people and has also improved my communication with team members, enabling them to perform their jobs more effectively (self - improvement/recovery).”
This answer is effective because the candidate mentions a genuine weakness that doesn't impede their ability to perform the job. Secondly, the candidate demonstrates eagerness to develop strategies to address the weakness.

After a solution is chosen, I assign tasks to group members based on their skills and ensure that everyone understands their role in implementing the solution.
