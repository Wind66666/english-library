一、会议开始
1. 等待迟到者
 “Hello, folks. A good number of you
are already here. Let's hold on for a
short while for those who might be
caught up in something and running a
bit behind.”
 诸位好，已有不少同仁莅临。让我们稍作
等候，想必有些同事或因事务羁绊，稍有
耽搁。
 “Hey everyone. It seems like the
majority of us have arrived. We'll give
it a couple more minutes for those
who are yet to join.”
 大家好呀，看样子多数人已到。我们再等
几分钟，迎接尚未到场的同事。
 “Good [morning/afternoon], team.
Many of you are present. Let's wait
patiently for our colleagues who might
be on their way.”
 团队的伙伴们，早上or下午好。众多伙伴
已齐聚于此，让我们耐心等待尚在途中的
同事。
2. 欢迎和介绍
 “Good morning/afternoon, dear
colleagues. Thank you so much for
taking the time to be part of this
meeting.”
 亲爱的同事们，早上or下午好。衷心感谢
各位拨冗参加此次会议。
 “Welcome, everyone. I'm glad to see
you all here. Before we start, let me
briefly introduce the purpose of this
meeting.”
 欢迎各位，看到大家齐聚一堂，我深感欣
喜。在会议开始前，容我简要阐述本次会
议的宗旨。
 “Hello, everyone. I'd like to kick - off
by giving a warm welcome to all of
you. And now, let's go around and
introduce ourselves briefly.”
 大家好，首先，我向各位致以热烈的欢迎。
此刻，让我们依次简要地做个自我介绍。
3. 共享屏幕
 “All right, I'm about to share my
screen. Please let me know if there are
any issues with the visibility.”
 好的，我即将共享屏幕。若有任何显示问
题，请告知于我。
 “Okay, here comes the screen sharing.
Can you all see the presentation
clearly? Raise your virtual hands if you
can.”
 开始共享屏幕了。大家能清晰看到演示内
容吗？若能看到，请在屏幕上举手示意。
 “I'm sharing my screen now. Someone
please confirm that the content is
visible on your end.”
 我正在共享屏幕，烦请哪位同事确认一下，
在您那边是否能看到内容。
4. 说明会议目的与议程
 “Today, we're gathered here to discuss

our upcoming product launch. The

的产品。议程涵盖产品特性概述、市场分
析以及营销活动规划。

review our quarterly performance.
We'll start with a department - by -

行各部门逐一总结，随后探讨改进方向，

potential revenue, and any necessary
adjustments to ensure we stay within
our financial constraints.”
 本次会议致力于敲定新项目的预算。议程
将涉及成本估算、潜在收益，以及为确保
符合财务限制而需做出的必要调整。

将成为我们 [项目 / 部门] 不可或缺的一员。

己？

[Field/Area], and we're excited to have
[him/her] on board. [New Member's
Name], please say a few words about
yourself.”
 让我们热烈欢迎 [新成员姓名] 加入团队。
[新成员] 在 [领域 / 方面] 经验丰富，我们
十分欣喜 [他 / 她] 的加入。[新成员姓名]，
请简单介绍几句吧。
 “Meet [New Member's Name], our
latest addition to the team. [New
Member] has a strong background in
[Relevant Skills/Expertise], which will
be very valuable for our upcoming
projects. [New Member's Name], why
don't you tell us a bit about yourself?”
 来认识一下 [新成员姓名]，我们团队的新
伙伴。[新成员] 在 [相关技能 / 专业知识]
方面底蕴深厚，这对我们即将开展的项目
颇具价值。[新成员姓名]，不妨跟我们讲讲
您自己吧。
6. 营造会议氛围
 “Before we dive into the serious stuff, I
hope everyone had a great start to the
week. Let's keep that positive energy
throughout this meeting and make it

 在我们开启严肃讨论之前，愿大家本周都
有一个美好的开端。希望在整个会议过程
中，我们能保持这份积极的活力，让会议
卓有成效。
 “Welcome to our meeting, folks! I
think we've got some really interesting
topics to cover today, so let's make
this an engaging and interactive
session.”
 欢迎各位参加本次会议！今日我们将探讨
一些饶有趣味的话题，让我们共同打造一
个引人入胜、互动性强的会议。

 “Good [morning/afternoon] everyone.

二、会议进行时
1. 提出问题
 “Excuse me, I'm a bit confused about
the data presented in the last slide.
Could you explain it in more detail?”
 打扰一下，我对上一张幻灯片展示的数据
有些困惑。能否请您详细阐释一下？
 “May I pose a question regarding the
market strategy you just mentioned?
How do we plan to implement it in the
short - term?”
 我 能 否 就 您 刚 才 提 及 的 市 场 策 略 提 个 问
题？短期内，我们计划如何实施？
 “Can you clarify what exactly you
meant by the term'synergy' in this
context? I want to make sure I
understand it correctly.”
 您能否阐明在该情境下，“协同效应” 这一
术语的确切含义？我想确保自己理解无误。
 “I have a question about the budget
allocation. Why was this particular
amount assigned to this project?”
 关于预算分配，我有个疑问。为何给此项
目分配这一特定金额？
2. 发表意见
 “In my opinion, we should focus on
improving our customer service as it
can significantly enhance our brand
reputation.”
 依我之见，我们应聚焦于提升客户服务，
因其能显著提升我们的品牌声誉。
 “I believe that we need to invest more
in research and development to stay
competitive in the market.”
 我认为，为在市场中保持竞争力，我们需
加大在研发方面的投入。
 “From my experience, it might be a
good idea to launch a pilot project
first to test the feasibility of this new
concept.”
 以我的经验，不妨先启动一个试点项目，
测试这一新概念的可行性。
 “I think we should consider
collaborating with other companies to
expand our market reach.”
 我觉得我们应考虑与其他公司合作，以拓
展市场覆盖范围。
3. 意见不统一
 “It's completely understandable that
we have different viewpoints. Let's dig
deeper and see if we can find some
common ground.”
 我们持有不同观点，这完全可以理解。让
我们深入探讨，看看能否找到一些共识。
 “I see where you're coming from, but
perhaps we could also look at this
from a different angle, like
considering the long - term costs.”
 我明白您的观点，但或许我们也可从另一
角度审视，比如考虑长期成本。
 “Let's respect each other's opinions.
For now, we'll agree to disagree on
this point and come back to it later if
necessary.”
 让我们尊重彼此的意见。目前，我们在这
一点上可保留各自看法，如有必要，稍后
再议。
 “While I understand your stance, we
4
should also take into account the
potential risks associated with your
proposal.”
 虽然我理解您的立场，但我们也应考虑您
提议中潜在的风险。
4. 解决问题
 “How about we meet halfway on this?
We could adjust the timeline slightly
to accommodate both parties' needs.”
 在此事上，我们各让一步如何？我们可稍
作时间调整，以兼顾双方需求。
 “Let's put our heads together and
come up with some innovative
solutions to this problem. What ideas
do you have?”
 让我们集思广益，为这个问题想出创新的
解决方案。大家有何想法？
 “Can we reach a common
understanding on this matter? We
need to make a decision soon.”
 我们能否就此事达成共识？我们亟需尽快
做出决策。
 “Let's consider all the options and find
a solution that works for everyone.
Any suggestions?”
 让我们权衡所有选项，寻求一个对大家都
可行的解决方案。有什么建议吗？
5. 寻求他人意见
 “What are your thoughts on this new

 对于这一新举措，各位有何见解？我们期
待聆听大家的想法。
 “I'm interested in knowing your

 我很想了解您的看法，[姓名]。您认为我们
怎样能让这一策略更具成效？
 “Before we move forward, let's get
some input from the team. What do
you all think about this approach?”
 在继续推进之前，让我们听听团队的意见。
大家对这种方法有何看法？
6. 表示赞同

 我完全赞同您的观点，它与我们公司的长

 您说得太对了。这个解决方案似乎能解决
我们一直面临的所有问题。

 我明白您所言的价值，但我对实施时间表

 我认同整体概念，不过，成本效益分析或
许还需进一步完善。

 您的提议有一定道理。但我们也应考量其

 讨论完营销计划，是时候引入新话题了。

的市场策略将带来更高的总收入。让我们

 简要概括，我们的重点将放在产品质量提
