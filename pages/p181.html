会议开始
1. 等待迟到者
• 线上会议：“Hello, everyone. Quite a few of you have joined already. Let's hold on for a few minutes
for those who might be having trouble joining due to network issues.”（诸位好，已有不少同仁上线。
我们稍等几分钟，有些同事可能因网络问题加入会议稍迟。）
• 线下会议：“Good [morning/afternoon], team. Many of you are here. Let's wait patiently for our
colleagues who might be stuck in traffic.”（团队的伙伴们，早上 / 下午好。众多伙伴已到场，让我们
耐心等待可能因交通拥堵还在路上的同事。）
2. 欢迎和介绍
2
• 正式会议：“Good morning/afternoon, esteemed colleagues. Thank you for sparing the time to
attend this significant meeting.”（尊敬的同事们，早上 / 下午好。感谢各位拨冗参加此次重要会议。）
• 新团队组建会议：“Welcome, everyone! I'm thrilled to see so many new faces. Before we start, let
me briefly introduce the goals of this meeting and our team structure.”（欢迎各位！看到这么多新面
孔我很激动。在会议开始前，我简要介绍下本次会议的目标和我们的团队架构。）
3. 共享屏幕
• 线上会议：“All right, I'm about to share my screen. Give me a thumbs - up if the screen looks
clear.”（好的，我即将共享屏幕。如果屏幕显示清晰，请给我点个赞示意。）
• 培训会议： “Okay, here comes the screen sharing. I'll be demonstrating some new software
features. Let me know right away if you can't see anything clearly.”（开始共享屏幕了。我将演示一
些新软件功能，如果有任何看不清的地方，请马上告诉我。）
4. 说明会议目的与议程
• 项目启动会议：“Today, we're here to kick - off our new project. The agenda includes a project
overview, a breakdown of tasks, and a discussion on the initial milestones.”（今日，我们相聚于此
启动新项目。议程涵盖项目概述、任务分解以及初始里程碑的讨论。）
• 季度总结会议：“The purpose of this meeting is to review our quarterly performance. We'll start
with a department - by - department analysis, then move on to discuss areas for improvement
and set goals for the next quarter.”（本次会议旨在回顾季度业绩。我们将先进行各部门逐一分析，随
后探讨改进方向，并为下一季度设定目标。）
5. 介绍新成员
• 团队内部会议：“I'd like to take a moment to introduce our new team member, [ New Member's
Name]. [ New Member's Name] has joined us with a strong background in [Relevant
Skills/Expertise], and will play a key role in our [Project/Department]. [ New Member's Name],
would you like to briefly introduce yourself?”（我想借此机会介绍我们的新团队成员，[新成员姓名]。
[新成员姓名] 带着在 [相关技能 / 专业知识] 方面的深厚背景加入我们，将在我们 [项目 / 部门] 中发
挥关键作用。[新成员姓名]，能否请您简要介绍一下自己？）
• 跨部门合作会议：“Let's welcome [New Member's Name] to our extended team. [ New Member]
comes with extensive experience in [Field/Area], and we're excited to have [him/her] on board for
this cross - departmental project. [New Member's Name], please share a bit about your role and
expectations.”（让我们热烈欢迎 [新成员姓名] 加入我们的跨部门团队。[新成员] 在 [领域 / 方面] 经
验丰富，我们很高兴 [他 / 她] 能参与这个跨部门项目。[新成员姓名]，请分享一下您的角色和期望。）
6. 营造会议氛围
• 创意项目会议：“Before we dive into the details, I hope everyone had a great start to the week.
3
Let's keep that creative energy flowing throughout this meeting and come up with some amazing
ideas.”（在我们深入细节之前，愿大家本周都有一个美好的开端。希望在整个会议过程中， 我们能保持这
份创意活力，想出一些绝妙的点子。）
• 日常团队会议：“Welcome to our meeting, folks! I think we've got some really interesting topics to
cover today. Let's make this an engaging and interactive session, and don't be shy to share your
thoughts.”（欢迎各位参加本次会议！今日我们将探讨一些饶有趣味的话题。让我们共同打造一个引人入
胜、互动性强的会议，大家别害羞，大胆分享想法。）
会议进行时
1. 提出问题
• 数据分析会议：“Excuse me, I'm a bit confused about the data in the last slide. Could you explain
the methodology behind this data collection?”（打扰一下，我对上一张幻灯片的数据有些困惑。您能
解释一下这个数据收集的方法吗？）
• 战略讨论会议：“May I pose a question regarding the market strategy you just mentioned? How do
we plan to measure the success of this strategy in the short - term?”（我能否就您刚才提及的市场
策略提个问题？短期内，我们计划如何衡量这个策略的成功与否？）
2. 发表意见
• 产品研发会议：“In my opinion, we should focus on enhancing the user experience of our product.
This can significantly increase user satisfaction and loyalty.”（依我之见，我们应聚焦于提升产品的
用户体验。这能显著提高用户满意度和忠诚度。）
• 成本控制会议： “I believe that we need to optimize our supply chain to reduce costs without
sacrificing quality.”（我认为，我们需要优化供应链，在不牺牲质量的前提下降低成本。）
3. 意见不统一（冲突化解）
• 跨文化团队：“It's completely understandable that we have different viewpoints. In our diverse team,
it's important to respect these differences. Let's dig deeper and see if we can find some common
ground.”（我们持有不同观点，这完全可以理解。在我们多元化的团队中，尊重这些差异很重要。让我们
深入探讨，看看能否找到一些共识。）
• 高压力决策会议：“I see where you're coming from, but perhaps we could also look at this from a
different angle, considering the potential risks involved. Let's analyze all aspects before making a
decision.”（我明白您的观点，但或许我们也可从另一角度审视，考虑其中潜在的风险。在做决策前，让
我们分析所有方面。）
4. 解决问题（应急调整）
• 项目进度受阻会议：“How about we meet halfway on this? We could reallocate some resources to
4
speed up the critical tasks and ensure the project stays on track.”（在此事上，我们各让一步如何？
我们可以重新分配一些资源，加快关键任务的进度，确保项目按计划进行。）
• 突发状况会议：“Let's put our heads together and come up with some immediate solutions to this
problem. We need to act quickly to minimize the impact.”（让我们集思广益，为这个问题想出一些即
时的解决方案。我们需要迅速行动，将影响降至最低。）
5. 寻求他人意见（跨团队协作）
• 跨部门项目会议 ：“What are your thoughts on this new initiative from the perspective of your
department? We'd love to hear your insights to make this project a success.”（从你们部门的角度来
看，对于这一新举措有何见解？我们期待聆听你们的见解，让这个项目取得成功。）
• 多团队合作会议：“I'm interested in knowing your perspective, [Name]. How do you think we can
better integrate our teams' efforts to achieve our common goals?”（我很想了解您的看法，[姓名]。
您认为我们怎样能更好地整合各团队的力量，实现共同目标？）
6. 表示赞同
• 策略讨论会议：“I completely agree with your point. It aligns perfectly with our long - term business
strategy.”（我完全赞同您的观点，它与我们的长期商业战略完美契合。）
• 方案评审会议：“That's a great idea. I think it has the potential to revolutionize our current approach
and bring in more efficiency.”（这是个绝妙的主意。我认为它有潜力彻底改变我们目前的方法，提高效
率。）
7. 表示部分赞同
• 预算规划会议：“I see the merit in what you're saying, but I'm not sure about the budget allocation
for this particular item. Maybe we need to re - evaluate it based on our priorities.”（我明白您所言
的价值，但我对这个特定项目的预算分配存疑。或许我们需要根据优先级重新评估。）
• 产品规划会议：“I agree with the overall concept, however, the timeline for product development
might need some adjustment considering the complexity of the features.”（我认同整体概念，不过，
考虑到功能的复杂性，产品开发的时间表或许还需调整。）
8. 澄清与验证观点
要求重复或澄清
• 线上会议（信号不佳）：“Sorry, I missed that last part due to a poor connection. Could you please
repeat it?”（抱歉，由于网络不好，我没听清最后一部分。能否请您重复一下？）
• 概念复杂会议：“I'm not sure I follow. Could you clarify what you mean by 'agile methodology' in
this context? Maybe give a simple example.”（我不太明白。您能否解释一下在这个情境下‘敏捷方法’
具体指什么？或许举个简单例子。）
5
要求数据支持
• 市场调研会议：“That's an interesting idea, but could you back it up with some market research
data? For example, what's the market share of our competitors in this segment?”（这是个有趣的想
法，但您能否用一些市场调研数据支持？例如，我们竞争对手在这个细分市场的份额是多少？）
• 投资决策会议： “Before we make a decision, I'd like to see some financial data. What's the
projected return on investment for this project?”（在做决策前，我想看到一些财务数据。这个项目的
预计投资回报率是多少？）
9. 引入新话题
• 行业趋势会议：“Now that we've covered the current issues, let's move on to a new topic - the
impact of artificial intelligence on our industry.”（既然已探讨完当前问题，现在让我们进入新话题 —
— 人工智能对我们行业的影响。）
• 业务拓展会议：“We've made some good progress on the previous points. I'd like to bring up a new
aspect for discussion - the potential of entering international markets.”（我们在之前的要点上已取
得一定进展。我想提出一个新的讨论方向 —— 进入国际市场的潜力。）
10. 关联话题
• 营销与产品会议：“This new idea of improving customer retention is closely related to what we were
discussing earlier about enhancing our product features. How can we integrate these two
concepts to create a more comprehensive strategy?”（这个提高客户留存率的新想法，与我们之前讨
论的增强产品特性紧密相关。我们如何融合这两个概念，制定一个更全面的策略？）
• 财务与项目会议：“The issue of budget constraints ties in with our conversation about the new
project timeline. If we have limited funds, how will it affect the resource allocation and the overall
project schedule?”（预算限制问题，与我们关于新项目时间安排的讨论相关。若资金有限，将如何影响
资源分配和整个项目进度？）
11. 强调紧迫性
• 竞争压力会议 ： “This problem is urgent. Our competitors are already implementing similar
strategies. We need to find a solution as soon as possible to stay ahead in the market.”（此问题十
分紧迫。我们的竞争对手已经在实施类似策略。我们需尽快找到解决方案，以在市场中保持领先。）
• 项目截止日期临近会议：“The deadline for this decision is approaching rapidly. We can't afford to
procrastinate any longer. Let's focus and come up with a viable solution now.”（此决策的截止日期
迫在眉睫。我们不能再拖延了。现在让我们集中精力，想出一个可行的解决方案。）
12. 举例说明观点
• 市场案例会议：“For instance, when Company X faced a similar situation, they decided to rebrand
their product, and it led to a 30% increase in market share. Maybe we could consider a similar
6
approach.”（例如，当 X 公司面临类似情况时，他们决定重新打造产品品牌，结果市场份额增长了
30%。或许我们可考虑类似方法。）
• 内部经验会议：“Take the recent marketing campaign of Product Y as an example. They used social
media influencers effectively, and their brand awareness skyrocketed. We could learn from their
strategy and apply it to our upcoming campaigns.”（以 Y 产品近期的营销活动为例。他们有效利用
社交媒体影响者，品牌知名度迅速提升。我们可借鉴其策略，应用到我们即将开展的活动中。）
13. 对比不同观点
• 战略选择会议：“On one hand, the first proposal emphasizes cost - cutting measures to improve
our profit margin. On the other hand, the second proposal focuses on increasing revenue through
market expansion. We need to weigh the pros and cons of both based on our current situation.”
（一方面，第一个提案强调通过削减成本措施提高利润率；另一方面，第二个提案侧重于通过市场扩张
增加收入。我们需要根据当前情况权衡两者利弊。）
• 产品策略会议：“The traditional approach suggests a slow and steady roll - out of the new product,
while the modern approach advocates for a rapid and aggressive launch. How do these two
approaches stack up against each other in terms of risk and return?”（传统方法建议新产品缓慢稳
步推出，而现代方法主张快速激进发布。从风险和回报的角度来看，这两种方法相较如何？）

文化与语言适配指南
标注文化注意事项
• 表达分歧：在东亚团队中，建议使用 “或许我们可以补充另一个视角 …” 替代直接否定，例如 “I think
we might also consider another perspective here. What if we look at it from the aspect of [具体方
面]?”。在一些注重等级制度的文化中，下级对上级表达不同意见时，语言应更委婉、谦逊，如 “With all
due respect, I wonder if we could explore another option...”。在拉丁文化中，人们交流较为热情直
接，表达分歧时可以适当运用一些情感性的表达，但也要注意尊重他人观点，例如 “I understand your
point, but I'm really passionate about this other idea we could try...”。
• 赞美与感谢：在北欧文化中，赞美通常比较简洁、实际，避免过度夸张，比如 “That's a really solid
plan. Well done.”；而在南欧文化中，赞美会更热情洋溢，像 “This is an absolutely brilliant idea!
You've really outdone yourself.”。在日本，表达感谢时除了常用的 “Thank you”，还可以加上具体的
感谢原因，如 “Thank you so much for your detailed report. It's been extremely helpful.”。
提供非母语者支持策略
• 遇到复杂术语时补充简单定义：在会议中使用复杂术语后，及时用简单易懂的语言解释其含义，例如
“We're going to focus on 'disruptive innovation' here. Simply put, it means creating new products
or services that change the existing market in a significant way.”。
• 主动确认理解：经常询问参会人员是否理解，如 “Am I making sense so far?” “Does everyone follow
what I'm saying?”，给非母语者提供反馈的机会。如果发现有人不理解，换一种表达方式重新阐述，比
如 “Let me explain that in a different way. Instead of [原表述], think of it like this...”。
• 放慢语速与简化句子结构： 讲话时适当放慢语速， 避免使用过于复杂的长难句， 将复杂的想法拆分成简单
的句子表达。例如，不要说 “The paradigm shift we're considering, which has been a topic of
extensive debate in our industry, will require a comprehensive and multifaceted approach to
implementation.”，可以改为 “We're thinking about a big change. It's been talked about a lot in our
15
industry. To make it work, we need to have a complete and multi - step plan.”。
