关系（中国）
—— "Guanxi"：A unique Chinese concept referring to the interpersonal network built on trust, mutual benefit, and long-term interactions, which plays a crucial role in business negotiations and cooperation. （中国特有的概念，指基于信任、互利及长期交往建立的人际网络，在商务谈判与合作中起关键作用。）
斋月调整（中东国家）
—— "Ramadan adjustments"：Measures taken to adapt to the Islamic holy month of Ramadan, such as adjusting negotiation times to avoid fasting hours or shortening meeting durations. （为适应伊斯兰教斋月而采取的调整措施，如调整谈判时间以避开斋戒时段或缩短会议时长。）
圩田模式（荷兰）
—— "Polder model"：A Dutch negotiation approach emphasizing consensus-building through equal dialogue, compromise, and collaboration among all stakeholders, rather than one-sided decision-making. （荷兰的谈判模式，强调通过所有利益相关者平等对话、妥协与协作达成共识，而非单方面决策。）
Pakikisama（菲律宾）
—— "Pakikisama"：A Filipino cultural term meaning maintaining harmonious relationships, fitting in with the group, and avoiding conflict, which influences communication styles and decision-making in business negotiations. （菲律宾文化术语，指维护和谐关系、融入群体、避免冲突，影响商务谈判中的沟通方式与决策过程。）
合十礼（印度 / 泰国等南亚、东南亚国家）
—— "Namaste/Wai gesture"：A traditional greeting gesture （palms pressed together at chest level with a slight bow） in India （Namaste）, Thailand （Wai）, and other countries, symbolizing respect and humility in business meetings. （印度（Namaste）、泰国（Wai）等国的传统问候手势（双手胸前合十并轻微鞠躬），在商务会面中象征尊重与谦逊。）
家族决策（中东 / 部分东亚国家）
—— "Family-based decision-making"：A decision-making model common in Middle Eastern and some East Asian cultures, where family members （especially senior relatives） participate in and influence business negotiation decisions. （在中东及部分东亚文化中常见的决策模式，家族成员（尤其长辈）参与并影响商务谈判决策。）
直接沟通（欧美国家）
—— "Direct communication style"：A communication approach typical in Western countries （Europe, America）, characterized by straightforward expression of opinions, focusing on key points, and avoiding vague or indirect language in negotiations. （欧美国家典型的沟通方式，特点是在谈判中直白表达观点、聚焦重点，避免模糊或含蓄的表述。）
礼尚往来（中国）
—— "Reciprocal courtesy"：A Chinese cultural principle emphasizing mutual respect and mutual benefit in interactions; in negotiations, it often manifests as exchanging favors, such as offering reasonable concessions in return for the other party's cooperation. （中国文化中的原则，强调交往中相互尊重、互利互惠；在谈判中常体现为礼尚往来，如为换取对方合作而做出合理让步。）
谦逊表达（日本 / 韩国等东亚国家）
—— "Humble expression"：A communication trait in East Asian cultures like Japan and South Korea, where speakers downplay their own achievements or advantages and use tentative language to avoid appearing arrogant during negotiations. （日本、韩国等东亚文化中的沟通特质，说话者在谈判中淡化自身成就或优势，使用试探性语言，避免显得傲慢。）
款待文化（中东国家）
—— "Hospitality culture"：A core cultural value in Middle Eastern countries, where hosts show respect to negotiation partners through generous hospitality （e.g., offering tea, snacks）, which helps build trust and promote cooperation. （中东国家的核心文化价值观，主人通过热情款待（如提供茶点）向谈判伙伴表达尊重，助力建立信任、推动合作。）
集体优先（日本 / 韩国等东亚国家）
—— "Collectivism priority"：A cultural orientation in East Asia that values group interests over individual interests; in negotiations, decisions often prioritize the team's or company's overall goals rather than personal preferences. （东亚文化中的取向，重视集体利益高于个人利益；在谈判中，决策常优先考虑团队或公司整体目标，而非个人偏好。）
非正式沟通（澳大利亚 / 新西兰）
—— "Informal communication style"：A relaxed communication approach common in Australia and New Zealand, featuring casual language, early use of first names, and minimal emphasis on hierarchy in business negotiations. （澳大利亚、新西兰常见的轻松沟通方式，特点是语言随意、较早使用名字称呼、商务谈判中对等级强调较少。）
宗教禁忌规避（中东 / 东南亚部分国家）
—— "Religious taboo avoidance"：The practice of avoiding topics, actions, or arrangements that conflict with local religious beliefs （e.g., avoiding pork-related discussions in Islamic countries） during international negotiations. （国际谈判中避免涉及与当地宗教信仰冲突的话题、行为或安排（如在伊斯兰国家避免讨论猪肉相关内容）的做法。）
长期导向（中国 / 日本等东亚国家）
—— "Long-term orientation"：A cultural characteristic in East Asia that focuses on long-term cooperative relationships rather than short-term gains; in negotiations, more attention is paid to building sustainable partnerships than immediate contract signing. （东亚文化中的特质，聚焦长期合作关系而非短期利益；在谈判中，更注重建立可持续伙伴关系，而非急于签署即时合同。）
个人主义表达（美国 / 加拿大等北美国家）
—— "Individualistic expression"：A communication trait in North American cultures where individuals freely express personal opinions, take individual responsibility, and prioritize personal contributions in negotiation discussions. （北美文化中的沟通特质，个人可自由表达观点、承担个人责任，在谈判讨论中重视个人贡献。）
等级尊重（韩国 / 印度等国家）
—— "Hierarchy respect"：A cultural norm in South Korea, India, and other countries that requires showing extra respect to those with higher positions （e.g., greeting seniors first, using formal titles） in business negotiations. （韩国、印度等国的文化规范，要求在商务谈判中对职位较高者表示额外尊重（如优先问候长辈、使用正式头衔）。）
灵活议价（南亚 / 拉美国家）
—— "Flexible bargaining"：A negotiation strategy common in South Asia and Latin America, involving repeated discussions on prices, terms, or benefits, with both parties making gradual concessions to reach an agreement. （南亚、拉美国家常见的谈判策略，涉及对价格、条款或利益的反复讨论，双方逐步让步以达成协议。）
礼仪性道歉（日本）
—— "Ceremonial apology"：A Japanese cultural practice of offering apologies not only for actual mistakes but also to show empathy, maintain harmony, or avoid making the other party uncomfortable during negotiations. （日本的文化做法，道歉不仅针对实际错误，也用于表达共情、维护和谐或避免让谈判对方感到不适。）
社区意见参考（非洲部分国家）
—— "Community opinion reference"：A decision-making factor in some African countries, where business negotiation decisions may refer to the opinions of local communities, elders, or tribal leaders to ensure social acceptance. （部分非洲国家的决策因素，商务谈判决策可能参考当地社区、长辈或部落领袖的意见，以确保获得社会认可。）
书面合同重视（德国 / 美国等国家）
—— "Written contract emphasis"：A business culture trait in Germany, the U.S., and other countries that values detailed, legally binding written contracts, with clear terms and strict adherence to agreement clauses in negotiations. （德国、美国等国的商业文化特质，重视详细、具法律约束力的书面合同，谈判中条款明确且严格遵守协议内容。）
情感联结建立（拉美国家）
—— "Emotional connection building"：A key step in Latin American business negotiations, involving building personal trust and emotional rapport through casual conversations, social events, or shared experiences before discussing formal terms. （拉美商务谈判中的关键步骤，在讨论正式条款前，通过闲聊、社交活动或共同经历建立个人信任与情感联结。）
含蓄拒绝（日本 / 中国等东亚国家）
—— "Indirect refusal"：A communication method in East Asian cultures where rejection of proposals is expressed indirectly （e.g., saying "we need to consider further" instead of "no"） to maintain the other party's "face" during negotiations. （东亚文化中的沟通方式，拒绝方案时采用含蓄表达（如用 “我们需进一步考虑” 代替 “不行”），以在谈判中维护对方 “面子”。）
时间灵活性（中东 / 东南亚国家）
—— "Time flexibility"：A cultural attitude towards time in the Middle East and Southeast Asia, where negotiation schedules may be adjusted due to religious events, personal matters, or unexpected situations, without strict adherence to fixed timelines. （中东、东南亚国家对时间的文化态度，谈判日程可能因宗教活动、个人事务或突发情况调整，不严格遵守固定时限。）
专业头衔使用（德国 / 奥地利等欧洲国家）
—— "Professional title usage"：A formal etiquette practice in Germany, Austria, and other European countries, where individuals are addressed by their professional titles （e.g., "Professor," "Engineer"） in business negotiations to show respect for their expertise. （德国、奥地利等欧洲国家的正式礼仪做法，在商务谈判中用专业头衔（如 “教授”“工程师”）称呼对方，以尊重其专业能力。）
斡旋沟通（中国 / 东南亚部分国家）
—— "Mediation-based communication"：A communication method common in China and some Southeast Asian countries, where a third party （intermediary） helps convey information, resolve conflicts, and promote consensus between negotiating parties to avoid direct confrontation. （中国及部分东南亚国家常见的沟通方式，由第三方（中间人）协助传递信息、化解矛盾、推动谈判双方达成共识，避免直接对抗。）
宗教节日避让（中东 / 南亚国家）
—— "Religious festival avoidance"：The practice of adjusting negotiation schedules to avoid important religious festivals （e.g., Eid al-Fitr in Islamic countries, Diwali in India） to show respect for the other party's religious beliefs and ensure their full focus on negotiations. （调整谈判日程以避开重要宗教节日（如伊斯兰国家的开斋节、印度的排灯节）的做法，体现对对方宗教信仰的尊重，确保其能全身心投入谈判。）
团队共识决策（日本 / 韩国）
—— "Team consensus decision-making"：A decision-making model in Japan and South Korea where negotiation decisions are made after full discussion and agreement within the team, rather than by individual leaders, to ensure alignment with collective interests. （日本、韩国的决策模式，谈判决策需经团队内部充分讨论并达成一致后确定，而非由个别领导者单独决定，以确保符合集体利益。）
非正式社交铺垫（拉美 / 非洲国家）
—— "Informal social groundwork"：A preparatory step in Latin American and African business negotiations, where parties engage in informal social activities （e.g., dinners, cultural outings） before formal talks to build trust and familiarity, which lays the foundation for smooth negotiations. （拉美、非洲商务谈判中的准备步骤，正式谈判前双方通过非正式社交活动（如聚餐、文化参观）建立信任与熟悉度，为顺利谈判奠定基础。）
契约精神强调（德国 / 瑞士等欧洲国家）
—— "Contractual spirit emphasis"：A core business value in Germany, Switzerland, and other European countries, emphasizing strict adherence to contract terms, integrity in fulfilling commitments, and resolution of disputes based on contractual agreements during negotiations. （德国、瑞士等欧洲国家的核心商业价值观，强调谈判中严格遵守合同条款、诚信履行承诺，以及依据合同约定解决争议。）
面子维护策略（中国 / 日本）
—— "Face-maintaining strategy"：Tactics used in Chinese and Japanese business negotiations to protect the other party's dignity and reputation, such as using indirect language when pointing out problems, acknowledging the other party's efforts before proposing revisions, and avoiding public criticism. （中国、日本商务谈判中用于维护对方尊严与声誉的策略，如指出问题时使用含蓄语言、提出修改意见前先认可对方努力、避免公开批评等。）
人情往来（中国）
—— "Human relationship exchanges"：A Chinese cultural practice in business negotiations involving mutual assistance, favor-giving, and maintaining regular contact beyond formal business, which helps strengthen interpersonal bonds and promote long-term cooperation. （中国商务谈判中的文化做法，包括相互协助、赠送人情、在正式业务之外保持日常联系等，有助于巩固人际联结，推动长期合作。）
快速决策导向（美国 / 加拿大）
—— "Fast decision-making orientation"：A cultural trait in the U.S. and Canada where business negotiations prioritize efficiency, with a focus on quick confirmation of key terms, avoidance of excessive delays, and timely finalization of agreements. （美国、加拿大的文化特质，商务谈判中重视效率，聚焦快速确认核心条款、避免过度拖延、及时敲定协议。）
文化禁忌敏感（中东 / 东南亚国家）
—— "Cultural taboo sensitivity"：The awareness and attention paid to local cultural taboos （e.g., avoiding alcohol in Islamic countries, avoiding certain hand gestures in Southeast Asia） during international negotiations to prevent offending the other party and damaging the negotiation atmosphere. （国际谈判中对当地文化禁忌（如伊斯兰国家避免提及酒精、东南亚国家避免特定手势）的认知与重视，以防止冒犯对方、破坏谈判氛围。）
层级汇报机制（韩国 / 印度）
—— "Hierarchical reporting mechanism"：A business process in South Korea and India where negotiation progress, key issues, and proposed solutions must be reported to superiors for approval before further action, which may affect the speed of decision-making in negotiations. （韩国、印度的商业流程，谈判进展、核心问题及解决方案需向上级汇报并获得批准后才能推进下一步，可能影响谈判决策速度。）
利益共享导向（中国 / 东南亚国家）
—— "Interest-sharing orientation"：A negotiation concept in China and Southeast Asian countries that emphasizes mutual benefit, win-win outcomes, and distribution of interests based on fair cooperation, rather than pursuing one-sided gains. （中国、东南亚国家的谈判理念，强调互利共赢、基于公平合作分配利益，而非追求单方面收益。）
礼仪性赠礼（日本 / 韩国）
—— "Ceremonial gift-giving"：A traditional practice in Japanese and Korean business negotiations where gifts are exchanged to show respect, gratitude, and goodwill; gifts are usually carefully selected to avoid taboos （e.g., avoiding sharp objects in Japan） and reflect sincerity. （日本、韩国商务谈判中的传统做法，通过交换礼物表达尊重、感谢与善意；礼物通常经过精心挑选，避免禁忌（如日本避免送尖锐物品），体现诚意。）
直接议价风格（美国 / 澳大利亚）
—— "Direct bargaining style"：A negotiation approach in the U.S. and Australia where parties openly state their price expectations, make clear concessions, and directly discuss differences in pricing, without excessive reliance on indirect hints or vague expressions. （美国、澳大利亚的谈判方式，双方公开表明价格预期、做出明确让步、直接讨论价格分歧，无需过度依赖含蓄暗示或模糊表述。）
长期信任培育（日本 / 中国）
—— "Long-term trust cultivation"：A focus in Japanese and Chinese business negotiations on building long-term trust through consistent actions, reliable performance, and honest communication, with the belief that trust is the foundation of sustainable cooperation. （日本、中国商务谈判中对长期信任的培育，通过持续行动、可靠表现、坦诚沟通建立信任，认为信任是可持续合作的基础。）
社区利益考量（非洲部分国家）
—— "Community interest consideration"：A factor in business negotiations in some African countries where the impact of cooperation on local communities （e.g., employment opportunities, social welfare） is taken into account to gain community support and ensure the smooth implementation of cooperative projects. （部分非洲国家商务谈判中的考量因素，需考虑合作对当地社区的影响（如就业机会、社会福利），以获得社区支持，确保合作项目顺利实施。）
正式称谓使用（德国 / 法国）
—— "Formal address usage"：A business etiquette practice in Germany and France where individuals are addressed by their last name with formal titles （e.g., "Herr" in Germany, "Monsieur" in France） in initial business negotiations, and first names are only used after a certain level of familiarity is established. （德国、法国的商务礼仪做法，初次商务谈判中用姓氏加正式头衔（如德国的 “Herr”、法国的 “Monsieur”）称呼对方，仅在建立一定熟悉度后才使用名字。）
柔性沟通技巧（日本 / 泰国）
—— "Flexible communication skills"：Communication techniques used in Japanese and Thai business negotiations, such as using tentative language （"maybe," "we could consider"）, listening patiently to the other party's opinions, and making adjustments based on the other party's reactions to maintain a harmonious atmosphere. （日本、泰国商务谈判中使用的沟通技巧，如使用试探性语言（“或许”“我们可以考虑”）、耐心倾听对方意见、根据对方反应调整表达，以维护和谐氛围。）
家族利益优先（中东国家）
—— "Family interest priority"：A cultural principle in Middle Eastern business negotiations where the interests of the family （e.g., family-owned businesses） take precedence over individual or short-term business interests, and family members' opinions have a significant impact on negotiation decisions. （中东商务谈判中的文化原则，家族利益（如家族企业利益）优先于个人或短期商业利益，家族成员的意见对谈判决策有重要影响。）
结果导向谈判（英国 / 加拿大）
—— "Result-oriented negotiation"：A negotiation style in the UK and Canada that focuses on achieving specific goals （e.g., confirming contract terms, resolving disputes）, with clear agendas, efficient discussions, and minimal time spent on unrelated topics. （英国、加拿大的谈判风格，聚焦实现特定目标（如确认合同条款、解决争议），议程明确、讨论高效，较少在无关话题上耗时。）
文化适应能力（跨国谈判）
—— "Cultural adaptability"：The ability to adjust one's communication style, negotiation strategy, and behavior according to the cultural background of the other party in cross-border negotiations, which is crucial for building mutual understanding and promoting cooperation. （跨国谈判中根据对方文化背景调整自身沟通方式、谈判策略与行为的能力，对建立相互理解、推动合作至关重要。）
