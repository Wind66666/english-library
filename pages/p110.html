商务电话沟通
无非就这600句，足够了
一、电话开头寒暄
Good morning/afternoon/evening, this is [Your Name] calling from [Company Name].
（早上 / 下午 / 晚上好，我是来自 [公司名称] 的 [你的名字]。）
Hello, it's a pleasure to speak with you.
（你好，很高兴能和你通话。）
Hi, this is [Your Name]. I hope you're well.
（嗨，我是 [你的名字]。希望你一切都好。）
Good [morning/afternoon/evening], may I speak with [Person's Name], please?
（早上 / 下午 / 晚上好，我可以和 [对方名字] 通话吗？）
This is [Your Name] calling to discuss [reason for the call].
（我是 [你的名字]，打电话来讨论 [打电话的原因]。）
How are you today? I trust everything is going smoothly at your end.
（你今天好吗？我相信你那边一切都进展顺利。）
May I have a moment of your time to discuss [topic]?
（我能占用一点你的时间来讨论 [主题] 吗？）
I hope I'm not catching you at a bad time.
（我希望我没有在你不方便的时候打扰你。）
Is this a convenient time for a brief discussion?
（现在方便进行一个简短的讨论吗？）
May I ask who is speaking, please?
（请问您是哪位？）
May I ask who I am speaking with?
（请问我正在和谁通话？）
What can I do for you? / Can I help you?
（我能帮您什么吗？）
May I speak to the person in charge of purchasing?
（请问我可以和负责采购的人通话吗？）
I'd like to introduce our products to you.
（我想向您介绍我们的产品。）
We specialize in [Product/Service].
（我们专注于 [产品 / 服务]。）
Could you tell me who handles your [Product/Service] needs?
（您能告诉我谁负责贵公司 [产品 / 服务] 的需求吗？）
I understand you're busy, so I'll keep this brief.
（我知道您很忙，所以我会尽量简短。）
Have you heard of our company before?
（您之前听说过我们公司吗？）
Good day, this is [Your Name] from [Company Name]. I’m following up on our previous email exchange.
（您好，我是 [公司名称] 的 [你的名字]。我来跟进一下我们之前的邮件沟通。）
Hi there, it’s [Your Name] calling. I wanted to touch base regarding [matter] we talked about last week.
（你好，我是 [你的名字]。我想就上周我们讨论过的 [事情] 跟进一下。）
Good [morning/afternoon/evening], I’m [Your Name] from [Company Name]. Is this a good time to chat about [topic]?
（早上 / 下午 / 晚上好，我是 [公司名称] 的 [你的名字]。现在方便聊一下 [主题] 吗？）
Hello, this is [Your Name]. I’m reaching out to see if you have any updates on [issue we discussed].
（你好，我是 [你的名字]。我来了解一下关于我们之前讨论的 [问题] 是否有最新进展。）
Hi, [Person's Name] recommended I give you a call. I’m [Your Name] from [Company Name], and I’d like to talk about [collaboration opportunity].
（嗨，[对方名字] 推荐我给您打电话。我是 [公司名称] 的 [你的名字]，想和您聊聊 [合作机会]。）
Good morning/afternoon/evening, I hope this call finds you in good spirits. This is [Your Name] from [Company Name].
（早上 / 下午 / 晚上好，希望您心情愉快。我是 [公司名称] 的 [你的名字]。）
Hello, it’s [Your Name] calling. I wanted to check if you’ve had a chance to review the [document/proposal] I sent earlier.
（你好，我是 [你的名字]。我想确认一下您是否有时间查看我之前发送的 [文件 / 提案]。）
Hi, this is [Your Name] from [Company Name]. I’m calling to provide you with some updates on [project/order].
（嗨，我是 [公司名称] 的 [你的名字]。我打电话来给您提供关于 [项目 / 订单] 的一些最新情况。）
Good [morning/afternoon/evening], may I speak with someone who can assist with [specific issue], please?
（早上 / 下午 / 晚上好，请问我可以和能处理 [具体问题] 的人通话吗？）
Hello, I’m [Your Name] from [Company Name]. I heard you might be interested in [our product/service], so I wanted to share more details.
（你好，我是 [公司名称] 的 [你的名字]。听说您可能对 [我们的产品 / 服务] 感兴趣，所以想和您分享更多细节。）
Hi, it’s [Your Name] calling. I wanted to apologize for the delayed response earlier and discuss [matter] now.
（嗨，我是 [你的名字]。我想为之前的延迟回复道歉，现在想和您讨论 [事情]。）
Good day, this is [Your Name] from [Company Name]. I’m calling to invite you to [event/meeting] we’re hosting next month.
（您好，我是 [公司名称] 的 [你的名字]。我打电话来邀请您参加我们下个月举办的 [活动 / 会议]。）
二、请求重复 / 澄清（应对听不清、听不懂）
Will you please repeat it? / Sorry, could you please repeat again? I can not hear clearly.
（请你重说一遍好吗？/ 抱歉，我没听清楚，能再重复一遍吗？）
I'm sorry, I didn't get that clearly. Could you say it again please?
（不好意思，我没听清楚，可以再重复一遍吗？）
Could you spell that for me please?
（可以拼写一下吗？）
Ok, let me just repeat that back to you.
（好的，让我重复一遍跟你确认一下。）
I'm sorry, could you speak a bit slower?
（抱歉，可以请你说慢一点吗？）
I'm sorry, I didn't catch that. Could you please explain it in a different way?
（抱歉，我没听懂。你可以用另一种方式解释一下吗？）
Would you mind slowing down?
（您能说慢一点吗？）
Would you mind speaking up a little bit?
（您能说大声一点吗？）
I'm sorry, could you speak up, please? / Would you mind speaking up a bit?
（您能说大声一点吗？）
I'm sorry I can't hear you very well. I think we have a bad connection.
（不好意思，线路不好，我听不太清楚。）
My English is poor, but I don't want to miss anything. Could we contact through email?
（我的英文不太好，我不想错过任何信息，我们可以通过邮件联系吗？）
I understand what you said, but could you send an email to me to confirm, please?
（我了解您讲的，但是请您再发一封邮件确认一下，好吗？）
I’m afraid I missed the last part of what you said. Could you go over that again?
（恐怕我没听到您说的最后一部分。您能再讲一遍吗？）
Sorry, the line is really fuzzy. Could you repeat the key points once more?
（抱歉，线路太模糊了。您能再重复一下关键点吗？）
I didn't quite grasp the details about [specific part]. Could you clarify that for me?
（我不太理解关于 [具体部分] 的细节。您能给我解释一下吗？）
Would you mind rephrasing that sentence? I’m not sure I understand the meaning.
（您能换种说法吗？我不确定我理解了意思。）
I think there’s some interference on the line. Could you speak a bit clearer?
（我觉得线路有干扰。您能说清楚一点吗？）
Sorry, I got distracted for a second. Could you repeat what you just said about [topic]?
（抱歉，我刚才分神了。您能重复一下刚才关于 [主题] 说的话吗？）
I’m not familiar with that term. Could you explain what [term] means in this context?
（我对那个术语不太熟悉。您能解释一下 [术语] 在这个语境里是什么意思吗？）
Could you break that down into smaller parts? It’s a bit too complex for me to follow right now.
（您能把它拆分成几个小部分说吗？现在对我来说有点太复杂了，跟不上。）
I’m sorry, I can’t make out the name you mentioned. Could you spell it letter by letter?
（抱歉，我听不清您提到的名字。您能一个字母一个字母地拼写吗？）
Did you say [something you think you heard]? Or did I get that wrong?
（您是说 [你认为自己听到的内容] 吗？还是我听错了？）
The sound is cutting out. Could you pause between each point so I can catch everything?
（声音断断续续的。您能在每个要点之间停顿一下，让我都听清楚吗？）
I’m confused about [specific part]. Could you give an example to illustrate?
（我对 [具体部分] 很困惑。您能举个例子说明一下吗？）
Sorry, I didn’t catch the number you said. Could you repeat it slowly?
（抱歉，我没听清您说的数字。您能慢慢重复一遍吗？）
I’m not sure if I understand correctly. When you talked about [matter], did you mean [your understanding]?
（我不确定我理解得对不对。您在说 [事情] 的时候，是指 [你的理解] 吗？）
The line is very noisy. Could you move to a quieter place if possible, or repeat your words?
（线路很吵。如果可以的话，您能换到安静一点的地方，或者重复一下您的话吗？）
I missed the deadline you mentioned. Could you tell me when it is again?
（我没听清您提到的截止日期。您能再告诉我是哪天吗？）
Sorry, I can’t hear the details of the plan. Could you go through each step again?
（抱歉，我听不清计划的细节。您能再把每个步骤说一遍吗？）
I’m not clear on who is responsible for [task]. Could you clarify that?
（我不清楚谁负责 [任务]。您能解释一下吗？）
Did you mention a meeting time earlier? I didn’t catch it. Could you repeat it?
（您之前提到会议时间了吗？我没听清。您能重复一下吗？）
The connection is really bad. Could you speak in shorter sentences so I can follow?
（信号真的很差。您能说短一点的句子，让我能跟上吗？）
I’m sorry, I didn’t understand the requirement you stated. Could you rephrase it?
（抱歉，我没理解您说的要求。您能换种说法吗？）
I can’t hear the location you mentioned. Could you tell me where it is again?
（我听不清您提到的地点。您能再告诉我是哪里吗？）
I’m confused about the next step. Could you explain what we need to do first?
（我对下一步该做什么很困惑。您能解释一下我们首先需要做什么吗？）
三、确认 / 核实细节
Could you please confirm the details we discussed earlier?
（您能确认一下我们之前讨论的细节吗？）
I'd like to verify some information to ensure its accuracy.
（我想核实一些信息以确保其准确性。）
Just to double-check, are we still on track with the plan we outlined?
（再次确认一下，我们是否还在按照我们概述的计划进行？）
Can you confirm the date and time for the upcoming meeting?
（您能确认一下即将举行的会议的日期和时间吗？）
I want to make sure I have the correct details before moving forward.
（在继续之前，我想确认一下我是否有正确的细节。）
Let's go over the specifics one more time to avoid any misunderstandings.
（让我们再次详细核对一下，以避免任何误解。）
Could you verify the accuracy of the figures provided in the report?
（您能核实一下报告中提供的数字的准确性吗？）
I just want to confirm the delivery address for the upcoming shipment.
（我只是想确认一下即将发货的地址。）
To ensure clarity, could you repeat the key points we agreed upon?
（为了确保清晰，您能重复一下我们达成一致的关键点吗？）
Let's confirm the details of the contract before finalizing the agreement.
（在最终敲定协议之前，让我们确认一下合同的细节。）
Can you please confirm your zip code?
（核实一下您的邮编好吗？）
Is this still your current billing address?
（这还是您当前的账单地址吗？）
How would you like to pay for the order? We accept PayPal, Western Union, or all major credit cards.
（您希望用什么方式支付订单？我们接受 PayPal、西联汇款或所有主流信用卡。）
Let me see if I got that right. When you say..., do you mean...? / So if I understand you correctly..../ Just to clarify, you said...
（我再跟您确认一下，您是说…… 对吗？/ 如果我理解得没错的话……/ 澄清一下，您刚才说……）
I'm not sure I understand. Did you mean...?
（我不确定我理解对了，您的意思是…… 吗？）
Could you confirm whether the project will start on [date] as we discussed?
（您能确认项目是否会像我们讨论的那样在 [日期] 开始吗？）
I want to verify the quantity of the products you ordered. Is it [number] units?
（我想核实您订购的产品数量。是 [数字] 件吗？）
Just to make sure, the payment terms we agreed on are [terms], right?
（只是确认一下，我们达成一致的付款条款是 [条款]，对吗？）
Can you confirm the contact person for this project at your company? Is it [Person's Name]?
（您能确认贵公司负责这个项目的联系人吗？是 [对方名字] 吗？）
I need to 核实 the delivery method you prefer. Do you want it shipped by air or sea?
（我需要核实您偏好的交货方式。您想空运还是海运？）
Let's confirm the scope of the work again to avoid any confusion later.
（让我们再次确认工作范围，以避免之后出现混乱。）
Could you verify the email address I should send the documents to? Is it [email]?
（您能核实一下我应该发送文件的邮箱地址吗？是 [邮箱] 吗？）
Just to double-check, the meeting will be held at [location], correct?
（再次确认一下，会议将在 [地点] 举行，对吗？）
I want to make sure the quality standards we need to meet are [standards]. Did I get that right?
（我想确认我们需要达到的质量标准是 [标准]。我理解得对吗？）
Can you confirm the lead time for the order? Will it take [time period] to deliver?
（您能确认订单的交货周期吗？需要 [时间段] 才能交货吗？）
I need to verify the price per unit of the product. Is it [amount] per piece?
（我需要核实产品的单价。是每件 [金额] 吗？）
Let's confirm the deadline for submitting the proposal. Is it by [date]?
（让我们确认提交提案的截止日期。是在 [日期] 之前吗？）
Could you confirm if you have received the sample we sent last week?
（您能确认是否收到了我们上周寄的样品吗？）
Just to clarify, the warranty period for the product is [period], isn't it?
（只是澄清一下，产品的保修期是 [时间段]，对吗？）
I want to make sure the changes you requested to the design are [changes]. Did I miss anything?
（我想确认您要求的设计修改是 [修改内容]。我有遗漏什么吗？）
Can you confirm the number of participants expected for the training session?
（您能确认参加培训课程的预计人数吗？）
I need to verify the terms of the contract regarding returns. Are returns allowed within [time] of delivery?
（我需要核实合同中关于退货的条款。交货后 [时间] 内允许退货吗？）
Let's confirm the schedule for the next phase of the project. Will it begin on [date]?
（让我们确认项目下一阶段的时间表。会在 [日期] 开始吗？）
Could you confirm the name of your company's bank for the wire transfer?
（您能确认贵公司用于电汇的银行名称吗？）
Just to make sure, the report should include [contents], right?
（只是确认一下，报告应该包含 [内容]，对吗？）
I want to verify the phone number I can reach you at for urgent matters. Is it [phone number]?
（我想核实紧急情况下可以联系到您的电话号码。是 [电话号码] 吗？）
Can you confirm if there are any additional requirements we need to meet for this order?
（您能确认这个订单我们是否还需要满足其他要求吗？）
Let's confirm the frequency of the progress updates you need. Do you want them weekly or monthly?
（让我们确认您需要的进度更新频率。您想要每周一次还是每月一次？）
Could you verify the weight of each package for shipping? Is it [weight] per package?
（您能核实每个运输包裹的重量吗？每个包裹是 [重量] 吗？）
Just to double-check, the discount we agreed on for this order is [percentage]%, correct?
（再次确认一下，这个订单我们达成一致的折扣是 [百分比]%，对吗？）
四、跟进相关事项
（1）跟进订单
I'm calling to follow up on the status of our recent order.
（我打电话来跟进我们最近订单的状态。）
Could you provide an update on the progress of our order placed on [date]?
（您能提供一下我们在 [日期] 下的订单的进展情况吗？）
I wanted to check in and see if there are any updates on the delivery schedule.
（我想了解一下，交货时间表是否有更新？）
We are interested in receiving confirmation on the estimated delivery date for our order.
（我们希望确认我们订单的预计交货日期。）
May I inquire about the current status of our order in your system?
（我可以了解一下我们的订单在贵系统中的当前状态吗？）
I'm reaching out to ensure that all necessary documentation for the order has been processed.
（我联系您是为了确保订单的所有必要文件都已处理完毕。）
Is there any additional information required from our end to facilitate the order fulfillment?
（我们这边是否还需要提供其他信息以推进订单完成？）
Can you confirm that the payment for the order has been received and processed?
（您能确认订单的付款已收到并处理完毕吗？）
I'd appreciate an update on any potential delays or issues with our order.
（若我们的订单有任何潜在延迟或问题，希望您能告知最新情况。）
I'm reaching out about the email I sent on Thursday. Did you have a chance to review it?
（我来跟进一下周四发给您的邮件，您有时间查看吗？）
I sent you the proforma invoice yesterday. Did you have a chance to look at it?
（我昨天给您发了形式发票，您有时间查看吗？）
The design mock-up has been sent by email. Just wanted to check if you received it.
（设计小样已通过邮件发送，想确认一下您是否收到。）
Is there any progress on the project? We need your feedback on our last discussion to move forward.
（项目有进展吗？我们需要您对上次讨论的反馈才能继续推进。）
I'd like to check if everything is clear with the quotation I sent you last week.
（想确认一下，上周发给您的报价单是否有不清楚的地方？）
Would you be able to finalize all the details this morning? We need to start production today, otherwise, we won't be able to deliver as scheduled.
（您今天上午能敲定所有细节吗？我们需要今天开始生产，否则无法按计划交货。）
I’m calling to follow up on our order # [order number]. Has it entered the production stage yet?
（我打电话来跟进我们的订单# [订单号]。它已经进入生产阶段了吗？）
Could you let me know if there are any problems with the order we placed on [date]? We haven’t received any updates recently.
（您能告诉我我们在 [日期] 下的订单是否有任何问题吗？我们最近没有收到任何更新。）
I wanted to check the status of the shipment for our order. Is it already on its way?
（我想查看我们订单的货运状态。已经在运输途中了吗？）
We are concerned about the delivery time of the order. Can you provide a more accurate estimated arrival date?
（我们很担心订单的交货时间。您能提供更准确的预计到达日期吗？）
I’m reaching out to confirm if all the materials for our order are in stock. Will there be any delays due to material shortages?
（我联系您是为了确认我们订单所需的所有材料是否有库存。会因为材料短缺而出现延迟吗？）
Did you encounter any issues while processing our order? We are ready to provide any necessary support.
（在处理我们的订单时，您遇到任何问题了吗？我们随时准备提供必要的支持。）
I’d like to know if the order has been inspected and passed quality control.
（我想知道订单是否已经过检验并通过质量控制。）
We haven’t received the shipping confirmation for our order yet. Could you send it to us as soon as possible?
（我们还没有收到订单的发货确认。您能尽快发给我们吗？）
I’m calling to follow up on the changes we requested for the order. Have they been implemented?
（我打电话来跟进我们为订单要求的修改。已经实施了吗？）
Can you confirm the number of items in the order? We want to make sure it matches our original request.
（您能确认订单中的物品数量吗？我们想确保它与我们最初的要求一致。）
I wanted to check if the delivery address for the order is correct. Is it still [address]?
（我想确认订单的交货地址是否正确。还是 [地址] 吗？）
We are waiting for the invoice for the order to make the payment. Could you send it to us soon?
（我们在等订单的发票以便付款。您能尽快发给我们吗？）
Did you need any additional information from us to complete the order? Please let us know.
（完成订单还需要我们提供任何额外信息吗？请告知我们。）
I’m reaching out to see if there’s any possibility of expediting the delivery of the order.
（我联系您是想了解是否有加快订单交货的可能。）
Can you provide a tracking number for the order so we can monitor its delivery progress?
（您能提供订单的跟踪号，以便我们监控交货进度吗？）
（2）跟进样品反馈
I'm calling to follow up on the feedback regarding the samples we sent.
（我打电话来跟进我们寄出的样品的反馈情况。）
Could you please share any comments or suggestions you have regarding the samples?
（您能分享一下对样品的意见或建议吗？）
We are interested in hearing your thoughts on the quality and functionality of the samples.
（我们想听听您对样品质量和功能的看法。）
May I inquire if there are any specific aspects of the samples that require further clarification?
（我可以询问一下，样品是否有需要进一步澄清的具体方面吗？）
I wanted to check in to ensure that the samples met your expectations.
（我想确认一下样品是否达到了您的期望。）
