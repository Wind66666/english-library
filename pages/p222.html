VOCABULARY FOREIGN TRADERS MUST MASTER
一、初次见面与开场
Nice to meet you. I'm glad to have the opportunity to discuss business with you.
很高兴见到你。我很高兴有机会和你讨论业务。
It's a pleasure to meet you. We've been looking forward to this meeting.
很荣幸见到你。我们一直期待着这次会面。
Thank you for coming all the way. I hope your journey was comfortable.
感谢你远道而来。希望你旅途愉快。
I'm [Your Name] from [Your Company]. This is my colleague [Colleague's Name].
我是 [你公司名称] 的 [你的名字]。这是我的同事 [同事名字]。
Let me introduce you to our team. This is [Team Member's Name], who is in charge of [Responsibility].
让我给你介绍一下我们的团队。这是 [团队成员名字]，负责 [具体职责]。
Shall we get started? First, I'd like to briefly introduce our company.
我们可以开始了吗？首先，我想简要介绍一下我们公司。
I'd like to open this negotiation by expressing our sincere interest in cooperating with you.
我想在这次谈判开始时表达我们与贵方合作的诚挚意愿。
We're very excited about the potential business opportunities between our two companies.
我们对两家公司之间潜在的商业机会感到非常兴奋。
Before we dive into the details, is there anything you'd like to clarify or discuss upfront?
在深入讨论细节之前，有没有什么你想先澄清或讨论的？
I believe this meeting will be a great chance for us to better understand each other's needs and expectations.
我相信这次会议将是我们更好地了解彼此需求和期望的绝佳机会。
二、产品介绍与询问
We are very interested in your products. Can you provide more details?
我们对你们的产品很感兴趣。你能提供更多细节吗？
Could you tell us about the features and benefits of your latest product line?
你能给我们讲讲你们最新产品线的特点和优势吗？
How does your product compare to similar ones in the market?
你们的产品与市场上类似产品相比有何优势？
We need samples to test the quality. Can you send them to us as soon as possible?
我们需要样品来测试质量。你能尽快寄给我们吗？
What are the key specifications of this product? For example, size, weight, and material.
这个产品的主要规格是什么？比如尺寸、重量和材质。
Does your product come with any warranties or after - sales services?
你们的产品有保修或售后服务吗？
Are there different models or versions of this product available?
这个产品有不同的型号或版本吗？
Can you explain how your product is environmentally friendly?
你能解释一下你们的产品如何环保吗？
We're interested in the customization options. What can you offer in terms of customizing the product?
我们对定制选项感兴趣。在产品定制方面你们能提供什么？
How often do you update your product features?
你们多久更新一次产品功能？
三、价格谈判
What's your best price for this item?
这个产品你的最优价格是多少？
Your price is a bit high compared to other suppliers. Can you offer a better price?
与其他供应商相比，你的价格有点高。你能提供更优惠的价格吗？
Could you give us a discount? Maybe based on the order quantity?
你能给我们一个折扣吗？或许根据订单数量？
Our budget is limited. Can you adjust the price to fit our budget?
我们的预算有限。你能调整价格以符合我们的预算吗？
Is there any room for price reduction?
价格还有下降的空间吗？
If we place a large order, what kind of price break can we expect?
如果我们下大订单，能得到什么样的价格优惠？
Can you match the price offered by your competitor?
你能和你的竞争对手给出的价格持平吗？
We understand that quality comes at a price, but we hope you can offer a more competitive price without sacrificing quality.
我们知道质量是有代价的，但我们希望你能在不牺牲质量的前提下提供更具竞争力的价格。
What's the lowest price you can go?
你能给出的最低价格是多少？
The price you quoted is not in line with the current market trend. Can you review it?
你报的价格与当前市场趋势不符。你能重新评估一下吗？
四、订单与数量
We're considering placing an order. What's the minimum order quantity?
我们正在考虑下订单。最小起订量是多少？
If we increase the order quantity, can you offer better terms?
如果我们增加订单数量，你能提供更好的条件吗？
How soon can you fulfill a large order?
你们多久能完成一笔大订单？
Are there any restrictions on the order quantity? For example, maximum or minimum.
订单数量有什么限制吗？比如最大或最小订单量。
We'd like to place a trial order first. What's your policy on trial orders?
我们想先下一笔试订单。你们对试订单有什么政策？
Can you handle an order of [Specific Quantity] within [Desired Timeframe]?
你们能在 [期望时间内] 完成 [具体数量] 的订单吗？
If we place a repeat order, can we get some preferential treatment?
如果我们下重复订单，能得到一些优惠待遇吗？
What's the lead time for an order of [Quantity]?
[数量] 的订单交货期是多久？
We may need to adjust the order quantity later. Is that possible?
我们以后可能需要调整订单数量。这可以吗？
How do you ensure the quality consistency when fulfilling large orders?
在完成大订单时，你们如何确保质量的一致性？
五、交货与运输
How long will it take for production?
生产需要多长时间？
Can you meet our delivery deadline? We need the goods by [Date].
你能满足我们的交货期限吗？我们需要在 [日期] 前收到货物。
What are your delivery terms? For example, FOB, CIF.
你们的交货条款是什么？比如离岸价、到岸价。
Which shipping company do you usually work with?
你们通常和哪家航运公司合作？
How will the goods be shipped? By sea, air, or land?
货物将如何运输？海运、空运还是陆运？
Can you provide a shipping schedule?
你能提供运输时间表吗？
What if there is a delay in delivery? How will you compensate us?
如果交货延迟怎么办？你们将如何补偿我们？
Are there any additional charges for shipping to our destination?
运往我们目的地有额外费用吗？
Do you arrange for door - to - door delivery?
你们安排门到门送货吗？
How do you handle the packaging to ensure the goods are well - protected during transportation?
你们如何处理包装以确保货物在运输过程中得到良好保护？
六、付款方式
What are your payment terms?
你们的付款条件是什么？
We prefer payment by letter of credit. Is that acceptable to you?
我们更喜欢用信用证付款。你们能接受吗？
Is there any possibility of extending the payment period? We usually need [Number] days.
有没有可能延长付款期限？我们通常需要 [天数] 天。
Can we pay in installments?
我们可以分期付款吗？
What's your policy on prepayment? How much is required?
你们的预付款政策是什么？需要预付多少？
Do you accept other payment methods, such as PayPal or wire transfer?
你们接受其他付款方式吗，比如贝宝或电汇？
If we pay in advance, can we get a discount?
如果我们提前付款，能得到折扣吗？
How soon do you expect to receive payment after delivery?
交货后你们期望多久收到付款？
Are there any handling fees associated with the payment method?
付款方式有手续费吗？
We need to clarify the payment terms before finalizing the contract.
在敲定合同前，我们需要明确付款条款。
七、质量与检验
The quality of your products is very important to us. How do you ensure high - quality products?
你们产品的质量对我们来说非常重要。你们如何确保产品质量？
What kind of quality control system do you have in place?
你们有什么样的质量控制系统？
Are your products compliant with international quality standards, such as ISO?
你们的产品符合国际质量标准吗，比如 ISO 标准？
We'll conduct our own inspection before accepting the goods. Is that okay?
在接受货物前我们会进行自己的检验。可以吗？
What if our inspection reveals quality issues? How will you handle it?
如果我们的检验发现质量问题，你们将如何处理？
Can you provide quality certificates for your products?
你能提供产品的质量证书吗？
How do you handle product recalls in case of quality problems?
如果出现质量问题，你们如何处理产品召回？
What's your process for handling customer complaints regarding product quality?
你们处理客户关于产品质量投诉的流程是怎样的？
We want to visit your factory to see the production process and quality control measures. Is that possible?
我们想参观你们的工厂，看看生产过程和质量控制措施。可以吗？
Do you offer any quality guarantees or compensations for sub - standard products?
对于不合格产品，你们提供质量保证或补偿吗？
八、合同条款
We need to discuss the terms of the contract in detail.
我们需要详细讨论合同条款。
Before signing the contract, we'd like to review some key clauses, such as the liability clause.
在签署合同前，我们想审查一些关键条款，比如责任条款。
What are the termination conditions of the contract?
合同的终止条件是什么？
Can you explain the force majeure clause in the contract?
你能解释一下合同中的不可抗力条款吗？
We want to add a confidentiality clause to the contract. Is that acceptable?
我们想在合同中添加保密条款。可以接受吗？
How will disputes be resolved according to the contract? Through arbitration or litigation?
根据合同，纠纷将如何解决？通过仲裁还是诉讼？
Are there any hidden costs or obligations in the contract that we should be aware of?
合同中有我们应该注意的隐性成本或义务吗？
We'd like to make some minor changes to the contract. Is that feasible?
我们想对合同做一些小改动。可行吗？
Once the contract is signed, both parties are legally bound. Let's make sure all terms are clear.
合同一旦签署，双方都受法律约束。让我们确保所有条款都清晰明了。
Can you provide a sample contract for us to review first?
你能先给我们提供一份合同样本供我们审查吗？
九、合作意向与展望
We're really interested in establishing long - term cooperation with your company.
我们非常有兴趣与贵公司建立长期合作关系。
This cooperation could be the beginning of a mutually beneficial partnership.
这次合作可能是互利伙伴关系的开始。
What are your expectations for this cooperation?
你们对这次合作有什么期望？
We hope to expand our business with you in the future. What are your plans for product development and market expansion?
我们希望未来与你们拓展业务。你们在产品开发和市场拓展方面有什么计划？
If this cooperation is successful, we may consider increasing our investment or cooperation scope.
如果这次合作成功，我们可能会考虑增加投资或扩大合作范围。
We believe that our combined resources and expertise can bring great opportunities. What do you think?
我们相信我们整合的资源和专业知识能带来巨大机会。你怎么看？
How do you see this cooperation contributing to our respective business growth?
你认为这次合作将如何促进我们各自的业务增长？
We're looking forward to a smooth cooperation process and achieving our common goals.
我们期待合作过程顺利，实现我们的共同目标。
To ensure a successful cooperation, what kind of communication and coordination mechanisms do you suggest?
为确保合作成功，你建议建立什么样的沟通和协调机制？
Let's work together to make this cooperation a win - win situation for both sides.
让我们共同努力，使这次合作成为双方共赢的局面。
十、沟通与协商
I will send you an email with the details later. Please check your inbox.
我稍后会给你发一封带有详细信息的电子邮件。请查收你的收件箱。
Please confirm the receipt of this email.
请确认收到这封电子邮件。
If you have any questions, feel free to contact me at any time.
如果你有任何问题，请随时联系我。
I look forward to hearing from you soon.
我期待着尽快收到你的回复。
Thank you for your prompt reply. It's very helpful.
感谢你的及时回复。这非常有帮助。
We're experiencing some technical difficulties. Please bear with us while we resolve them.
我们正在遇到一些技术困难。在我们解决问题期间，请耐心等待。
The shipment has been delayed due to bad weather. We'll keep you updated on the new delivery time.
由于天气不好，货物运输被延迟了。我们会随时告知你新的交货时间。
I apologize for the inconvenience caused. We'll do our best to fix the problem.
对于造成的不便，我深表歉意。我们将尽最大努力解决问题。
We need to have a further discussion on this issue. When would be a convenient time for you?
我们需要进一步讨论这个问题。你什么时候方便？
Let's try to find a solution that works for both sides. What's your opinion on this?
让我们试着找到一个对双方都有效的解决方案。你对此有什么看法？
十一、让步与妥协
In order to move forward with this deal, we're willing to make some concessions.
为了推进这笔交易，我们愿意做出一些让步。
We can meet you halfway on the price issue if you can increase the order quantity.
如果你能增加订单数量，我们可以在价格问题上各让一步。
We understand your concerns. How about we adjust the payment terms as a compromise?
我们理解你的担忧。作为妥协，我们调整一下付款条件怎么样？
If you agree to our proposed delivery schedule, we can offer a small discount.
如果你同意我们提议的交货时间表，我们可以提供一个小折扣。
We're willing to waive the inspection fee this time as a gesture of our good faith.
这次我们愿意免去检验费，以示我们的诚意。
To show our flexibility, we can consider a longer payment period, but we need some guarantees.
为了显示我们的灵活性，我们可以考虑延长付款期限，但我们需要一些保证。
We can't meet all your demands, but we can make some adjustments to reach a mutually acceptable agreement.
我们无法满足你所有的要求，但我们可以做出一些调整以达成双方都能接受的协议。
If you're willing to make a concession on the packaging requirements, we can offer a better price.
如果你愿意在包装要求上做出让步，我们可以提供更优惠的价格。
Let's find a middle ground. What if we split the difference on the price?
让我们找到一个中间立场。如果我们在价格上平分差价怎么样？
We're open to negotiation. What kind of compromise do you have in mind?
我们乐于协商。你心里有什么样的妥协方案？
十二、拒绝与回应
I'm sorry, but we can't accept your proposed price. It's too low for us to make a profit.
很抱歉，我们不能接受你提议的价格。对我们来说这个价格太低了，无法盈利。
We appreciate your offer, but it doesn't meet our expectations in terms of quality.
我们感谢你的报价，但在质量方面不符合我们的期望。
We can't agree to your request for a 60 - day payment period. Our standard terms are 30 days.
我们不能同意你提出的 60 天付款期限的要求。我们的标准条款是 30 天。
Unfortunately, we're not in a position to provide free samples at this stage.
很遗憾，在现阶段我们无法提供免费样品。
We have to decline your offer of changing the production process. It will increase our costs significantly.
我们不得不拒绝你改变生产流程的提议。这将大幅增加我们的成本。
I understand your point, but our current capacity doesn't allow us to fulfill such a large order in a short time.
我理解你的观点，但我们目前的产能不允许我们在短时间内完成这么大的订单。
We can't meet your demand for a 15We can't meet your demand for a 15% discount. The maximum discount we can offer is 8% considering our costs and profit margin.
我们无法满足你 15% 的折扣要求。考虑到成本和利润率，我们能提供的最大折扣是 8%。
I'm afraid we can't change the production materials as you requested. The current materials are carefully selected to ensure product quality and stability.
恐怕我们不能按你要求更改生产材料。目前的材料是经过精心挑选以确保产品质量和稳定性的。
We regret to say that we can't provide exclusive distribution rights at this stage. Our market strategy requires a more comprehensive evaluation.
很遗憾，现阶段我们无法提供独家经销权。我们的市场策略需要更全面的评估。
Your proposed delivery location is too remote. It will incur extremely high transportation costs, so we can't guarantee the delivery there.
你提议的交货地点太偏远了。这将产生极高的运输成本，所以我们无法保证在那里交货。
We are unable to accept your request for free installation services. Installation is a specialized service, and we have to charge a reasonable fee for it.
我们无法接受你免费安装服务的要求。安装是一项专业服务，我们必须为此收取合理费用。
I'm sorry, but we can't extend the warranty period to 3 years. Our standard warranty is 1 year, which is in line with industry norms.
很抱歉，我们不能将保修期延长到 3 年。我们的标准保修期是 1 年，这符合行业规范。
We can't agree to your suggestion of sharing our production technology. Our technology is our core competitiveness and is protected by strict confidentiality agreements.
我们不能同意你分享我们生产技术的建议。我们的技术是我们的核心竞争力，受到严格保密协议的保护。
Unfortunately, we are not in a position to provide financial support for your marketing activities. Our resources are mainly focused on product development and production.
很遗憾，我们无法为你的营销活动提供资金支持。我们的资源主要集中在产品开发和生产上。
We have to decline your offer to jointly develop a new product at this time. We are currently fully engaged in existing projects and lack the capacity for new initiatives.
我们不得不拒绝你此时共同开发新产品的提议。我们目前全力投入现有项目，没有能力开展新的行动。
I understand your need for a shorter lead time, but due to our current production schedule and order backlog, we can't meet your expectation.
我理解你对更短交货期的需求，但由于我们目前的生产计划和订单积压，无法满足你的期望。
十三、确认与承诺
We confirm that we can meet your order quantity of [Quantity] within the specified time.
我们确认能在规定时间内满足你 [数量] 的订单量。
Please rest assured that we will guarantee the quality of the products according to the agreed standards.
请放心，我们将按照商定的标准保证产品质量。
We promise to ship the goods as soon as they are ready and keep you updated on the shipping progress.
我们承诺货物一准备好就发货，并随时向你通报运输进度。
I confirm that the price we quoted is valid for [Number] days.
我确认我们报的价格在 [天数] 内有效。
We are committed to providing excellent after - sales service. If you encounter any problems, we will respond within [Number] hours.
我们致力于提供优质的售后服务。如果你遇到任何问题，我们将在 [小时数] 内回复。
We confirm that the payment terms are [Payment Terms Details], and we will abide by them.
我们确认付款条款是 [付款条款详情]，我们将遵守这些条款。
Please note that we will arrange for a pre - shipment inspection to ensure the goods meet your requirements.
请注意，我们将安排装运前检验，以确保货物符合你的要求。
We guarantee that all the information provided in the contract is accurate and reliable.
我们保证合同中提供的所有信息准确可靠。
I confirm that we have received your order and are processing it immediately.
我确认已收到你的订单，正在立即处理。
We promise to cooperate closely with you throughout the entire cooperation process to achieve our common goals.
我们承诺在整个合作过程中与你密切合作，以实现我们的共同目标。
十四、市场与竞争
What's the market trend like in your region for this type of product?
这种产品在你们地区的市场趋势如何？
Are there any emerging competitors in your market? How do they impact your business?
你们市场上有新兴竞争对手吗？他们如何影响你们的业务？
How do you plan to expand your market share in the international market?
你们计划如何在国际市场上扩大市场份额？
What are the key factors that influence the demand for this product in the global market?
在全球市场上，影响这种产品需求的关键因素是什么？
Can you analyze the market potential of our product in your area?
你能分析一下我们产品在你们地区的市场潜力吗？
How do you keep up with the changing market demands and customer preferences?
你们如何跟上不断变化的市场需求和客户偏好？
Are there any trade barriers or regulations that we need to be aware of in your market?
在你们市场上有我们需要注意的贸易壁垒或法规吗？
What marketing strategies do you usually adopt to promote your products?
你们通常采用什么营销策略来推广你们的产品？
How do you differentiate your products from those of your competitors?
你们如何使你们的产品与竞争对手的产品区分开来？
In your opinion, what are the biggest challenges and opportunities in the current international market for our industry?
在你看来，当前国际市场对我们行业来说最大的挑战和机遇是什么？
十五、包装与标签
How do you usually package your products? We need to ensure they are suitable for long - distance transportation.
你们通常如何包装产品？我们需要确保包装适合长途运输。
Can you customize the packaging according to our design requirements?
你们能根据我们的设计要求定制包装吗？
What kind of materials do you use for product packaging? Are they environmentally friendly?
你们用什么材料进行产品包装？它们环保吗？
We require specific labels on the products. Can you meet this requirement?
我们要求产品上有特定标签。你们能满足这个要求吗？
Please explain the information that should be included on the product labels according to international standards.
请说明根据国际标准，产品标签上应包含哪些信息。
How do you ensure the durability of the packaging during transportation and storage?
你们如何确保包装在运输和储存过程中的耐用性？
Can you provide samples of the packaging and labels for our reference?
你能提供包装和标签的样品供我们参考吗？
We want to add our company logo to the packaging. Is that possible?
我们想在包装上添加我们公司的标志。可以吗？
What are the costs associated with customizing the packaging and labels?
定制包装和标签的相关成本是多少？
Do you have any experience in packaging products for different target markets? For example, different cultures may have different preferences.
你们有为不同目标市场包装产品的经验吗？例如，不同文化可能有不同偏好。
十六、知识产权与认证
Do your products have any intellectual property rights issues? We need to ensure that we are not involved in any legal disputes.
你们的产品有知识产权问题吗？我们需要确保不卷入任何法律纠纷。
Can you provide relevant intellectual property certificates, such as patents or trademarks?
你能提供相关的知识产权证书，如专利或商标吗？
