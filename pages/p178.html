Just hanging out with friends.（就跟朋友聚聚。）

Sure, why not?（好啊，没问题。）

How about that new café?（那家新开的咖啡店怎么样？）
Sounds good to me.（我觉得不错。）
Let's head there now.（我们现在就过去吧。）
Do you need a ride?（要搭车吗？）
Nah, I'll walk.（不用了，我走就行。）

I'm craving some pizza.（我想吃披萨。）

Nah, let's pick it up.（不用，我们去拿吧。）
Cool, I'll drive.（好，我开车。）

Yeah, why not!（要，当然要！）

Yeah, it's always busy.（是啊，总是很忙。）

It starts at 8 pm.（晚上 8 点开始。）

I'll finish it by 5 pm.（下午 5 点前我会完成。）

Sure, I'll do it after lunch.（当然，午饭后我来做。）

Believe in yourself—if you don’t, how can you expect others to?（相信自己，连你自己都不相信自己，又怎么指望别人相信你呢？）
Don’t give up now—you’ve come so far, and quitting would be a shame.（别现在放弃，你都坚持这么久了，放弃就太可惜了。）
Try your best with this—even if it doesn’t work out, you’ll know you gave it your all.（尽力去做吧，就算没成，你也知道自己已经全力以赴了。）
It’s definitely worth a try—you never know what might happen until you do.（这绝对值得一试，不试怎么知道会有什么结果呢。）
There’s no harm in trying it out—at worst, we’ll just go back to the original plan.（试试也无妨，大不了我们就回到原来的计划。）
What’s the worst that could happen if we try? I don’t think it’ll be that bad.（就算我们试了，最坏能怎么样呢？我觉得也不会太糟。）
I have a really good feeling about this project—I think it’s going to be a big success.（我对这个项目有种很好的预感，我觉得它会非常成功。）
I have a bad feeling about this decision—I think it might lead to problems later.（我对这个决定有种不好的预感，我觉得之后可能会出问题。）
It’s totally up to you to decide—I’ll support whatever choice you make.（这事完全听你的，你做什么决定我都支持。）
You can be the one to decide—I trust your judgment on this.（你来决定吧，我相信你的判断。）
It’s your choice in the end, so go with whatever feels right for you.（最终还是你的选择，选你觉得对的就好。）
I don’t really care one way or the other—it makes no difference to me.（我其实不在乎是这样还是那样，对我来说都一样。）
It doesn’t really matter in the long run—let’s not waste time arguing about it.（从长远来看这其实没什么大不了的，别浪费时间争论了。）
Who even cares about something like that? It’s not worth getting upset over.（谁会在乎这种事啊，不值得为它生气。）
So what if it happened? It’s not like it changes anything important.（那又怎么样呢？又不会改变什么重要的事。）
I’m really sorry I’m late—the traffic was so bad that I couldn’t get here any sooner.（对不起我迟到了，路上太堵了，实在没办法早点到。）
It’s totally okay—don’t worry about it, I just got here a minute ago.（没关系，别担心，我也是一分钟前才到的。）
Thank you so much for your help with this—I couldn’t have done it without you.（非常感谢你帮我做这个，没有你我肯定完成不了。）
It’s my pleasure to help—just let me know if you need anything else.（能帮上忙是我的荣幸，有其他需要尽管告诉我。）
I really appreciate all the effort you put into this—it means a lot to me.（我非常感激你在这上面花的所有心血，这对我来说意义重大。）
You’re being so kind to go out of your way to help me like this.（你特意这么帮我，真是太好了。）
He’s such a nice person—he’s always willing to lend a hand when someone needs help.（他真是个好人，谁需要帮忙他总是愿意伸出援手。）
She’s not a good person at all—she’s always trying to take advantage of others.（她根本不是个好人，总想着占别人便宜。）
They’re really friendly people—they welcomed us warmly when we first moved here.（他们人很友好，我们刚搬来的时候就热情地欢迎了我们。）
He’s so rude that he never says “please” or “thank you” to anyone.（他太粗鲁了，对任何人都从来不说 “请” 或 “谢谢”，甚至经常打断别人说话。）
She is really intelligent; no matter how complex the problem is, she can always find a solution quickly.（她真的很聪明，不管多复杂的问题，她总能快速找到解决办法。）
He is really not that smart; even if things are explained clearly, he often misunderstands the meaning.（他实在不太聪明，就算把事情解释得很清楚，他也常常理解错意思。）
They are extremely hardworking people; in order to achieve their goals, they often work overtime until late at night without any complaints.（他们是非常勤奋的人，为了完成目标，经常加班到深夜也毫无怨言。）
He is so lazy; even though there are many things to do, he always procrastinates and is unwilling to start.（他太懒惰了，明明有很多事情要做，却总是拖延着不愿意动手。）
She is not only beautiful but also has an excellent temperament; she attracts attention wherever she goes.（她不仅长得漂亮，气质也特别好，走到哪里都很引人注目。）
He is not only handsome but also very courteous; he is always considerate when getting along with others.（他不仅英俊，还特别有风度，和别人相处时总是很体贴周到。）
