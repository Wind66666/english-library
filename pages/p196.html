商务英语口语交流对话 一、商务基础沟通 添加联系方式 A: May I have your WeChat? （我能加你微信吗？） B: Sure. Let me scan you. （当然，我来扫你。） A: Here's my QR code. （这是我的二维码。） B: Okay. Please approve it. （好的，请通过。） A: Hi, it was great talking to you about the project. May I have your WeChat so we can keep in touch? （嗨，刚才和你聊项目很愉快。我能加你微信吗？这样我们可以保持联系。） B: Absolutely! That’d be good. Do you want me to scan your QR code or should I show you mine? （当然可以！这样挺好的。你想让我扫你的二维码，还是我给你看我的？） A: Let me show you mine. It’s easier. Here's my QR code. （我给你看我的吧，这样更方便。这是我的二维码。） B: Perfect. Let me scan that... Alright, sent you a friend request. （太好了。我扫一下…… 好了，已经发了好友请求。） A: Got it, let me check my phone. Oh, there it is. I’ll approve it right away. （收到，我看看手机。哦，在这儿呢。我马上通过。） B: Thanks. By the way, do you usually use WeChat for work stuff or just chatting? （谢谢。对了，你微信一般用来处理工作还是单纯聊天呀？） A: Both, actually. I’ll add a note to your contact so I remember who you are. （其实都用。我会在你的联系人信息里加个备注，免得忘了你是谁。） B: Good idea. I’ll do the same. Should I send you a quick message so you have my name saved? （好主意。我也这么做。要不我发你条消息，方便你存下我的名字？） A: Sure, that’d help. I’ll reply once I see it. （当然，这样省事。我看到了就回复你。） B: Great. Look forward to staying in touch! （太好了。期待保持联系！） 初次联系与业务洽谈 A: Good morning, my name is Tom and I'm from ABC Company. I'd like to speak with someone about potential business opportunities. （早上好，我叫汤姆，来自 ABC 公司。我想和贵公司谈一下潜在的商业机会。） B: Good morning, Tom. I'm Sarah, the sales representative for XYZ Company. How can I assist you today? （早上好，汤姆。我是 XYZ 公司的销售代表莎拉。今天有什么我可以帮助您的？） A: Well, I've been looking for a reliable supplier of high-quality office supplies for our company, and I was wondering if your company could meet our needs. （嗯，我们公司正在寻找可靠的高质量办公用品供应商，我想知道贵公司是否能满足我们的需求。） B: Absolutely, we offer a wide range of office supplies, including stationery, paper, and toner. Our products are known for their durability and reliability. （当然可以，我们提供各种各样的办公用品，包括文具、纸张和墨盒。我们的产品以其耐用性和可靠性而闻名。） A: That's great to hear. Could you provide me with some more information about your pricing and delivery options? （听起来很不错。您能给我提供一些有关定价和交货选项的更多信息吗？） B: Of course. We offer competitive pricing and can work with you to develop a customized solution that meets your budget. As for delivery, we have a variety of options to choose from, including next-day delivery and standard shipping. （当然。我们提供有竞争力的价格，并可以与您合作制定满足您预算的定制解决方案。至于交货，我们有多种选择，包括次日交货和标准运输。） A: Sounds good. What about your customer service? Is there someone I can contact if I have any issues or concerns? （听起来不错。那你们的客户服务呢？如果我有任何问题或疑虑，我可以联系谁？） B: We pride ourselves on our excellent customer service. You can reach our support team 24/7 via phone or email, and we'll do our best to address any issues promptly. （我们以优质的客户服务为荣。您可以通过电话或电子邮件 24/7 联系我们的支持团队，我们将尽力及时解决任何问题。） A: Okay, thank you. I'll definitely keep your company in mind. Do you have any samples or brochures that you could send me? （好的，谢谢。我一定会记住贵公司。您能给我寄一些样品或小册子吗？） B: Certainly, I'll be happy to send you some samples and brochures. Just let me know your mailing address and I'll get them out to you as soon as possible. （当然，我很乐意给您寄一些样品和小册子。只要告诉我您的邮寄地址，我会尽快给您寄出去。） A: Thanks again, Sarah. I appreciate your time and assistance. （再次感谢，莎拉。感谢您的时间和帮助。） B: You're welcome, Tom. It was a pleasure speaking with you. If you have any other questions or needs in the future, don't hesitate to contact me. （不客气，汤姆。很高兴和您交谈。如果您将来有任何其他问题或需求，请随时与我联系。） 跨文化沟通与合作 A: Our team from Germany will visit next month. Do they need to follow any local customs? （我们德国团队下个月来访，他们需要注意什么当地习俗吗？） B: It’s polite to bring small gifts, like local snacks. Also, meetings usually start on time, so punctuality is important. （带些小礼物比如当地零食是礼貌的，另外会议通常准时开始，守时很重要。） A: Thanks for the tips. Can you help arrange a translator if needed? （谢谢提醒，需要的话能帮忙安排翻译吗？） B: Sure, we have bilingual staff who can assist. （当然，我们有双语员工可以协助。） A: We noticed the business culture in Japan differs—for example, they prefer face-to-face meetings over emails. （我们注意到日本的商业文化不同，比如他们更喜欢面对面会议而非邮件沟通。） B: Yes, building trust is key there. We suggest scheduling monthly video calls and an annual on-site visit. （是的，建立信任是关键，建议安排每月视频会议和每年一次实地拜访。） A: Also, should we adjust the contract language to be more indirect, as per their communication style? （另外，按他们的沟通风格，合同语言是不是要更委婉些？） B: That’s a good idea. We can soften terms like “must” to “we recommend” where appropriate. （好主意，适当把 “必须” 这类词改成 “我们建议” 会更合适。） A: Expanding to the Middle East requires understanding their cultural norms—especially around religious holidays. （拓展中东市场需要了解他们的文化规范，尤其是宗教节日方面。） B: We’ve partnered with a local consultant. For example, during Ramadan, meetings should be scheduled outside fasting hours, and alcohol should never be offered as a gift. （我们和当地顾问合作过，比如斋月期间会议要安排在非斋戒时间，绝对不能送酒精类礼物。） A: How about gender-related protocols in business interactions? （商务互动中性别相关的礼仪呢？） B: In some regions, male and female colleagues may prefer separate meetings. We’ll tailor the itinerary accordingly. （有些地区男女同事可能更倾向分开开会，我们会相应调整行程。） A: Let’s include these details in the team briefing to avoid misunderstandings. （我们把这些细节纳入团队简报，避免误解吧。） 团队协作与沟通优化 A: Our remote team often misses updates because we rely on emails. How can we improve this? （远程团队经常因依赖邮件而错过更新，怎么改进？） B: Let’s use a team chat app like Slack—we can create channels for different projects and pin important info. （用 Slack 这类团队聊天软件吧，为不同项目建频道，重要信息可以置顶。） A: That’s a good idea. Can we also set a daily 15-minute check-in? （好主意，能每天开 15 分钟的简短会议吗？） B: Sure, a video call at 10 AM to share progress and blockers. （可以，上午 10 点视频会议，分享进展和遇到的障碍。） A: Cross-departmental projects are delayed because of miscommunication. What’s the solution? （跨部门项目因沟通不畅而延迟，怎么办？） B: We can assign a project coordinator to centralize updates. A shared dashboard with timelines and responsibilities will also help. （派项目协调员集中处理更新，建共享仪表盘显示时间线和责任分工也有帮助。） A: Let’s also hold biweekly meetings with reps from each department to align goals. （每两周让各部门代表开次会，统一目标。） B: I’ll draft a RACI matrix to clarify who is Responsible, Accountable, Consulted, and Informed for each task. （我会拟一份 RACI 矩阵，明确每项任务的负责人、审批人、咨询人和知情人。） A: The global team has time zone challenges—Asian and European offices rarely overlap. How to ensure collaboration? （全球团队有 timezone 问题，亚洲和欧洲办公室几乎没有重叠工作时间，怎么确保协作？） B: We’ll implement asynchronous workflows: recorded video updates, detailed documentation in Notion, and 24-hour response SLA for urgent queries. （实施异步工作流：录制视频更新、在 Notion 做详细文档、紧急问题 24 小时响应机制。） A: Cultural differences in communication styles also cause friction—some are direct, others indirect. （沟通风格的文化差异也引发摩擦，有的直接，有的委婉。） B: We’ll roll out a cross-cultural training program and create a “communication guide” tailored to each region’s norms. （开展跨文化培训，为每个地区定制 “沟通指南”。） 二、交易与合同 询价与报价 A: I was told that your company has been in the furniture industry for many years. What kinds of products do you generally handle? （有人告诉我，你们公司在家居行业做了好多年了。请问你们公司一般都经营什么样的产品？） B: We mostly handle office furniture. We supply first-class furniture. Here are our catalogues and pattern books. You can see the material is superior and with all the latest styles. Can you give me some idea about what you're looking for? （我们主要经营办公家具，我们供应的家具都是一流的。这是目录和样本，您可以看出材料都是优质的，样式也很新颖。您能告诉我您想买什么样的家具吗？） A: You know, we want to totally furnish our new office building. This is a list of what we need. Could you give me a ballpark figure for everything on this list? （您知道，我们想要彻底装备我们的新办公大楼。这是一张单子，上面列出了我们需要的家具。您能就这张表上的商品给我一个大概的价格吗？） B: The price varies somewhat according to the size of your order. Would you tell us the quantity you want so that we can work out an offer? （根据您的订单大小，我们的价格会多少有点儿变化。您能告诉我您要订的数量吗？这样我们能够做出报价。） A: If the furniture is pleasantly designed and high-quality stuff, we intend to buy several thousand sets. （如果家具式样好，而且质量也好的话，我们打算订几千套。） B: It's our pleasure to do business with you. We'll let you have the offer next Wednesday at the latest. （我们很高兴跟您做这笔生意，我们最晚下周三把我们的报价给您。） A: I hope you will make us your best offer CIF New York. （我希望您能报给我们纽约到岸价的最优惠价格。） 议价 A: This is our rock bottom price, Mr. Lee. （李先生，这是我们的最低价格了。） B: If that's the case, there's not much point in further discussion. We might as well call the whole deal off. （如果是这样的话，那就没有什么意义再谈下去了，我们还不如取消这笔生意算了。） A: What I mean is that we'll never be able to come down to your price. The gap is too great. （我的意思是说，我们永远不可能把价格降到你们要求的价格，差距太大了。） B: I think it unwise for either of us to be inflexible. How about meeting each other halfway? （我认为我们都这么强硬很不明智，我们能不能各让一半？） A: What's your proposal? （您的提议是什么？） B: Your unit price is $100 higher than we want. Well, I guess we meet each other halfway. （你们的单价比我们想要的价格高出100美元。我建议各让一步。） A: Do you mean a further reduction of $50 in our price? That's impossible. （您是说让我们再减 50 美元吗？那真的不可能。） B: What would you suggest? （您的意见呢？） A: The best we can do is another $30 off. That's definitely the lowest we can go. （我们最多只能再减 30 美元，这可绝对是最低价了。） B: That still leaves a gap of twenty dollars. Let's meet each other halfway again and split the difference. I think this is a price we can both be satisfied with. （这样还留下 20 美元的差额呢。咱们再各让一半分担差额吧。我认为我们双方都能满意这个价格。） A: OK, we can meet halfway again. （好吧，我们就再各让一半吧。） 合同条款协商 A: We’ve received the draft contract. Can we discuss a few terms? （我们收到了合同草案，能讨论几个条款吗？） B: Of course. Which parts would you like to go over? （当然可以，您想讨论哪些部分？） A: The payment term—we hope to extend it to 30 days after delivery. （付款条款方面，我们希望延长到交货后 30 天。） B: Let me check. That should be acceptable. I’ll update the draft. （我确认一下，这应该可以接受，我会更新草案。） A: Also, please clarify the liability clause for late delivery. （另外，请明确延迟交货的责任条款。） B: Sure, I’ll add details about compensation for delays over 7 days. （好的，我会补充超过 7 天延迟的赔偿细则。） A: After reviewing the contract, we have concerns about the termination clause. （看完合同后，我们对终止条款有疑问。） B: What specifically worries you? （具体是哪些地方呢？） A: The current terms allow termination with only 10 days’ notice, which is too short for us to adjust. （目前条款允许仅提前 10 天通知终止，这对我们来说调整时间太短。） B: How about extending it to 30 days? That gives both sides more flexibility. （延长到 30 天如何？这样双方都有更多灵活性。） A: That works. Also, we’d like to add a clause for force majeure, such as natural disasters. （可以。另外，我们想增加不可抗力条款，比如自然灾害的情况。） B: Agreed. I’ll revise the contract and send it back this afternoon. （同意，我会修改合同并在今天下午发回。） A: The indemnification clause in the contract is too broad. We need to limit our liability to direct damages only. （合同中的赔偿条款范围太广，我们需要将责任限制在直接损失内。） B: We understand, but our legal team requires coverage for indirect losses caused by breach. （我们理解，但法务团队要求涵盖违约导致的间接损失。） A: Let’s compromise—we’ll accept indirect losses up to 10% of the contract value, but exclude consequential damages. （我们折中一下：我们接受不超过合同金额 10% 的间接损失，但不包括结果性损害。） B: I’ll consult our lawyers. Also, regarding intellectual property, we need to clarify that ownership remains with us after delivery. （我会咨询律师。另外，关于知识产权，我们需要明确交货后所有权仍归我们所有。） A: That’s fair, as long as we have perpetual usage rights for the delivered products. （没问题，只要我们对交付的产品有永久使用权即可。） B: I’ll incorporate these changes and send a revised version for final approval. （我会纳入这些修改，发修订版供最终确认。） 订单确认与修改 A: I’d like to confirm the order: 500 units of Model A, delivery by the end of this month. （我想确认订单：500 台 A 型号，本月底交货。） B: Let me check. Yes, that’s correct. We’ll send a formal order confirmation shortly. （我核实一下，没错。我们会尽快发正式订单确认函。） A: Wait, can we change the quantity to 600 units? （等一下，我们能把数量改成 600 台吗？） B: No problem. I’ll update the order and confirm via email. （没问题。我会更新订单并通过邮件确认。） A: We need to modify the order we placed last week. The delivery address has changed. （我们需要修改上周下的订单，交货地址变了。） B: Could you send the new address in writing? Also, does this affect the delivery date? （您能书面发一下新地址吗？另外，这会影响交货日期吗？） A: The new address is in the same city, so no delay is needed. Also, we’d like to add 20 units of Model B. （新地址在同一个城市，不会延迟。另外，我们想加订 20 台 B 型号。） B: Got it. I’ll adjust the order and send a revised invoice. Please confirm receipt. （明白，我会调整订单并发送修订后的发票，请确认收到。） A: Due to market changes, we need to postpone the order delivery by 2 weeks and reduce the quantity by 15%. （由于市场变化，我们需要将订单交货期推迟 2 周，并减少 15% 的数量。） B: This will impact our production schedule—we’ve already started manufacturing. Can we agree on a 10% reduction instead? （这会影响我们的生产计划，我们已经开始生产了。能否改为减少 10%？） A: We can accept 10% if you can offer a 3% discount for the delay. （如果你们能因延迟提供 3% 的折扣，我们可以接受 10% 的减少量。） B: Let’s settle on 10% reduction, 2-week delay, and a 2% discount. We’ll also adjust the payment terms to align with the new delivery. （我们就定在减少 10%、延迟 2 周，以及 2% 的折扣吧。我们还会调整付款条款以配合新的交货期。） A: That’s acceptable. Please send a formal amendment to the order. （可以接受，请发送正式的订单修改函。） 发票与付款 A: We haven’t received the invoice for the last shipment. （我们还没收到上批货物的发票。） B: I’m sorry for the delay. I’ll send it immediately. （抱歉延迟了，我马上发送。） A: How should we make the payment? （我们应该如何付款？） B: You can transfer to our company account. The details are in the invoice. （您可以转账到我们的公司账户，详情在发票里。） A: The invoice amount seems incorrect—there’s a discrepancy of $500. （发票金额似乎不对，有 500 美元的差异。） B: Let me check. Oh, we missed the bulk discount. I’ll send a corrected invoice. （我查一下，哦，我们漏掉了批量折扣，我会发送更正后的发票。） A: Thank you. Also, can we split the payment into two installments? （谢谢。另外，我们能分两期付款吗？） B: We can accept 50% upfront and 50% upon delivery. Let me update the terms. （我们可以接受预付 50%，交货后付 50%，我会更新条款。） A: We noticed the invoice includes a 15% VAT, but our company is tax-exempt in this region. （我们注意到发票包含 15% 的增值税，但我们公司在该地区是免税的。） B: Could you provide your tax exemption certificate? We’ll adjust the invoice accordingly. （您能提供免税证明吗？我们会相应调整发票。） A: Here’s the certificate. Also, we’d like to use a letter of credit for payment to reduce risk. （这是证明。另外，我们想用信用证付款以降低风险。） B: We accept L/C, but it should be irrevocable and confirmed by a bank in our country. （我们接受信用证，但必须是不可撤销的，并由我国银行保兑。） A: Agreed. We’ll instruct our bank to issue it within 3 business days. （同意，我们会指示银行在 3 个工作日内开具。） 常用询价句型 We are very interested in Chinese carpets. I'd like to make an inquiry. （我们对中国的地毯非常感兴趣，我想就这方面进行询价。） Would you please send us your price list or catalogue? （请把你们的价格表或目录寄给我，好吗？） Could you make offers for the items listed in your catalogue? （您能把目录中所有产品的价格报给我们吗？） May I ask the price of this product? （我能问一下这种产品的价格吗？） Can you tell me the unit price of this product? （您能告诉我这个产品的单价是多少呢？） Please quote us prices including insurance and freight to Vancouver. （请报包括到温哥华的保险和运费的价格。） I hope you will make us your best offer CIF New York. （希望您能报给我们纽约到岸价的最优惠价格。） 常用议价句型 You're asking too much. （您开的价也太高了吧。） Our rates are in line with the world market. （我们的价格与国际市场上的是一致的。） You can't consider the price separately from the quality. （您不能只看价格不看质量。） How about reducing the price by 5%? （价格减少 5% 如何？） I propose a reduction of 4%. （我建议降价 4%。） This is the best we can offer; we can't go any lower. （这是我们最优惠的价格，不能再低了。） Let's meet each other halfway. （咱们各让一步吧。） I appreciate your counter-offer, but find it too low to accept. （我还是感谢您的还价，但是我觉得这个价太低了我无法接受。） 三、供应链与物流 货物运输 A: Hi, I just wanted to ask if there are any issues with the shipment? （你好，我想问一下货物的运输有没有出问题？） B: Hey! There's a slight delay because the port is short on workers. （你好，有一点延迟，因为港口那边人手不够。） A: Oh, will there be any loss or damage? （哦，那会不会丢货或者损坏？） B: We've got insurance, so in case anything happens, it's covered. （我们已经买了保险，万一有问题也能赔偿。） A: Alright, that's a relief. （好的，那我就放心了。） B: No worries. We'll keep tracking it to make sure everything arrives safely. （没问题，我们也会继续跟进，确保货物安全送达。） A: Our latest shipment got delayed again. The client’s been following up every day. （最近我们一票货又延误了，客户那边催得很紧。） B: Yes, we got notice that the vessel schedule was pushed back. A 20-minute speech plus Q&A would be perfect. （行业趋势和可持续解决方案，20 分钟演讲加问答环节就很合适。） B: I’ll prepare a presentation. Also, we can sponsor a booth to display eco-friendly products. （我会准备演讲，另外我们可以赞助一个展位展示环保产品。） A: The annual Global Sourcing Summit is coming up. We plan to organize a closed-door roundtable with top suppliers. Would you join? （一年一度的全球采购峰会要来了，我们计划组织顶级供应商闭门圆桌会，你们参加吗？） B: Absolutely. What’s the key agenda? （当然，核心议程是什么？） A: Supply chain resilience and carbon-neutral strategies. We’ll also share our 5-year procurement plan. （供应链韧性和碳中和策略，我们还会分享 5 年采购计划。） B: We’ll bring our sustainability director. Can we present our green manufacturing initiatives? （我们会派可持续发展总监参加，能展示我们的绿色制造方案吗？） A: That fits perfectly. I’ll send the agenda and logistics details by the end of the week. （非常合适，我周末前发议程和物流细节给您。） 合作总结与后续规划 A: The first batch of products has been delivered successfully. Thank you for your cooperation. （第一批产品已顺利交付，感谢合作。） B: It’s our pleasure. We hope the quality meets your expectations. （很荣幸，希望质量符合您的预期。） A: It does. We plan to place another order next month. （符合的，我们计划下个月再下一批订单。） B: Great! We’ll prepare the best price for you. （太好了，我们会为您准备最优惠的价格。） A: Let’s review our cooperation over the past quarter. The delivery was on time, but there were minor quality issues. （我们回顾一下上季度的合作吧，交货很准时，但有一些小的质量问题。） B: We apologize for that. We’ve improved the inspection process. What are your plans for next quarter? （对此我们道歉，我们已经改进了检验流程。您下季度有什么计划？） A: We’ll increase the order quantity by 30% and hope for a 5% discount based on our long-term partnership. （我们会增加 30% 的订单量，希望基于长期合作能有 5% 的折扣。） B: We can offer 4% and provide a dedicated account manager for faster communication. （我们可以提供 4% 的折扣，并安排专属客户经理以加快沟通效率。） A: After evaluating our partnership, we’d like to propose a strategic collaboration—exclusive supply for our new product line. （评估合作后，我们想提出战略合作：为我们的新产品线独家供应。） B: That’s a big commitment. What terms are you considering? （这是很大的承诺，您考虑什么条款？） A: A 3-year contract, 10% annual price adjustment, and joint R&D for product optimization. （3 年合同，每年 10% 的价格调整，以及联合研发优化产品。） B: We need a minimum order volume guarantee and shared costs for R&D. （我们需要最低订单量保证，以及研发成本共担。） A: Let’s draft a memorandum of understanding and discuss details next week. （我们先起草一份谅解备忘录，下周讨论细节吧。） B: Perfect. This could be a win-win for both sides. （太好了，这对双方都是双赢。） 纠纷处理与解决方案 A: The shipment arrived 5 days late, and our customer is upset. How can we resolve this? （货物晚到了 5 天，客户很不满，我们怎么解决？） B: We apologize sincerely. We can offer a 10% discount on the next order as compensation. （我们深表歉意，可以在下订单时提供 10% 的折扣作为补偿。） A: That might calm them down. Please also send a formal apology letter to the customer. （这可能会安抚他们，请再给客户发一封正式道歉信。） B: We’ll do that right away and ensure no delays in future shipments. （我们马上处理，并确保未来交货不再延迟。） A: The product specifications don’t match the sample we approved. This is a breach of contract. （产品规格和我们确认的样品不符，这是违约。） B: We’re investigating—there was a mix-up in production. We can rework the batch within 10 days. （我们在调查，是生产时混料了，我们可以 10 天内返工这批货。） A: Rework will delay our launch. We need a 15% discount and air freight for the corrected order. （返工会延误我们的上市，我们需要 15% 的折扣，并且返工后的订单用空运。） B: We can agree to 12% and cover the air freight cost. Let’s confirm in writing. （我们可以接受 12% 的折扣并承担空运费，我们书面确认吧。） A: The recall of 1,000 units due to safety issues has damaged our brand. We need a comprehensive solution. （因安全问题召回 1000 台产品损害了我们的品牌 ，需要一个全面的解决方案。） B: We take full responsibility. We’ll cover all recall costs, provide a public apology, and implement a third-party safety audit. （我们承担全部责任，会支付所有召回成本、公开道歉，并安排第三方安全审核。） A: We also require a 6-month quality monitoring plan and compensation for lost sales—estimated at $50,000. （我们还需要6个月的质量监控计划，以及销售损失赔偿——约5万美元。） B: We can pay $40,000 and share the monitoring reports monthly. Let’s sign a settlement agreement. （我们可以支付 4 万美元，并每月共享监控报告，我们签个和解协议吧。） 危机公关与应急处理 A: A customer posted a negative review online about our after-sales service. What should we do? （有客户在网上发差评抱怨我们的售后服务，该怎么办？） B: We should respond publicly within 24 hours, apologize, and invite them to contact us privately to resolve the issue. （我们应该 24 小时内公开回复，道歉并邀请他们私下联系解决问题。） A: Good. After resolving it, can we ask them to update the review? （好的，解决后能请他们更新评论吗？） B: Yes, but we should focus on fixing the problem first—satisfaction will lead to a better review. （可以，但首先要专注解决问题，客户满意了自然会改好评。） A: A media report claims our products contain harmful substances. It’s not true, but it’s going viral. （有媒体报道说我们的产品含有害物质，这不是真的，但正在疯传。） B: We need to issue an official statement with test reports from a third party to prove safety. Also, contact the media to correct the misinformation. （我们需要发布官方声明，附第三方检测报告证明安全，同时联系媒体更正错误信息。） A: Should we inform our key clients proactively to prevent panic? （要不要主动通知核心客户以防恐慌？） B: Absolutely. We can send a personalized email with the statement and offer to answer any questions. （必须的，我们可以发个性化邮件附声明，并随时解答疑问。） A: A major supplier’s factory was destroyed in a fire, disrupting our entire supply chain. We need a contingency plan. （主要供应商的工厂因火灾被毁，打乱了我们整个供应链，需要应急方案。） B: We’ve identified two backup suppliers who can start production within a week, though their prices are 15% higher. （我们找到了两家备用供应商，一周内可以开始生产，不过价格高 15%。） A: Approve the backup plan—we can’t afford production halts. Also, let’s activate the business interruption insurance. （批准备用方案，我们不能承受生产停滞，另外启动营业中断险。） B: I’ll negotiate with the backup suppliers for a 10% increase and extend our own delivery deadlines by 3 days with clients, offering a 3% discount for the delay. （我会和备用供应商谈判到 10% 的涨幅，并向客户申请延迟 3 天交货，提供 3% 的延迟折扣。） A: Move quickly—we need to stabilize the chain within 48 hours. （尽快行动，48 小时内稳住供应链。） 数字化转型与工具应用 A: Our team is still using spreadsheets to track orders. It’s inefficient. What tools do you recommend? （我们团队还在用表格跟踪订单，效率太低，你推荐什么工具？） B: Try inventory management software like TradeGecko—it automates updates and sends low-stock alerts. （试试 TradeGecko 这类库存管理软件，能自动更新并发送缺货提醒。） A: Is it easy to learn? Our staff isn’t tech-savvy. （容易学吗？我们员工不太懂技术。） B: Yes, it has a simple interface. I can arrange a 1-hour training session. （是的，界面简单，我可以安排 1 小时的培训。） A: We’re considering adopting a CRM system to manage customer relationships. What features are essential? （我们考虑用 CRM 系统管理客户关系，哪些功能是必需的？） B: Contact management, sales pipeline tracking, and customer interaction history. Integration with email and invoicing tools is also helpful. （联系人管理、销售漏斗跟踪、客户互动历史。能与邮件和 invoicing 工具集成也很有用。） A: Can it generate reports on customer retention rates? （能生成客户留存率报告吗？） B: Absolutely. Let’s demo 2-3 options next week to pick the best fit. （当然，下周我们演示 2-3 个选项，选最适合的。） A: The digital transformation plan includes AI-powered demand forecasting. How will this integrate with our existing ERP system? （数字化转型计划包括 AI 驱动的需求预测，这如何与我们现有的 ERP 系统集成？） B: Our IT team will build an API bridge. The AI model will analyze historical sales, market trends, and even weather data to predict demand with 85% accuracy. （IT 团队会建 API 接口。AI 模型会分析历史销售、市场趋势甚至天气数据，预测准确率达 85%。） A: We need to ensure data security, especially with customer and pricing information. （要确保数据安全，尤其是客户和定价信息。） B: We’ll implement end-to-end encryption and role-based access controls. A phased rollout starting next quarter is proposed. （我们会实施端到端加密和基于角色的访问控制，建议下季度分阶段推出。） 业绩目标与激励机制 A: The sales team’s motivation is low. Maybe we need a new incentive plan. （销售团队积极性低，或许需要新的激励计划。） B: Let’s offer a 5% bonus for hitting monthly targets, and 10% for exceeding them. （完成月度目标发 5% 奖金，超额完成发 10%。） A: What about non-monetary rewards? （非金钱奖励呢？） B: “Salesperson of the Month” recognition with a certificate and extra day off. （“月度销售之星” 荣誉，发证书和额外一天休假。） A: The quarterly targets seem unachievable—team morale is dropping. How to adjust? （季度目标似乎无法完成，团队士气低落，怎么调整？） B: Let’s revise them based on current performance, then break them into weekly milestones with check-ins. （根据当前表现调整目标，再拆分成每周里程碑，定期检查。） A: We should also link incentives to effort, not just results—like for client follow-ups. （激励不仅看结果，也看努力，比如客户跟进。） B: A points system where activities earn points redeemable for rewards could work. （积分制可行，完成活动得积分，可兑换奖励。） A: The annual OKRs (Objectives and Key Results) need to align with the company’s 3-year vision. How to cascade them effectively? （年度 OKR 需要与公司 3 年愿景一致，怎么有效分解？） B: Leadership sets company-level OKRs, then departments draft theirs with 60% alignment. Each team member’s OKRs will have 2-3 key results tied to department goals. （领导层定公司级 OKR，各部门拟定 60% 与之对齐的部门 OKR。每个成员的 OKR 有 2-3 个关键结果与部门目标挂钩。） A: We need to balance stretch goals with realism to avoid burnout. （要平衡挑战性目标和现实性，避免过度劳累。） B: We’ll include a “confidence level” rating (1-10) for each OKR and adjust based on mid-quarter reviews. （每个 OKR 设 1-10 分的 “信心值”，季度中期评审时调整。） 可持续发展与社会责任 A: Customers are asking about our environmental efforts. What can we start with? （客户问我们的环保举措，从什么开始做？） B: Let’s switch to recyclable packaging and reduce single-use plastics in the office. （改用可回收包装，减少办公室一次性塑料用品。） A: Can we also mention this on our website? （能在网站上提这个吗？） B: Yes, a “Sustainability” page with our goals and progress updates. （可以，设 “可持续发展” 页面，写目标和进展。） A: We want to join the local community initiative.
