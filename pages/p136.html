外企沟通必备
540个职场高频词汇，速存！
Essential for foreign company communication 540 high-frequency words in the workplace
一、核心基础
ASAP (As Soon As Possible)：尽快，用于强调任务紧迫性，
例：“Please send me the report ASAP.”
FYI (For Your Information)：供您参考，分享无需对方行动的信息，
例：“FYI, the new company policy will be effective next month.”
BRB (Be Right Back)：马上回来，即时通讯中临时离开告知，
例：“BRB, I need to grab a coffee.”
Feedback：反馈，收集对工作、方案的意见，
例：“We need your feedback on the new marketing strategy.”
Follow-up：跟进，追踪已安排事项的后续进展，
例：“I will do a follow-up on the customer complaints.”
Touch base：简短交流，快速沟通情况、确认进度，
例：“Let's touch base later to discuss the project status.”
Reach out：联系，主动与他人建立沟通，
例：“You can reach out to the IT department for technical issues.”
Deadline：截止日期，任务必须完成的时间点，
例：“The project deadline is next Friday.”
Extension：延期，申请延长截止日期，
例：“Can we request an extension for the report submission?”
Prioritize：优先处理，按重要性排序任务，
例：“We need to prioritize tasks by urgency.”
Deliverable：交付物，项目需提交的成果，
例：“The final report is a key deliverable of this project.”
Stakeholder：利益相关者，与项目相关的个人或组织，
例：“We need to communicate with all stakeholders regularly.”
Accountability：责任，对工作结果承担的义务，
例：“Each team member should take accountability for their tasks.”
Compliance：合规性，符合法律法规与行业规范，
例：“The company ensures full compliance with relevant regulations.”
Milestone：里程碑，项目关键阶段或成果节点，
例：“Completing the prototype is a major milestone.”
Scope：范围，项目涵盖的工作边界与内容，
例：“Define the project scope clearly at the beginning.”
Timeline：时间线，各阶段任务的时间安排，
例：“The timeline requires finishing testing by month-end.”
Budget：预算，项目或工作的资金与资源计划，
例：“We need to stay within the project budget.”
Resource：资源，完成工作所需的人力、物力等，
例：“Allocate resources 合理 to avoid waste.”
Risk：风险，可能影响目标达成的潜在问题，
例：“Identify potential risks before starting the project.”
Progress：进展，工作推进的程度，
例：“We made steady progress on the client's project.”
Update：更新，同步最新信息或进展，
例：“Please provide a progress update by noon.”
Task：任务，需单独完成的具体工作，
例：“Assign each team member a clear task.”
Goal：目标，期望达成的结果，
例：“Set specific and measurable goals for the quarter.”
Objective：目标，更具体的阶段性目标，
例：“Our main objective is to increase sales by 20%.”
Priority：优先级，任务的重要紧急程度，
例：“This task is high priority - finish it first.”
Efficiency：效率，单位时间内的工作产出，
例：“Optimize processes to improve work efficiency.”
Productivity：生产力，整体工作产出的价值，
例：“Time management tools boost team productivity.”
Collaboration：协作，团队成员共同完成工作，
例：“Effective collaboration drives project success.”
Communication：沟通，信息的传递与交流，
例：“Open communication avoids misunderstandings.”
二、场景化沟通
（1）邮件沟通
Subject line：主题行，简洁概括邮件核心内容，
例：“Subject line: Project Update - Q2 Results.”
CC (Carbon copy)：抄送，发送邮件副本供相关人员知晓，
例：“Please CC the marketing manager on this email.”
BCC (Blind Carbon copy)：密送，收件人信息不向其他收件人公开，
例：“I'll BCC all clients on this promotional email.”
Attachments：附件，邮件中附带的文件资料，
例：“Please find relevant documents in the attachments.”
Forward：转发，将收到的邮件转给其他需要知晓的人，
例：“I'm forwarding the client's email to you for review.”
Reply all：回复所有人，回应邮件的所有收件人和抄送对象，
例：“When replying all, address everyone's concerns clearly.”
Out of office：外出自动回复，休假或离岗时的自动告知，
例：“I've set an out of office message for my vacation.”
Signature：签名，邮件末尾的个人身份信息，
例：“Add your contact details in the email signature.”
Thread：邮件线索，相关邮件的往来记录，
例：“Keep the email thread organized for future reference.”
Confidential：机密的，标注邮件内容需保密，
例：“This email contains confidential information - handle with care.”
Draft：草稿，未发送的邮件初稿，
例：“Save the email as a draft and review it later.”
Send：发送，将邮件递交给收件人，
例：“Double-check the details before hitting send.”
Reply：回复，单独回应发件人，
例：“Reply to the client's inquiry within 24 hours.”
Follow-up email：跟进邮件，针对之前沟通的后续邮件，
例：“Send a follow-up email if you don't get a response.”
Reminder email：提醒邮件，提醒对方相关事项，
例：“Send a gentle reminder email about the deadline.”
Introduction email：介绍邮件，为他人建立联系的邮件，
例：“Write an introduction email for the new team member.”
Inquiry email：咨询邮件，向他人询问信息的邮件，
例：“Send an inquiry email about the product details.”
Confirmation email：确认邮件，确认安排或信息的邮件，
例：“Send a confirmation email after booking the meeting room.”
Apology email：道歉邮件，为失误或延误表达歉意，
例：“Draft an apology email for the late delivery.”
Thank-you email：感谢邮件，向他人表达谢意，
例：“Send a thank-you email to the client for their support.”
Proposal email：提案邮件，发送方案或提议的邮件，
例：“Attach the project proposal in the email.”
Update email：更新邮件，同步最新信息的邮件，
例：“Send a weekly update email to the project team.”
Request email：请求邮件，向他人提出协助请求，
例：“Write a request email for additional resources.”
Notification email：通知邮件，告知重要信息的邮件，
例：“Send a notification email about the policy change.”
Feedback email：反馈邮件，提供或收集意见的邮件，
例：“Reply with a feedback email on the draft report.”
Formal email：正式邮件，用于商务或官方沟通，
例：“Use a formal tone in emails to clients.”
Informal email：非正式邮件，用于同事间日常沟通，
例：“An informal email is acceptable for team updates.”
Subject line optimization：主题行优化，让主题更清晰醒目，
例：“Optimize the subject line to include key details.”
Email etiquette：邮件礼仪，发送邮件需遵循的规范，
例：“Follow email etiquette to maintain professionalism.”
Email attachment size：邮件附件大小，避免附件过大影响发送，
例：“Compress large files before adding them as attachments.”
Read receipt：已读回执，确认收件人已查看邮件，
例：“Request a read receipt for important emails.”
Delay delivery：延迟发送，设置邮件在特定时间发送，
例：“Use delay delivery for emails sent outside working hours.”
Junk mail：垃圾邮件，无关或恶意的邮件，
例：“Check the junk mail folder for missing emails.”
Spam：垃圾邮件，批量发送的无关邮件，
例：“Mark unwanted emails as spam.”
Inbox：收件箱，接收邮件的文件夹，
例：“Organize your inbox with folders and labels.”
Archive：归档，将无用但需保留的邮件存档，
例：“Archive old emails to keep the inbox clean.”
Delete：删除，移除不需要的邮件，
例：“Delete spam and irrelevant emails regularly.”
Flag：标记，为重要邮件做标记提醒，
例：“Flag the client's email as high priority.”
Draft folder：草稿箱，存放未发送的邮件草稿，
例：“Retrieve the draft from the draft folder to edit.”
Sent folder：已发送邮件箱，保存已发送的邮件，
例：“Check the sent folder to confirm the email was sent.”
（2）会议沟通
Agenda：议程，会议的内容与顺序安排，
例：“Share the meeting agenda with attendees 24 hours in advance.”
Minutes：会议记录，记录会议核心内容与行动项，
例：“The secretary will take and distribute the meeting minutes.”
Chair：主持，引导会议进程的人或行为，
例：“She will chair tomorrow's project review meeting.”
Adjourn：休会，暂时中断会议，
例：“We'll adjourn the meeting and resume next Monday.”
Motion：动议，会议中提出的正式提议，
例：“A motion was made to approve the budget increase.”
Unanimous：一致同意，所有参会者达成共识，
例：“The decision to expand the team was made unanimously.”
Postpone：推迟，延迟会议的时间，
例：“We postponed the client meeting due to bad weather.”
Quorum：法定人数，召开有效会议所需的最低人数，
例：“We don't have a quorum, so we can't vote today.”
Resolution：决议，会议中正式做出的决定，
例：“The board passed a resolution to invest in new technology.”
Action items：行动项，会议确定的需执行的具体任务，
例：“Assign owners and deadlines to all action items.”
Attendee：参会者，参加会议的人，
例：“Send meeting invites to all required attendees.”
Facilitator：会议引导者，协助会议高效进行的人，
例：“Hire a facilitator for the strategic planning meeting.”
Presenter： presenter，在会议中做展示的人，
例：“The product manager will be the presenter for the demo.”
Discussion：讨论，会议中的交流探讨环节，
例：“Allocate 30 minutes for open discussion.”
Q&A (Question and Answer)：问答环节，解答参会者疑问，
例：“Save 15 minutes at the end for Q&A.”
Recap：总结，会议结束前回顾核心内容，
例：“Let's do a quick recap before adjourning.”
Follow-up meeting：跟进会议，针对之前会议的后续会议，
例：“Schedule a follow-up meeting to track progress.”
Kick-off meeting：启动会议，项目或任务的开篇会议，
例：“The kick-off meeting clarified roles and expectations.”
Weekly meeting：周会，每周定期召开的会议，
例：“Our team has a weekly meeting every Monday morning.”
Monthly meeting：月会，每月定期召开的会议，
例：“The department holds a monthly meeting to review results.”
Annual meeting：年会，每年召开的总结或规划会议，
例：“The company's annual meeting will be held in December.”
Emergency meeting：紧急会议，应对突发情况的临时会议，
例：“Call an emergency meeting to address the crisis.”
Virtual meeting：线上会议，通过网络召开的会议，
例：“Use Zoom for the virtual meeting with remote team members.”
In-person meeting：线下会议，参会者现场聚集的会议，
例：“The client prefers an in-person meeting for the contract discussion.”
Stand-up meeting：站会，简短的站立式会议，
例：“We have a 15-minute stand-up meeting every morning.”
Brainstorming meeting：头脑风暴会议，收集创意的会议，
例：“Organize a brainstorming meeting for new product ideas.”
Decision-making meeting：决策会议，讨论并做出决定的会议，
例：“The decision-making meeting will finalize the supplier.”
Progress meeting：进度会议，汇报工作进展的会议，
例：“The progress meeting tracks how close we are to the deadline.”
Budget meeting：预算会议，讨论资金分配的会议，
例：“The budget meeting will approve next year's department funds.”
Strategy meeting：战略会议，规划长期发展的会议，
例：“The leadership team holds a quarterly strategy meeting.”
Meeting room：会议室，召开会议的场所，
例：“Book the large meeting room for 20 attendees.”
Meeting agenda item：会议议程项，议程中的具体内容，
例：“Add 'budget review' as the third agenda item.”
Meeting duration：会议时长，会议持续的时间，
例：“Keep the meeting duration under one hour.”
Meeting notes：会议笔记，个人记录的会议要点，
例：“Take detailed meeting notes for future reference.”
Meeting reminder：会议提醒，通知参会者会议时间，
例：“Send a meeting reminder one hour before the start.”
Meeting cancellation：会议取消，告知参会者会议取消，
例：“Email everyone about the meeting cancellation.”
Meeting rescheduling：会议改期，调整会议的时间，
例：“Reschedule the meeting to next Wednesday.”
Meeting protocol：会议礼仪，参会者需遵循的规范，
例：“Follow meeting protocol by arriving on time.”
Meeting outcome：会议成果，会议达成的结果，
例：“Document the meeting outcome and share it with the team.”
Meeting follow-up：会议跟进，落实会议决议的行动，
例：“Assign a coordinator for meeting follow-up.”
（3）日常职场沟通
Let's circle back on this.：回头再讨论，暂时搁置后后续深入交流，
例：“We don't have time now - let's circle back on this later.”
I'll touch base with you later.：稍后联系，后续进行简短沟通确认，
例：“I'll touch base with you once I have the updated data.”
That's a good point.：这是个好主意，认可对方的观点或提议，
例：“That's a good point - we should consider it seriously.”
Let's drill down into the details.：深入讨论细节，细化具体执行内容，
例：“Let's drill down into the implementation details.”
I need to escalate this issue.：升级问题，将问题提交更高层级处理，
例：“I need to escalate this to management for a decision.”
Can we take this offline?：私下讨论，避免在公开场合交流敏感内容，
例：“This is sensitive - let's take it offline.”
I'll follow up on this.：跟进此事，后续关注并推动处理，
例：“I'll follow up on the customer's complaint next week.”
Let's think outside the box.：跳出框框思考，鼓励创新思路，
例：“Let's think outside the box for cost-saving solutions.”
We're on the same page.：达成共识，双方对事情的理解一致，
例：“We're on the same page about the next steps.”
I'll keep you in the loop.：及时同步，随时告知进展情况，
例：“I'll keep you in the loop on any updates from the client.”
Could you clarify that?：你能说明一下吗？请求对方解释清楚，
例：“Could you clarify that point about the timeline?”
Let's align on this.：我们就此达成一致，确保双方目标统一，
例：“Let's align on the project scope before starting.”
What's your take on this?：你怎么看？询问对方的观点，
例：“What's your take on the new marketing plan?”
I'm on board with that.：我同意，支持对方的提议，
例：“I'm on board with the decision to launch next month.”
Let's put this on hold.：先搁置这个，暂时不推进，
例：“Let's put this task on hold until we get more information.”
We need to troubleshoot this.：我们需要排查这个问题，找出并解决故障，
例：“The system is down - we need to troubleshoot this.”
Let's wrap this up.：我们结束这个吧，完成当前讨论或工作，
例：“It's getting late - let's wrap this up.”
I appreciate your input.：感谢你的意见，认可对方的贡献，
例：“I appreciate your input on the design.”
Let's collaborate on this.：我们一起合作做这个，共同推进，
例：“Let's collaborate on the client presentation.”
Can you keep me updated?：你能及时告知我进展吗？请求同步信息，
例：“Can you keep me updated on the negotiation?”
I need your support on this.：我需要你在这方面的支持，请求协助，
例：“I need your support on the budget approval.”
Let's prioritize this task.：我们优先处理这个任务，明确优先级，
例：“Let's prioritize this task over others.”
This is a top priority.：这是最高优先级，强调任务的重要性，
例：“This client request is a top priority.”
Let's schedule a call.：我们安排一次通话，约定沟通时间，
例：“Let's schedule a call to discuss the details.”
I'll get back to you.：我会回复你，告知对方将后续反馈，
例：“I'll check and get back to you by tomorrow.”
Let's discuss this further.：我们进一步讨论这个，深入交流，
例：“Let's discuss this further in the team meeting.”
I'm available anytime.：我随时有空，告知对方可随时沟通，
例：“I'm available anytime for a quick chat.”
Let's find a solution.：我们找到一个解决方案，聚焦问题解决，
例：“Let's find a solution to the delay.”
Can you assist me with this?：你能帮我一下吗？请求具体帮助，
例：“Can you assist me with the data analysis?”
Let's stay in touch.：我们保持联系，维持沟通渠道，
例：“After the project, let's stay in touch.”
This needs to be addressed.：这个需要处理，指出需关注的问题，
例：“The quality issue needs to be addressed immediately.”
Let's move forward.：我们继续推进，推动工作进展，
例：“Now that we have approval, let's move forward.”
I have a question about this.：我对此有个问题，提出疑问，
例：“I have a question about the budget allocation.”
Let's clarify the requirements.：我们明确要求，确保理解一致，
例：“Let's clarify the client's requirements first.”
What's the status on this?：这个的进展如何？询问任务状态，
例：“What's the status on the report you're working on?”
Let's set a deadline.：我们设定一个截止日期，明确完成时间，
例：“Let's set a deadline for next Friday.”
I'm happy to help.：我很乐意帮忙，主动提供协助，
例：“I'm happy to help with the presentation.”
Let's avoid delays.：我们避免延误，确保按时推进，
例：“Let's avoid delays by starting early.”
This is time-sensitive.：这是时间敏感的，强调时效性，
例：“This document is time-sensitive - send it right away.”
Let's review this together.：我们一起审核这个，共同检查，
例：“Let's review the contract together.”
三、项目管理
Milestone：里程碑，项目关键阶段的标志性成果，
例：“Completing user testing is another important milestone.”
Scope：范围，明确项目包含与不包含的工作，
例：“Avoid scope creep by sticking to the agreed plan.”
Timeline：时间线，可视化的项目各阶段时间安排，
例：“Update the timeline to reflect the new deadline.”
Budget：预算，规划项目的资金分配，
例：“Track expenses to stay within the approved budget.”
Resource allocation：资源分配，合理调配人力、物力等资源，
例：“Proper resource allocation ensures no team is overloaded.”
Risk assessment：风险评估，识别并分析潜在风险，
例：“Conduct a risk assessment at the start of the project.”
Status update：状态更新，定期汇报项目进展，
例：“Share a weekly status update with stakeholders.”
Kick-off meeting：启动会议，项目启动时的动员与规划会议，
例：“The kick-off meeting introduced the project team.”
Scrum：敏捷开发框架，通过迭代推进项目，
例：“The team uses Scrum to deliver features quickly.”
Kanban：看板，可视化工作流程的工具，
例：“The Kanban board shows which tasks are in progress.”
Agile methodology：敏捷方法，灵活响应需求变化的管理方式，
例：“Agile methodology allows for frequent adjustments.”
Waterfall methodology：瀑布方法，线性顺序推进的项目管理方式，
例：“Waterfall works well for projects with fixed requirements.”
Project backlog：项目待办事项，未完成的任务清单，
例：“Groom the project backlog to prioritize tasks.”
Sprint planning：冲刺计划，确定迭代周期内的任务，
例：“Sprint planning takes place every two weeks.”
Daily stand-up：每日站会，简短同步工作进展的会议，
例：“The daily stand-up lasts no more than 15 minutes.”
Retrospective：回顾会议，总结迭代中的经验教训，
例：“The retrospective helps the team improve processes.”
User story：用户故事，从用户视角描述需求，
例：“Write user stories to focus on user needs.”
Acceptance criteria：验收标准，判断交付物是否合格的条件，
例：“Define clear acceptance criteria for each user story.”
Project plan：项目计划，整体的项目执行方案，
例：“The project plan outlines tasks, timelines, and resources.”
Project manager：项目经理，负责统筹项目的人，
例：“The project manager coordinates all team activities.”
Project team：项目团队，参与项目执行的成员，
例：“The project team consists of developers and designers.”
Stakeholder management：利益相关者管理，维护与相关方的关系，
例：“Effective stakeholder management ensures support.”
Scope management：范围管理，控制项目范围不随意扩大，
例：“Scope management requires formal change requests.”
Time management：时间管理，确保项目按时完成，
例：“Use time management tools to track task duration.”
Cost management：成本管理，控制项目费用，
例：“Cost management includes budget planning and tracking.”
Quality management：质量管理，确保项目成果符合标准，
例：“Implement quality checks throughout the project.”
Risk management：风险管理，识别、评估并应对风险，
例：“Develop a risk management plan to mitigate issues.”
Issue management：问题管理，处理项目中出现的问题，
例：“Log and track issues in the issue management system.”
Change management：变更管理，规范项目变更流程，
例：“Submit a change request for any scope adjustments.”
Dependency：依赖关系，任务之间的关联关系，
例：“Identify dependencies between tasks to avoid delays.”
Task prioritization：任务优先级排序，按重要紧急程度安排任务，
例：“Use the Eisenhower Matrix for task prioritization.”
Project schedule：项目进度计划，详细的时间安排，
例：“The project schedule includes start and end dates for each task.”
Gantt chart：甘特图，可视化项目进度的图表，
例：“Create a Gantt chart to track the project schedule.”
Critical path：关键路径，影响项目总工期的任务序列，
例：“Focus on the critical path to meet the deadline.”
Resource planning：资源规划，提前安排所需资源，
例：“Resource planning ensures resources are available when needed.”
Capacity planning：产能规划，评估团队的工作能力，
例：“Capacity planning avoids overloading team members.”
Project closure：项目收尾，完成项目后的总结与交接，
例：“The project closure includes a final report and handover.”
Post-project review：项目后评审，总结项目经验，
例：“Conduct a post-project review to identify improvements.”
Deliverable acceptance：交付物验收，确认交付物符合要求，
例：“Obtain client sign-off for deliverable acceptance.”
