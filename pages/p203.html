外企沟通口语500句
一、日常沟通与社交
（一）表示准备就绪
I am down for it; be down to do something (准备好做某事 / 想要做某事（表示愿意参与或做好准备，能立刻行动）)
I am so down to see that movie tonight, I've been waiting for it to come out for months! (我今晚超想去看那部电影，等它上映等了好几个月了！)
I'm down—just let me know the time. (可以啊，告诉我时间就行。)
She's always down to try new restaurants in the neighborhood. (她总愿意去试试附近的新餐馆。)
I am up for it. (我准备好了（表示愿意接受挑战或参与活动，随时能开始）)
I'm up for it = 算我一个；算上我 (算我一个；算上我)
I haven't written a business plan in a while, but I'm up for it. Let's give it a try! (我有阵子没写商业计划了，但我可以试试。咱来搞一把！)
I'm up for it—what do you need me to do? (我来！你需要我做啥？)
He said he's up for any outdoor activity, even if it rains. (他说不管啥户外活动他都乐意参加，下雨也不怕。)
Are you up for a late-night coffee? I need to talk about the project. (你愿意深夜喝杯咖啡不？我得聊聊项目的事。)
All set (一切都安排妥当了（口语里表示准备就绪，能开始了）)
We're all set for the launched pop-up shop tonight. The food is prepared, decorations are up, and music is playing. (今晚快闪店的事儿都搞定了。吃的准备好了，装饰弄好了，音乐也放着了。)
Are you all set for your presentation tomorrow? Have you practiced enough? (你明天演讲都准备好了不？练够了没？)
All set—tank's full, snacks are packed. (都弄好了，油加满了，零食也装好了。)
The kids are all set for school—backpacks are on, shoes tied. (孩子们上学的东西都准备好了，书包背上了，鞋带系好了。)
Ready Freddy (准备好了（因为押韵，有点俏皮，常用在轻松场合）)
Ready Freddy! (好嘞，准备好了！)
Yes, I'm ready to go. (嗯，准备好了。)
Ready Freddy! Let's go! (早好啦！走！)
Breakfast is done! Ready Freddy? (早饭好了！都准备好了没？)
Good to go (准备好了（表示万事俱备，能立刻行动）)
These contracts are good to go. (这些合同都没问题了，可以推进了。)
So, we can start the meeting if you're both good to go. (要是你们俩都准备好了，咱就开会吧。)
The equipment is set up and tested. We're good to go for the live performance. (设备装好也测试过了，现场表演可以开始了。)
Yep, good to go—you can send it anytime. (嗯，弄好了，你随时能发。)
We're good to go for the party. (派对可以开始啦。)
Five by five (状态不错，准备就绪（俗语，指一切顺利或准备好了）)
Yeah, everything is five by five. (嗯，一切都好着呢。)
Five by five—we'll finish on time. (一切顺利，我们能按时完成。)
Everything's five by five for the trip. (旅行的事儿都妥了。)
It's five by five now—no more issues. (现在没问题了，不会再出岔子了。)
Ready to roll (准备就绪（表示可以出发或开始某项活动）)
Once you're all set and ready to roll, read on to get started on the examples. (你一准备好，就接着读例子开始吧。)
With the recent launch of our new product, our application is ready to roll. (随着新产品最近发布，我们的应用程序可以上线了。)
Let's go—ready to roll! (走，开始吧！)
36.We're ready to roll—let's play! (我们准备好了，开始演奏！)
（二）自我介绍
37. Hello, everyone. My name is XXX, a seasoned business leader in the world of beauty products. (大家好，我叫 XXX，是美容产品领域的资深业务负责人。)
38. Hello, team. As most of you already know, my name is XXX. I am the senior graphic designer at ArtWorks. (各位同事好，你们大多都认识我，我叫 XXX，是 ArtWorks 的高级平面设计师。)
39. Let me start by saying just a few words about my own background. I'm a PhD student of environmental science. (先简单说说我的背景吧，我是环境科学专业的博士生。)
40. For those of you who don't know me already, my name's Lisa and I'm responsible for customer service here. (还不认识我的人，我叫莉萨，负责这儿的客户服务。)
41. Hi everyone, I'm Mike. I joined the company last month and I work in the marketing department. (大家好，我是迈克，上个月入职的，在市场部工作。)
42. Let me introduce myself: I'm Sarah, and my main job is to manage the sales team in the northern region. (我自我介绍下：我是萨拉，主要负责管理北部地区的销售团队。)
43. Good morning, I'm Tom. I've been with the company for 5 years, and I handle product development now. (早上好，我是汤姆，在公司干了 5 年了，现在负责产品研发。)
44. For those new to the team, I'm Jenny—I oversee all the training programs here. (新加入团队的伙伴，我是珍妮，负责这儿所有的培训项目。)
（三）表达感谢与道歉
45. Thanks a lot for your help with the report. (非常感谢你帮我弄报告。)
46. I really appreciate you staying late to finish this. (很感谢你熬夜完成这个。)
47. Thank you for going out of your way to assist me. (谢谢你特意帮我。)
48. I can't thank you enough—this means a lot. (太感谢了，这对我意义重大。)
49. Thanks for your hard work on this project. (谢谢在这个项目上的辛苦付出。)
50. I'm sorry I was late—the traffic was terrible. (抱歉我迟到了，交通太堵了。)
51. I apologize for the mistake in the email. (为邮件里的错误道歉。)
52. Sorry for the confusion—I should have explained better. (抱歉造成混乱，我本该解释清楚的。)
53. I'm really sorry for the delay—we'll fix it right away. (很抱歉拖延了，我们马上解决。)
54. My apologies for not replying sooner. (抱歉没早点回复。)
（四）社交场合常用语
55. Hi there! Long time no see—how have you been? (嗨！好久不见，最近咋样？)
56. Nice to meet you—I’ve heard a lot about your work. (很高兴认识你，我听过很多关于你工作的事。)
57. How’s the family? I remember you said your kid started school. (家人还好吗？我记得你说你家孩子上学了。)
58. This party is great—have you tried the snacks? They’re delicious. (派对超棒，你尝过点心吗？超好吃。)
59. I’ve been getting into hiking lately—there’s a great trail near my house. (我最近迷上徒步了，我家附近有个超棒的路线。)
60. Do you like cooking? I’ve been experimenting with Italian recipes. (你喜欢做饭吗？我最近在试意大利菜谱。)
61. What do you do in your free time? I’m always looking for new books to read. (你空闲时做啥？我总在找新书看。)
62. It was nice chatting—let’s catch up again soon. (聊得很开心，咱改天再聚。)
63. I have to head out, but thanks for having me! (我得走了，谢谢你邀请我！)
64. Take care—see you at the next meeting. (保重，下次会议见。)
（五）电话沟通常用句型
65. Hello, this is Lisa from Marketing—how can I help you? (你好，我是市场部的莉萨，有什么能帮你？)
66. Hi, speaking. Who’s calling, please? (你好，我就是。请问你是哪位？)
67. Good morning, ABC Company—this is Mike. How may I direct your call? (早上好，ABC 公司，我是迈克。请问要转接到哪里？)
68. I’ll put you through to Sarah—she’s handling that project. (我帮你转给萨拉，她负责那个项目。)
69. I’m afraid she’s in a meeting right now. Can I take a message? (抱歉她正在开会，我能帮你留个言吗？)
70. Would you like to leave your number, and I’ll ask her to call you back? (你可以留下号码吗？我让她回给你。)
71. Thanks for calling—let me know if you need anything else. (谢谢来电，有其他需要随时告诉我。)
72. I’ll send that info to your email right away. Talk to you soon. (我马上把信息发你邮箱，回头聊。)
73. I think that covers everything—have a good day! (该说的都差不多了，祝你今天愉快！)

75. I couldn't agree more. (我完全同意。)
76. You're absolutely right. (你说得太对了。)
77. That's a good point. (说得好。)
78. I think so too. (我也这么觉得。)
79. Exactly! (可不是嘛！)
80. No doubt about it. (毫无疑问。)
81. You've got a point there. (你这话有道理。)
82. I'm not sure I agree with that. (我不确定我同意这个。)
83. I see what you mean, but I think differently. (我懂你的意思，但我想法不一样。)
84. That's not how I see it. (我不这么看。)
85. I'm afraid I have to disagree. (恐怕我不能同意。)
86. Maybe, but have you thought about...? (也许吧，但你有没有想过...？)
87. I get where you're coming from, but I don't think that's right. (我懂你的意思，但我觉得不对。)
二、演讲与演示
（一）介绍主题
88. What I'd like to present to you today is the future of renewable energy. (今天我想给大家讲的是可再生能源的未来。)
89. As you can see on the screen, our topic today is "How to Improve Team Communication". (正如你们在屏幕上看到的，我们今天的主题是 “如何改善团队沟通”。)
90. The subject of my presentation is the impact of social media on young people. (我这次报告的主题是社交媒体对年轻人的影响。)
91. In this talk, we would like to concentrate on the latest trends in digital marketing. (这次演讲中，我们想重点讲数字营销的最新趋势。)
92. In my presentation, I would like to report on the results of our customer survey. (在我的报告里，我想讲讲我们客户调查的结果。)
93. I'm here today to present our new product line and how it can benefit our clients. (我今天来是要介绍我们的新产品线，以及它能给客户带来啥好处。)
94. The focus of my speech is the importance of mental health in the workplace. (我演讲的重点是职场心理健康的重要性。)
95. Today's talk will center on ways to reduce waste in office environments. (今天的演讲会围绕减少办公环境浪费的方法展开。)
96. What I want to talk about today is how to balance work and personal life. (我今天想聊的是如何平衡工作和生活。)
97. As the title suggests, this presentation is about the history of artificial intelligence. (正如标题所示，这次报告是关于人工智能的历史。)
（二）提问开场白
98. Suppose your team misses an important deadline because of unexpected delays. How would you handle it? (假设你的团队因为意外延误错过了重要截止日期，你会咋处理？)
99. Have you ever wondered why some projects succeed while others fail? You have? Well, if I could show you three key factors, would you be interested? (你有没有想过为啥有些项目成功了，有些却失败了？想过？那如果我给你讲三个关键因素，你有兴趣不？)
100. How many of you have ever struggled with time management? Do you think a new app could solve that? (你们中有多少人在时间管理上犯难过？你觉得一个新应用能解决这问题不？)
101. Suppose you have to give a speech to 500 people tomorrow and you haven't prepared yet. How would you get ready? (假设你明天要给 500 人演讲，还没准备，你会咋准备？)
102. Have you ever thought about why some ads are more memorable than others? If I told you it's all about storytelling, would that surprise you? (你有没有想过为啥有些广告更让人记住？如果我说这全靠讲故事，你会惊讶不？)
103. How many of you have tried working from home? Do you think it's more productive than working in the office? (你们中有多少人试过居家办公？你觉得这比在办公室工作效率更高不？)
104. Suppose your company decides to switch to a new software system, and most employees hate it. How would you encourage them to adapt? (假设公司决定换个新软件系统，大多数员工都不喜欢，你会咋鼓励他们适应？)
105. Have you ever wondered why people buy brand-name products even when cheaper ones are similar? Well, if I explained it's about trust, would that make sense? (你有没有想过为啥人们买名牌产品，哪怕便宜的替代品差不多？如果我说这和信任有关，说得通不？)
（三）惊人事实开场白
106. Did you know that the average person spends over 4 hours a day on their phone? (你们知道不，普通人每天花在手机上的时间超过 4 小时？)
107. According to a new study, people who read books for 30 minutes a day are 20% less likely to feel stressed. (根据一项新研究，每天读 30 分钟书的人，压力大的可能性会降低 20%。)
108. Statistics show that 70% of small businesses fail within the first 5 years. (数据显示，70% 的小企业在头 5 年就倒闭了。)
109. I read somewhere the other day that a single tree can produce enough oxygen for 4 people a day. (我前几天在哪儿看到说，一棵树一天能产生够 4 个人用的氧气。)
110. Did you know that honey never goes bad? Archaeologists have found 3000-year-old honey that's still edible. (你们知道不，蜂蜜永远不会坏？考古学家发现过 3000 年前的蜂蜜，还能吃呢。)
111. According to recent research, laughing for 10 minutes can burn as many calories as walking for 15 minutes. (根据最近的研究，笑 10 分钟消耗的卡路里和走 15 分钟差不多。)
112. Statistics show that people who eat breakfast every day are more likely to maintain a healthy weight. (数据显示，每天吃早餐的人更可能保持健康体重。)
113. I read the other day that the number of people working remotely has doubled in the past 5 years. (我前几天看到说，过去 5 年远程工作的人数翻了一倍。)
（四）对问题表示感谢
114. Good point. (说得好。)
115. I'm glad you asked that. (很高兴你问了这个问题。)
116. That's a very good question. (这问题问得好。)
117. That's actually a question I frequently get asked. (这其实是我常被问到的问题。)
118. That's a question a lot of people have asked us recently. (这是最近很多人问我们的问题。)
119. I'm not surprised you're asking that given how complex the topic is. (考虑到这话题挺复杂的，你问这个我不意外。)
120. I think the point you're making is a good one. (我觉得你说的这点很有道理。)
121. That's a question we have discussed at length within our team. (这是我们团队详细讨论过的问题。)
122. Many thanks for your question. You've reminded me to talk about something important. (非常感谢你的问题，你提醒了我要讲个重要的事儿。)
123. That's a really interesting point and not one we had considered. (这观点很有意思，我们之前没考虑过。)
124. Thanks for bringing that up—it's worth diving into. (谢谢你提出来，这值得深入说说。)
125. I appreciate you asking that—it gives me a chance to clarify. (感谢你问这个问题，这让我有机会说清楚。)
（五）告知他人可解答
126. Sorry, that's not my field. But I'm sure Peter from the Sales Department could answer your question. (抱歉，这不是我的领域，但我肯定销售部的彼得能回答你的问题。)
127. I haven't given this subject as much thought as I have other topics. I'm wondering if there's someone else in the room who wants to respond... (我对这个话题的思考没其他话题多，不知道在场有没有人想说说...)
128. I'm not familiar with this use case. Could you please share how your team approaches this topic? (我对这个案例不熟，你能说说你们团队是咋处理这个问题的不？)
129. That is a great question. However, it falls outside the scope of my research and expertise. But I'd love to put you in touch with someone who I think might be able to help answer your question. (这问题很棒，但超出了我的研究和专业范围。不过我很乐意帮你联系个我觉得能回答的人。)
130. Great question. I never actually thought about that before, and I'm not sure. I would like to turn this question on to the audience to find out if maybe someone else here knows the answer. (好问题，我以前从没这么想过，也不确定。我想把这问题抛给观众，看看这儿有没有人知道答案。)
131. I'm afraid I'm not knowledgeable in that area, but I would be happy to direct you to someone who is more qualified to assist you. (恐怕我在这方面不太懂，但我很乐意为你指个更专业的人来帮你。)
132. I'll talk to my team and come back with a better answer. (我会和团队聊聊，然后给你个更准确的答案。)
133. Can I connect you with someone who might be better able to answer your question? (我能帮你联系个更能回答这问题的人不？)
134. This is more of a technical issue—I think the IT team would have a better answer for you. (这更多是技术问题，我觉得 IT 团队能给你更好的答案。)
135. I'm not the best person for this, but Maria in Finance has worked on similar issues—let me introduce you. (我不是回答这个的最佳人选，但财务部的玛丽亚处理过类似问题，我给你介绍下。)
（六）告知自己不知道
136. Sorry, I don't know that off the top of my head but I would be happy to look into it further and get back to you. (抱歉，我一时想不起来，但我很乐意去查查，然后回复你。)
137. That's a thoughtful question, and it deserves a thoughtful response. I'd like to get back to you later after I've had time to consider it. (这问题想得挺深的，值得好好回答。我想等我琢磨琢磨再回复你。)
138. I apologize but I don't have a ready answer for that. Can we come back to it later? (不好意思，我这会儿没现成的答案。咱稍后再聊这个行吗？)
139. That's a good question. To be perfectly honest, I don't have an answer for you. But let me find out and get back to you. Let's connect after the session and I will get your email address. (这问题不错，说实话，我答不上来。但我去查查再告诉你。会后咱联系下，我记一下你的邮箱。)
140. I don't know, but can we schedule a time to discuss this further following the presentation? (我不知道，但咱能不能安排个时间，等演讲结束后再深入聊聊？)
141. Unfortunately, that falls outside the scope of my research/this project, but thanks for your interest. If it's something we decide to tackle in the future, I'll be sure to let you know. (不巧的是，这超出了我的研究 / 这个项目的范围，但谢谢你感兴趣。如果我们以后决定研究这个，我肯定告诉你。)
142. I'm not certain about that—let me check with my supervisor and get back to you by tomorrow. (我不确定，我问问我上司，明天给你回复。)
143. I don't have that information right now, but I can look it up and send it to you by the end of the day. (我现在没这信息，但我可以查查，今天下班前发给你。)

151. Now, let's look at the pie chart on the screen. (现在，咱看看屏幕上的圆饼图。)
152. To illustrate this, let's have a closer look at the flow chart. (为了说明这一点，咱仔细看看这个流程图。)

（九）深入讲解图表
158. First, let me quickly explain the graph: the x-axis is time, and the y-axis is revenue. (首先，我快速解释下这个图表：x 轴是时间，y 轴是收入。)
159. As the graph shows, our profits have steadily increased since January. (如图所示，自 1 月以来，我们的利润稳步增长。)
160. I'd like us to focus our attention on the significance of this figure here—it's our highest monthly sales ever. (我想让大家注意这个数字的意义，这是我们有史以来最高的月销售额。)
161. From Table 1 we can see that most customers prefer our online service. (从表 1 我们能看出，大多数客户更喜欢我们的在线服务。)
162. The chart compares the performance of our product with our main competitors. (这个图表比较了我们的产品和主要竞争对手的表现。)
163. You can see here the development over the past five years—we've expanded into three new markets. (你在这儿能看到过去五年的发展，我们已经拓展到三个新市场了。)
164. This pie chart shows that 60% of our budget goes to research and development. (这个圆饼图显示，我们 60% 的预算用于研发。)
165. The bar graph indicates that sales are highest in the fourth quarter. (长条图显示，第四季度的销售额最高。)
（十）重点强调的信息
166. I'd like to stress the following points: quality, safety, and customer satisfaction. (我想强调以下几点：质量、安全和客户满意度。)

五、描述与表达
（一）描述趋势
448. Sales increased by 20% in the last quarter. (上季度销售额增长了 20%。)
449. Sales shot up by 50% after the new ad campaign. (新广告活动后，销售额猛增了 50%。)
450. Sales grew by 10% compared to last year. (和去年相比，销售额增长了 10%。)
451. Sales rose by 15% in the first half of the year. (上半年销售额上升了 15%。)
452. Sales declined by 5% due to the bad weather. (因为天气不好，销售额下降了 5%。)
453. Sales decreased by 8% in the third quarter. (第三季度销售额下降了 8%。)
454. Sales dropped by 12% when our competitor launched their new product. (竞争对手推出新产品后，我们的销售额下降了 12%。)
455. Sales fell by 3% in July. (7 月销售额下降了 3%。)
456. There was a sudden increase in website visitors after the social media post. (社交媒体帖子发出后，网站访问量突然增加。)
457. There was a sudden decrease in orders during the holiday week. (假期那周订单突然减少。)
458. In 2010, we saw a moderate fall in production. (2010 年，我们的产量略有下降。)
459. This was followed by a gradual decline in customer complaints. (之后，客户投诉逐渐减少。)
460. The increase reached a peak in December. (增长在 12 月达到顶峰。)
461. The number of users levelled off after June. (6 月后，用户数量趋于平稳。)
462. Marketing costs have been holding steady for the past few months. (过去几个月，营销成本一直保持稳定。)
463. Prices increased slightly over the summer. (夏天期间，价格略有上涨。)
464. The number of employees grew slowly in the first year. (第一年员工数量增长缓慢。)
465. Demand for the product rose gradually throughout the year. (全年对该产品的需求逐渐上升。)
466. Our market share increased steadily over the past three years. (过去三年，我们的市场份额稳步增长。)
467. Competition in the industry has grown markedly since 2018. (自 2018 年以来，行业竞争明显加剧。)
468. The popularity of the app dropped dramatically after the scandal. (丑闻后，这款应用的受欢迎程度大幅下降。)
469. Profits fell steeply during the economic crisis. (经济危机期间，利润急剧下降。)

471. The number of customers grew rapidly after we opened the new store. (我们开了新店后，客户数量快速增长。)
472. There was a suddenly rise in interest after the TV interview. (电视采访后，关注度突然上升。)
（二）更多描述趋势的短语

480. The market for this product has stagnated; demand isn't growing. (这种产品的市场停滞了，需求没有增长。)
481. Our profits have stagnated since we lost that major client. (失去那个大客户后，我们的利润就停滞了。)
482. There was a sharp rise in applications after the new policy was announced. (新政策公布后，申请量急剧上升。)
483. We saw a sharp drop in attendance when the event was rescheduled. (活动改期后，出席人数急剧下降。)
