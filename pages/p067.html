一、工厂生产类
Factory Tour（工厂参观）
例句：The clients requested a factory tour to inspect our production standards before signing the contract.（客户在签约前要求进行工厂参观，以考察我们的生产标准。）
Production Line（生产线）
例句：This new production line can assemble 500 smartphones per hour, doubling our previous output.（这条新生产线每小时可组装 500 部智能手机，使我们之前的产出翻倍。）
Assembly（组装）
例句：The assembly of this machinery requires skilled workers to ensure all parts fit perfectly.（该机械的组装需要熟练工人操作，以确保所有零件完美契合。）
Quality Control（质量控制）
例句：Our quality control team tests every batch of products to prevent defective items from reaching customers.（我们的质量控制团队会检测每一批产品，防止不合格品流入客户手中。）
Workshop（车间）
例句：The metal-processing workshop is equipped with fire extinguishers and smoke detectors for safety.（金属加工车间配备了灭火器和烟雾探测器，以保障安全。）
Machinery（机械）
例句：We invested in advanced machinery to replace outdated equipment and improve production efficiency.（我们投资购置了先进机械，替换陈旧设备，提升生产效率。）
Conveyor Belt（输送带）
例句：The conveyor belt transports raw materials from the storage area to the production line automatically.（输送带自动将原材料从储存区输送到生产线。）
Raw Materials（原材料）
例句：The shortage of raw materials has delayed the production of our seasonal products.（原材料短缺导致我们的季节性产品生产延误。）
Finished Goods（成品）
例句：All finished goods are stored in the warehouse with temperature control to maintain quality.（所有成品都存放在恒温仓库中，以保持品质。）
Packaging（包装）
例句：The eco-friendly packaging of our products has received positive feedback from environmentally conscious customers.（我们产品的环保包装得到了环保意识较强客户的积极反馈。）
Storage（储存）
例句：Proper storage of chemicals is essential to avoid safety hazards and quality deterioration.（化学品的正确储存对避免安全隐患和品质变质至关重要。）
Safety Measures（安全措施）
例句：The factory has updated safety measures, including mandatory safety training for all new employees.（工厂已更新安全措施，包括对所有新员工进行强制性安全培训。）
Workforce（劳动力）
例句：The company plans to expand its workforce by hiring 200 new workers next quarter to meet growing demand.（公司计划下季度招聘 200 名新员工，扩大劳动力规模以满足增长的需求。）
Efficiency（效率）
例句：Implementing lean manufacturing techniques has significantly improved the efficiency of our production process.（实施精益生产技术大幅提升了我们生产流程的效率。）
Automation（自动化）
例句：The introduction of automation in the packaging process has reduced labor costs by 30%.（在包装流程中引入自动化，使人工成本降低了 30%。）
Maintenance（维护）
例句：Regular maintenance of the production line is scheduled every month to prevent unexpected breakdowns.（生产线每月安排定期维护，以避免突发故障。）
Inventory（库存）
例句：The warehouse manager checks the inventory weekly to ensure we have enough stock for customer orders.（仓库经理每周检查库存，确保有足够库存满足客户订单。）
Blueprint（蓝图）
例句：The engineers referred to the blueprint to assemble the new machinery correctly.（工程师参考蓝图，正确组装新机械。）
Sustainability（可持续性）
例句：The factory is committed to sustainability by using solar energy and recycling waste materials.（工厂通过使用太阳能和回收废料，致力于实现可持续发展。）
Ergonomics（人体工程学）
例句：The company redesigned workstations based on ergonomics to reduce employees’ back pain and fatigue.（公司根据人体工程学重新设计工作站，以减少员工的背痛和疲劳。）
Capacity（产能）
例句：Our current production capacity is 10,000 units per month, which is insufficient for the new order.（我们目前的产能为每月 10,000 件，无法满足这笔新订单的需求。）
Output（产出）
例句：The output of the factory increased by 15% this year due to improved equipment and processes.（由于设备和流程的改进，工厂今年的产出增长了 15%。）
Yield（产量）
例句：The yield of this crop has increased by 20% since we adopted new farming techniques.（自从采用新的种植技术后，这种作物的产量增长了 20%。）
Process Flow（流程）
例句：The process flow chart clearly shows each step from raw material input to finished product delivery.（流程图表清晰展示了从原材料投入到成品交付的每个步骤。）
Workstation（工作站）
例句：Each workstation is equipped with a computer and specialized tools for efficient work.（每个工作站都配备了电脑和专用工具，以提高工作效率。）
Calibration（校准）
例句：The testing equipment requires monthly calibration to ensure accurate measurement results.（检测设备需要每月校准，以确保测量结果准确。）
Testing（测试）
例句：The product undergoes rigorous testing in extreme temperatures to verify its durability.（该产品在极端温度下接受严格测试，以验证其耐用性。）
Certification（认证）
例句：Our products have obtained international certification, allowing us to sell them in European markets.（我们的产品已获得国际认证，可在欧洲市场销售。）
ISO Standards（国际标准化组织标准）
例句：The company has implemented ISO Standards to standardize its production and management processes.（公司已实施国际标准化组织标准，使生产和管理流程标准化。）
Lean Manufacturing（精益生产）
例句：Lean manufacturing focuses on eliminating waste and optimizing resources to reduce costs.（精益生产注重消除浪费、优化资源，以降低成本。）
Just-In-Time (JIT)（及时生产）
例句：The JIT system helps the company reduce inventory costs by delivering raw materials exactly when needed.（及时生产系统通过在需要时精准交付原材料，帮助公司降低库存成本。）
Supply Chain（供应链）
例句：Disruptions in the global supply chain have caused delays in the delivery of key components.（全球供应链中断导致关键零部件交付延误。）
Logistics（物流）
例句：The company partners with a professional logistics firm to ensure fast and safe delivery of products.（公司与专业物流公司合作，确保产品快速、安全交付。）
Material Handling（物料搬运）
例句：The use of forklifts and automated carts has simplified material handling in the warehouse.（叉车和自动搬运车的使用简化了仓库中的物料搬运工作。）
Robotics（机器人技术）
例句：Robotics is widely used in the automotive industry to assemble car bodies with high precision.（机器人技术在汽车行业广泛应用，用于高精度组装车身。）
Prototype（原型）
例句：The design team created a prototype of the new product to test its functionality and appearance.（设计团队制作了新产品的原型，以测试其功能和外观。）
Artificial Intelligence（AI）（人工智能）
例句：Artificial intelligence is used in quality control to detect defects that are invisible to the human eye.（人工智能用于质量控制，检测人眼无法识别的缺陷。）
Batch Processing（批量处理）
例句：The factory uses batch processing to produce 1,000 identical parts at a time, improving efficiency.（工厂采用批量处理方式，一次生产 1,000 个相同零件，提升效率。）
Data Analysis（数据分析）
例句：Data analysis of production data helps managers identify bottlenecks and optimize the process.（对生产数据的数据分析帮助管理者发现瓶颈并优化流程。）
Quality Assurance（QA）（质量保证）
例句：Quality assurance involves setting quality standards and monitoring the entire production process to meet them.（质量保证包括制定质量标准，并监控整个生产流程以符合标准。）

Product Demonstration（产品演示）

三、经济与金融类
Absolute Advantage（绝对优势）
例句：Country A has an absolute advantage in wheat production because it can grow 100 tons with the same labor and land as Country B uses to grow 50 tons.（A 国在小麦生产上具有绝对优势，因为它用与 B 国相同的劳动力和土地能产出 100 吨小麦，而 B 国仅能产出 50 吨。）
Comparative Advantage（比较优势）
例句：Even though Country X is less efficient than Country Y in both car and textile production, it has a comparative advantage in textiles, so both countries benefit from trading textiles for cars.（尽管 X 国在汽车和纺织品生产上都不如 Y 国高效，但它在纺织品生产上具有比较优势，因此两国通过 “纺织品换汽车” 的贸易实现互利。）
Bargaining Power（议价能力）
例句：Large retailers have strong bargaining power with suppliers, allowing them to negotiate lower wholesale prices for goods.（大型零售商对供应商拥有较强的议价能力，能够协商获得更低的商品批发价。）
Barrier（壁垒）
例句：High import tariffs are a common trade barrier that protects domestic industries from foreign competition.（高额进口关税是常见的贸易壁垒，可保护国内产业免受外国竞争冲击。）
Basic Currency（基础货币 / 被标价货币）
例句：In the currency pair USD/EUR, USD is the basic currency, meaning the exchange rate shows how many euros are needed to buy one US dollar.（在货币对 USD/EUR 中，USD 是基础货币，即汇率表示购买 1 美元需要多少欧元。）
Benchmark（基准回报）
例句：Investors often use the S&P 500 Index as a benchmark to evaluate whether their stock portfolio’s returns are above average.（投资者通常以标普 500 指数为基准，评估其股票组合的回报是否高于平均水平。）

例句：The classification of bond by issuer includes government bonds, corporate bonds, and municipal bonds, each with different risk levels.（按发行主体划分，债券的分类包括政府债券、公司债券和市政债券，各类债券的风险水平不同。）

例句：Many institutional investors prefer corporate debt securities with high credit ratings because they offer stable returns and lower default risk.（许多机构投资者偏好高信用评级的企业债，因为这类债券收益稳定且违约风险较低。）
Government Bond（政府债券）
例句：Government bonds issued by developed countries are often considered low-risk investments, as they are backed by national fiscal strength.（发达国家发行的政府债券通常被视为低风险投资，因为它们有国家财政实力作为支撑。）
Callable（可赎回的）
例句：This callable bond allows the issuer to repurchase it before maturity if interest rates fall, which may affect investors’ expected returns.（这款可赎回债券允许发行方在利率下降时提前赎回，可能会影响投资者的预期收益。）
Noncallable（不可赎回的）
例句：Investors seeking stable long-term returns often choose noncallable bonds, as the issuer cannot redeem them early.（追求稳定长期回报的投资者通常会选择不可赎回债券，因为发行方无法提前赎回。）
Convertible（可转换的）
例句：The convertible bond gives holders the right to convert their bonds into a fixed number of the issuer’s common stocks after a certain period.（这款可转换债券允许持有者在一定期限后，将债券转换为发行方固定数量的普通股。）
Refundable（可偿还的）

Nonrefundable（不可偿还的）
例句：The airline stated that the ticket fee is nonrefundable, but customers can change their travel dates for a small fee.（航空公司表示机票费用不可退还，但客户可支付少量费用更改出行日期。）

例句：Speculative grade bonds (also called junk bonds) offer higher yields but carry higher default risk, appealing to risk-tolerant investors.（投机级债券（又称垃圾债券）收益较高但违约风险也更大，对风险承受能力强的投资者有吸引力。）

例句：Index funds use passive management by tracking a specific market index (e.g., the Dow Jones Industrial Average) with low management fees.（指数基金采用被动管理模式，追踪特定市场指数（如道琼斯工业平均指数），管理费用较低。）

Business Cycles（经济周期）
例句：Business cycles consist of four phases: expansion, peak, contraction, and trough, and they affect employment and economic growth.（经济周期包括扩张、繁荣、衰退和谷底四个阶段，会对就业和经济增长产生影响。）
Peak（经济周期中的）（繁荣）
例句：During the peak of the business cycle, consumer spending is high, unemployment is low, and economic growth reaches its maximum.（在经济周期的繁荣阶段，消费支出旺盛、失业率低，经济增长达到峰值。）
Contraction or Recession（经济周期中的）（衰退）
例句：A contraction (or recession) occurs when economic activity declines for two consecutive quarters, leading to lower production and higher unemployment.（当经济活动连续两个季度下滑，导致生产减少、失业率上升时，就会出现衰退。）
Trough（经济周期中的）（谷底）
例句：The trough of the business cycle is the lowest point of economic activity, after which the economy typically begins to recover.（经济周期的谷底是经济活动的最低点，之后经济通常会开始复苏。）
Expansion（经济周期中的）（扩张）
例句：During the expansion phase, businesses invest in new equipment, hire more workers, and GDP grows steadily.（在扩张阶段，企业投资新设备、雇佣更多员工，国内生产总值（GDP）稳步增长。）
Decline Stage（行业生命周期中的）（衰退期）
例句：The traditional camera industry is in the decline stage, as most consumers now use smartphones for photography.（传统相机行业正处于衰退期，因为大多数消费者现在使用智能手机拍照。）
Embryonic Stage（行业生命周期中的）（胚胎期 / 幼稚期）
例句：The quantum computing industry is still in the embryonic stage, with only a few companies able to develop commercial products.（量子计算行业仍处于胚胎期，仅有少数公司能够开发商用产品。）
Growth Stage（行业生命周期中的）（成长期）
例句：The electric vehicle industry is in the growth stage, with rapidly increasing sales and new companies entering the market.（电动汽车行业正处于成长期，销量快速增长，新企业不断进入市场。）

例句：The airline hedged against rising fuel prices by buying oil futures, ensuring it could control its fuel costs for the next year.（该航空公司通过购买石油期货对冲燃油价格上涨风险，确保未来一年能控制燃油成本。）
Historical Cost（历史成本）
例句：The building is recorded on the balance sheet at its historical cost of $2 million (the price paid when purchased in 2010), not its current market value.（该建筑物在资产负债表中按 200 万美元的历史成本（2010 年购买时的价格）记录，而非当前市场价值。）
Import（进口）
例句：The country imports most of its crude oil, as it has limited domestic oil reserves and cannot meet domestic demand independently.（该国因国内石油储量有限、无法独立满足内需，大部分原油依赖进口。）
Incentive（激励）
例句：The company offers a sales incentive—employees who exceed their quarterly targets receive a bonus of 10% of their salary.（该公司设置了销售激励机制：季度业绩超标的员工可获得薪资 10% 的奖金。）
Incentive Fee（激励费用）
例句：The hedge fund charges a 20% incentive fee on profits above a certain threshold, which rewards the fund manager for outperforming expectations.（该对冲基金对超过特定阈值的利润收取 20% 的激励费用，以此奖励基金经理的超额业绩。）
Fixed Cost（固定成本）
例句：Rent for the factory and salaries of management are fixed costs—they remain the same even if the company produces more or fewer products.（工厂租金和管理人员薪资属于固定成本，即使公司产量增减，这些成本也保持不变。）
Income Elasticities（收入弹性）
例句：Income elasticity of demand for luxury goods is greater than 1—if consumers’ income increases by 10%, their demand for luxury watches may rise by 15%.（奢侈品的需求收入弹性大于 1：若消费者收入增长 10%，其对豪华手表的需求可能增长 15%。）
Fixed Income Security（固定收益证券）
例句：Bonds and treasury bills are common fixed income securities, as they promise regular interest payments and return of principal at maturity.（债券和短期国库券是常见的固定收益证券，它们承诺定期支付利息，并在到期时返还本金。）
Income Statement（损益表）
例句：The income statement shows the company’s revenue, expenses, and net profit for the year, helping investors evaluate its profitability.（损益表呈现了公司年度收入、费用和净利润，帮助投资者评估其盈利能力。）
Indenture（契约合约）
例句：The bond indenture outlines the terms of the bond, including the coupon rate, maturity date, and the issuer’s obligations to bondholders.（债券契约合约明确了债券条款，包括息票率、到期日以及发行方对债券持有人的义务。）
Indices（Stock Indices）（指数；股票指数）
例句：Major stock indices like the NASDAQ Composite and Nikkei 225 reflect the overall performance of specific stock markets.（纳斯达克综合指数、日经 225 指数等主要股票指数，反映了特定股票市场的整体表现。）
Indirect Taxes（间接税）
例句：Sales tax and value-added tax (VAT) are indirect taxes—they are collected by sellers from consumers and then remitted to the government.（销售税和增值税（VAT）属于间接税，由销售方从消费者处收取后再上缴给政府。）
Inflation（通货膨胀）
例句：High inflation erodes the purchasing power of money—if inflation is 5%, a product that costs $100 today will cost $105 next year.（高通货膨胀会削弱货币购买力：若通胀率为 5%，今年售价 100 美元的商品明年将涨到 105 美元。）
Interest Rate（Policy Interest Rate）（利率；政策利率）
例句：Central banks adjust the policy interest rate to control inflation—raising rates to cool the economy and lowering rates to stimulate growth.（央行通过调整政策利率调控通胀：加息以给经济降温，降息以刺激经济增长。）
International Trade（国际贸易）
例句：International trade allows countries to specialize in producing goods they have a comparative advantage in, such as Brazil exporting coffee and Japan exporting electronics.（国际贸易使各国能专注生产具有比较优势的商品，例如巴西出口咖啡、日本出口电子产品。）
Inventory（存货）
例句：The retail store checks its inventory weekly to ensure popular items are in stock and avoid losing customers due to shortages.（这家零售店每周盘点存货，确保畅销商品有货，避免因缺货流失客户。）
Money（Money Markets）（货币；货币市场）
例句：Money markets deal with short-term financial instruments, such as treasury bills and commercial paper, with maturities of less than one year.（货币市场交易短期金融工具，如短期国库券和商业票据，期限通常不超过一年。）
Monopolistic Competition（垄断竞争）
例句：The restaurant industry is an example of monopolistic competition—many restaurants sell similar but differentiated food, and each has some control over its prices.（餐饮业是垄断竞争的典型例子：众多餐厅出售相似但差异化的食品，每家餐厅对自身价格都有一定控制权。）
Net Exports（净出口）
例句：Net exports are calculated as exports minus imports—if a country exports $100 billion and imports $80 billion, its net exports are $20 billion.（净出口 = 出口额 - 进口额：若一国出口 1000 亿美元、进口 800 亿美元，其净出口为 200 亿美元。）
Net Present Value (NPV)（净现值）
例句：When evaluating an investment, a positive NPV means the investment’s future cash inflows are worth more than its initial cost, so it is financially viable.（评估投资时，净现值（NPV）为正表明投资的未来现金流入现值高于初始成本，具有财务可行性。）
Nominal GDP（Real GDP）（名义国内生产总值；实际国内生产总值）
例句：Nominal GDP is calculated using current prices, while real GDP adjusts for inflation to show the true growth of economic output.（名义 GDP 按当前价格计算，而实际 GDP 会剔除通胀影响，反映经济产出的真实增长。）
Nominal Interest Rate（Real Interest Rate）（名义利率；实际利率）
例句：The real interest rate is the nominal interest rate minus inflation—if the nominal rate is 6% and inflation is 2%, the real rate is 4%.（实际利率 = 名义利率 - 通胀率：若名义利率为 6%、通胀率为 2%，实际利率则为 4%。）
Oligopoly（寡头垄断）
例句：The automobile and airline industries are oligopolies—only a few large companies dominate the market, and their pricing decisions affect each other.（汽车和航空业属于寡头垄断市场：少数大型企业主导市场，它们的定价决策相互影响。）
Labor（Land/Leverage）（劳动力；土地；杠杆）
例句：Labor, land, and capital are key production factors—without sufficient labor, even a company with large land holdings may struggle to operate.（劳动力、土地和资本是核心生产要素：若劳动力不足，即便拥有大量土地的公司也可能难以运营。）
例句：Financial leverage allows companies to use borrowed money to expand operations—if a company borrows $1 million to invest and earns a 15% return, it can increase shareholder profits.（财务杠杆使企业能通过借款扩大经营：若公司借款 100 万美元投资并获得 15% 回报，可提升股东收益。）
Financial Leverage（Operating Leverage）（财务杠杆；运营杠杆）
例句：High financial leverage means a company has high debt—while it can boost profits during good times, it increases risk during economic downturns.（高财务杠杆意味着公司负债高：经济繁荣时可放大利润，但经济下行时会增加风险。）
例句：Operating leverage measures how much fixed costs affect profits—manufacturing companies with high fixed costs (e.g., factories) have higher operating leverage.（运营杠杆衡量固定成本对利润的影响程度：拥有高额固定成本（如工厂）的制造企业，运营杠杆更高。）
Measures of Leverage（杠杆度量）
例句：Common measures of leverage include the debt-to-equity ratio (for financial leverage) and the degree of operating leverage (DOL) (for operating leverage).（杠杆度量的常用指标包括资产负债率（衡量财务杠杆）和运营杠杆系数（DOL，衡量运营杠杆）。）
Liability（负债）
例句：The company’s liabilities include accounts payable to suppliers and long-term loans from banks, which are recorded on the balance sheet.（该公司的负债包括应付供应商账款和银行长期贷款，均记录在资产负债表中。）
Liquidity Risk（流动性风险）
例句：A bank faces liquidity risk if it cannot meet sudden customer withdrawals—this is why regulators require banks to hold sufficient reserves.（若银行无法应对客户突发性取款，就面临流动性风险 —— 这也是监管机构要求银行持有充足准备金的原因。）
Listed Companies（上市公司）
例句：Listed companies trade their shares on public stock exchanges, such as the New York Stock Exchange (NYSE), and must disclose financial information to the public.（上市公司的股票在纽约证券交易所（NYSE）等公开证券交易所交易，且需向公众披露财务信息。）
Long Position（Short Position）（多头；空头）
例句：An investor takes a long position by buying a stock, hoping its price will rise to sell later for a profit.（投资者通过买入股票建立多头头寸，期望股价上涨后卖出获利。）
例句：A short position involves selling borrowed stocks, with the goal of buying them back at a lower price to return to the lender and earn a profit.（空头头寸指卖出借入的股票，目标是以更低价格买回股票还给出借方，从中获利。）
Long Call（Short Call/Long Put/Short Put）（买入看涨期权；卖出看涨期权 / 买入看跌期权 / 卖出看跌期权）
例句：A long call option gives the holder the right to buy a stock at a predetermined price (strike price) before expiration—investors use it to profit from expected price increases.（买入看涨期权赋予持有者在到期前以预定价格（行权价）买入股票的权利，投资者用它从预期股价上涨中获利。）
例句：A short put option requires the seller to buy a stock at the strike price if the option is exercised—sellers earn a premium but face the risk of buying the stock at a higher price.（卖出看跌期权要求卖方在期权被行权时以行权价买入股票，卖方获得期权费，但需承担以更高价格买入股票的风险。）
Option Contract（Call Option）（期权合约；看涨期权）
例句：An option contract gives the holder the right (but not the obligation) to buy or sell an asset—call options are for buying, and put options are for selling.（期权合约赋予持有者买入或卖出资产的权利（非义务）：看涨期权用于买入，看跌期权用于卖出。）
Owner's Equity（所有者权益）
例句：Owner’s equity is the residual interest in the company’s assets after deducting liabilities—if assets are $500,000 and liabilities are $300,000, owner’s equity is $200,000.（所有者权益是公司资产扣除负债后的剩余权益：若资产为 50 万美元、负债为 30 万美元，所有者权益则为 20 万美元。）
Payback Period (PBP)（回收期）
例句：The payback period of the new machine is 3 years—meaning the company will recover its initial investment of $300,000 through increased profits in 3 years.（新机器的回收期为 3 年，即公司将在 3 年内通过新增利润收回 30 万美元的初始投资。）
Peer Group（同辈群体；对等群体）
例句：When analyzing a company’s performance, analysts compare it to its peer group—e.g., comparing a tech startup to other similar-sized tech companies.（分析公司业绩时，分析师会将其与对等群体对比，例如将某科技初创公司与其他规模相近的科技企业比较。）
Perfect Competition（完全竞争）
例句：The agricultural market (e.g., wheat) is close to perfect competition—many small producers sell identical products, and no single producer can influence prices.（农产品市场（如小麦）接近完全竞争：众多小生产者出售同质产品，没有任何一个生产者能影响价格。）
Pure Monopoly（垄断）
例句：A pure monopoly exists when only one company provides a good or service with no close substitutes—e.g., a local utility company that is the only provider of electricity.（完全垄断指仅一家公司提供某种商品或服务，且无相近替代品，例如当地唯一的电力供应公司。）
Prepaid Expense（待摊费用 / 预付费用）
例句：Prepaid rent (paid 6 months in advance) is a prepaid expense—it is recorded as an asset and gradually recognized as an expense over the 6 months.（预付租金（提前 6 个月支付）属于待摊费用，先作为资产记录，再在 6 个月内逐步确认为费用。）
Prepayment Risk（提前还款风险）
例句：Mortgage lenders face prepayment risk—if interest rates fall, borrowers may refinance their mortgages to get lower rates, repaying the original loan early.（抵押贷款机构面临提前还款风险：若利率下降，借款人可能为获得更低利率而 refinance（再融资），提前偿还原有贷款。）
Price（Price Elasticities/Pricing Currency）（价格；价格弹性；标价货币）
例句：Price elasticity of demand for essential goods (e.g., water) is low—consumers will still buy them even if prices rise slightly.（生活必需品（如饮用水）的需求价格弹性低：即便价格小幅上涨，消费者仍会购买。）
例句：In international trade, the pricing currency is often the US dollar—most oil contracts are priced in USD, regardless of the buyer’s or seller’s country.（国际贸易中，标价货币通常为美元：大多数石油合约以美元定价，无论买卖双方所在国家。）
Share Repurchase（股票回购）
例句：The company announced a $2 billion share repurchase program, which will reduce the number of outstanding shares and increase earnings per share (EPS).（该公司宣布了 20 亿美元的股票回购计划，这将减少流通股数量，提升每股收益（EPS）。）
Primary Market（Secondary Market）（一级市场；二级市场）
例句：Companies issue new stocks in the primary market—e.g., an IPO (Initial Public Offering) where shares are sold to investors for the first time.（企业在一级市场发行新股票，例如首次公开发行（IPO），股票首次向投资者出售。）
例句：The secondary market is where investors trade existing stocks—most stock exchange transactions (e.g., buying/selling Apple shares) happen in the secondary market.（二级市场是投资者交易已发行股票的场所，大多数证券交易所交易（如买卖苹果公司股票）都在二级市场进行。）
Private Equity（私募股权融资）
例句：Private equity firms invest in private companies (not listed on stock exchanges), often providing capital to help the companies expand or restructure.（私募股权公司投资于非上市私营企业，通常提供资金帮助企业扩张或重组。）
Sunk Cost（沉没成本）
例句：The money spent on developing a failed product is a sunk cost—decisions about future investments should not be influenced by this irrecoverable expense.（用于开发失败产品的资金属于沉没成本，未来投资决策不应受这笔无法收回的费用影响。）
Stock Dividends（股票股利）
例句：The company issued a 5% stock dividend—shareholders received 5 additional shares for every 100 shares they owned, instead of cash.（该公司发放了 5% 的股票股利：股东每持有 100 股，可额外获得 5 股，而非现金。）
Stock Splits（Reverse Stock Splits）（股票分割；反向股票分割）
例句：A 2-for-1 stock split doubles the number of outstanding shares and halves the stock price—e.g., a $100 stock becomes $50 per share, with 2 shares for every 1 held.（1 拆 2 的股票分割使流通股数量翻倍、股价减半，例如 100 美元的股票变为 50 美元 / 股，股东每持有 1 股变为 2 股。）
例句：A reverse stock split (e.g., 1-for-10) reduces the number of shares and increases the price—used by companies to avoid being delisted for low stock prices.（反向股票分割（如 10 并 1）减少股数、提高股价，常用于企业避免因股价过低被退市。）
Stocks（Common Stock/Preferred Stock）（股票；普通股；优先股）
例句：Common stock gives shareholders voting rights and a share of profits (dividends), while preferred stock has priority in dividend payments but usually no voting rights.（普通股赋予股东投票权和利润分红权，而优先股在分红时具有优先权，但通常无投票权。）
Security（Portfolio Management）（证券；组合管理）
例句：Securities include stocks, bonds, and mutual funds—investors build portfolios with different securities to diversify risk.（证券包括股票、债券和共同基金，投资者通过组合不同证券构建投资组合以分散风险。）
例句：Portfolio management involves selecting and managing securities to achieve an investor’s goals, such as long-term growth or stable income.（组合管理包括选择和管理证券，以实现投资者目标，如长期增长或稳定收益。）
Positions（头寸）
例句：The fund manager adjusted the portfolio’s positions by increasing holdings in tech stocks and reducing positions in energy stocks, based on market trends.（基金经理根据市场趋势调整投资组合头寸：增加科技股持仓，减少能源股持仓。）
Risk-free Rate（无风险利率）
例句：The risk-free rate is usually the yield on government bonds (e.g., US Treasury bills), as they are considered to have almost no default risk. Investors use it as a baseline to calculate the expected return of risky assets.（无风险利率通常以政府债券（如美国短期国库券）的收益率为基准，因其几乎无违约风险。投资者将其作为计算风险资产预期收益的基准。）
Supply（供给）
例句：A severe drought reduced the supply of wheat, leading to a 30% increase in wheat prices in the global market.（严重干旱导致小麦供给减少，使全球市场小麦价格上涨 30%。）
Surplus（盈余）
例句：The country’s trade surplus reached $80 billion this year, as its exports of manufactured goods far exceeded imports of raw materials.（该国今年贸易盈余达 800 亿美元，因其制成品出口远超原材料进口。）
Reinvestment Risk（再投资风险）
例句：When interest rates fall, bondholders face reinvestment risk—they may have to reinvest the interest payments they receive at lower rates, reducing their overall returns.（利率下降时，债券持有人面临再投资风险：他们可能不得不以更低的利率将收到的利息进行再投资，导致整体收益降低。）
Reserve Requirements（法定准备金）
例句：The central bank raised reserve requirements from 10% to 12%, forcing commercial banks to hold more funds in reserves and reducing the amount of money they can lend.（央行将法定准备金率从 10% 上调至 12%，迫使商业银行持有更多准备金，减少可放贷资金规模。）
Retained Earnings（留存收益）
例句：The company used $5 million of its retained earnings to fund the development of a new product line, instead of paying higher dividends to shareholders.（该公司动用 500 万美元留存收益为新产品线研发提供资金，而非向股东发放更高股利。）
Revenue（Accrued Revenue）（收入；应计收入）
例句：The company’s annual revenue increased by 15% to $200 million, driven by strong sales in the Asian market.（受亚洲市场强劲销售推动，该公司年度收入增长 15%，达 2 亿美元。）
例句：Accrued revenue refers to income earned but not yet received—for example, a consulting firm records accrued revenue for services provided but not yet invoiced.（应计收入指已赚取但尚未收到的收入，例如咨询公司为已提供但未开票的服务记录应计收入。）
Accounts Payable（应付账款）
例句：The company’s accounts payable totaled $30 million at the end of the quarter, representing money owed to suppliers for raw materials.（截至季度末，该公司应付账款总额达 3000 万美元，为欠供应商的原材料款项。）
Accounts Receivable（应收账款）
例句：To reduce the risk of bad debts, the company reviews customers’ credit scores before extending credit and sets a 60-day limit for accounts receivable.（为降低坏账风险，该公司在提供赊销前会审核客户信用评分，并将应收账款还款期限设定为 60 天。）
Accrual Basis（权责发生制）
例句：Most businesses use the accrual basis of accounting, where revenue is recorded when earned and expenses when incurred, rather than when cash is exchanged.（大多数企业采用权责发生制会计基础，收入在赚取时确认，费用在发生时确认，而非在收付现金时确认。）
Accrued Expense（预提费用）
例句：Accrued expenses include salaries owed to employees for work performed but not yet paid, which are recorded as liabilities on the balance sheet.（预提费用包括应付给员工但尚未支付的薪酬，在资产负债表中列为负债。）
Appreciation（Depreciation）（升值；贬值 / 折旧）
例句：The euro appreciated by 5% against the US dollar this month, making European exports more expensive for American buyers.（本月欧元对美元升值 5%，导致欧洲出口商品对美国买家而言价格更高。）
例句：The company depreciates its factory equipment over 10 years, deducting a portion of the equipment’s cost as an expense each year.（该公司对工厂设备按 10 年计提折旧，每年将设备成本的一部分作为费用扣除。）
Buy-side（买方）
例句：The buy-side includes institutional investors like mutual funds, pension funds, and hedge funds, which purchase securities for investment purposes.（买方包括共同基金、养老基金、对冲基金等机构投资者，它们为投资目的购买证券。）
Monetary Policy（货币政策）

例句：The financial technology company strengthened data security management by encrypting customer financial data, implementing multi-factor authentication for employee accounts, conducting quarterly cybersecurity audits, and training employees on phishing prevention—which prevented all data breaches in two years and maintained customer trust.（金融科技公司通过以下方式加强数据安全管理：加密客户财务数据、为员工账户启用多因素认证、每季度开展网络安全审计、培训员工防范钓鱼攻击，两年内未发生任何数据泄露事件，维护了客户信任。）
Event Management（活动管理）
例句：The marketing agency’s event management team planned a product launch event for a client—securing a venue, coordinating with speakers and sponsors, managing event logistics (e.g., catering, seating), and promoting the event on social media—which attracted 500+ attendees and generated 300+ qualified leads for the client.（营销机构的活动管理团队为客户策划产品发布会：确定场地、协调演讲嘉宾和赞助商、管理活动后勤（如餐饮、座位）、在社交媒体推广活动，吸引 500 多名参与者，为客户获取 300 多个潜在客户。）
Supplier Relationship Management (SRM)（供应商关系管理）
例句：The electronics manufacturer invested in supplier relationship management—holding quarterly meetings with key suppliers to discuss quality improvements and future demand, offering long-term contracts to reliable suppliers, and collaborating on cost-saving initiatives—which reduced raw material lead times by 20% and improved supplier responsiveness.（电子制造商投入资源开展供应商关系管理：与核心供应商每季度召开会议讨论质量提升和未来需求、向可靠供应商提供长期合同、合作推进成本节约计划，使原材料交货周期缩短 20%，供应商响应速度提升。）
Employee Performance Improvement Management（员工绩效改进管理）
例句：The retail store’s HR team implemented employee performance improvement management—identifying underperforming staff through regular evaluations, creating individual improvement plans (e.g., additional sales training), and providing one-on-one coaching—which increased the average sales performance of underperforming employees by 25% within 3 months.（零售店的人力资源团队实施员工绩效改进管理：通过定期评估识别绩效不佳员工、制定个人改进计划（如额外销售培训）、提供一对一辅导，使绩效不佳员工的平均销售业绩在 3 个月内提升 25%。）
Green Management（绿色管理；环保管理）
例句：The hotel chain adopted green management practices—installing energy-saving light bulbs and water-saving faucets, using biodegradable cleaning products, and encouraging guests to reuse towels (with a small discount incentive)—which reduced the hotel’s carbon emissions by 18% and water consumption by 22%.（连锁酒店采用绿色管理举措：安装节能灯泡和节水龙头、使用可生物降解清洁产品、鼓励客人重复使用毛巾（提供小额折扣激励），使酒店碳排放减少 18%，用水量降低 22%。）
Knowledge Sharing Management（知识共享管理）
例句：The consulting firm promoted knowledge sharing management by building an internal wiki where employees could upload project case studies and best practices, organizing monthly knowledge-sharing workshops, and assigning senior employees as mentors to junior staff—which reduced the time new employees needed to become fully productive by 30%.（咨询公司通过以下方式推进知识共享管理：搭建内部维基平台供员工上传项目案例和最佳实践、每月组织知识共享研讨会、安排资深员工担任初级员工导师，使新员工达到完全胜任工作的时间缩短 30%。）
