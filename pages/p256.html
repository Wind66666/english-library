一、项目启动与推进
Initiate 启动
eg：I initiated the project by outlining the primary objectives and establishing a timeline.
我通过概述主要目标和制定时间表，启动了这个项目。
Kick off 启动
eg：Let's kick off the new year with a renewed focus on innovation.
让我们以重新聚焦创新，开启新的一年。
Get the ball rolling 启动
eg：Let's get the ball rolling on this project as soon as possible.
我们尽快启动这个项目吧。
Set in motion 开始实施
eg：The CEO set the new initiative in motion last week.
上周，首席执行官启动了新计划。
Propel 推动
eg：Our latest marketing campaign aims to propel our brand into new markets.
我们最新的营销活动旨在把品牌推向新市场。
Drive forward 推动
eg：We need to drive forward the project to meet the quarterly deadlines.
我们要推进项目，赶上季度截止日期。
Expedite 加速
eg：The manager expedited the approval process to meet the tight deadline.
经理加快了审批流程，赶上了紧迫的截止日期。
Accelerate 加速
eg：We need to accelerate the approval process to meet the client's deadline.
我们需要加速审批流程，以满足客户的截止日期。
Hasten 加快
eg：The urgent request from the client hastened our decision-making process.
客户的紧急需求加快了我们的决策流程。
Ramp up 加速 / 增加
eg：We need to ramp up production to meet the tight deadline.
为了赶上紧迫的截止日期，我们得加快生产。
Step up 加强 / 提升
eg：We need to step up our compliance efforts before the audit.
审计前，我们得加强合规工作。
Escalate 升级
eg：I escalated the priority of the project to ensure its completion ahead of schedule.
我提高了这个项目的优先级，确保能提前完成。
Sustain 维持
eg：I sustained project momentum by continuously engaging stakeholders and addressing concerns.
我通过不断和利益相关方沟通、解决问题，维持了项目的推进势头。
Sustain momentum 保持势头
eg：We need to sustain momentum on last year's successes as we enter the new year.
新年里，我们要延续去年的成功势头。
Drag on 持续
eg：The meeting dragged on for hours without any concrete conclusions.
会议开了好几个小时，也没得出什么具体结论。
二、任务分配与管理
Delegate 委派
eg：I delegated smaller tasks to free up time for high-priority work.
我把小事派给别人做，腾出时间处理优先事项。
Apportion 分摊
eg：I apportioned the available time for each phase of the project to ensure steady progress.
我给项目的每个阶段分配了相应的时间，确保进度顺利。
Amortize 分摊
eg：I amortized the team's workload over several weeks to prevent burnout and ensure steady progress.
我把团队的工作量分摊到几周内，避免大家过度劳累，保证稳步推进。
Distribute 分配
eg：I distributed tasks evenly among team members to ensure balanced workloads.
我把任务平均分配给团队成员，保证工作量均衡。
Disperse 分散
eg：I dispersed the tasks across the week to avoid overwhelming the team on any given day.
我把任务分散到一周内，避免团队某天负担过重。
Partition 划分
eg：I partitioned the project into smaller phases to make it easier to manage.
我把项目分成几个小阶段，方便管理。
Section 划分
eg：I sectioned off the project into distinct tasks to ensure we could focus on one area at a time.
我把项目分成不同的任务，确保我们能一次专注一个领域。
Segregate 分离
eg：I segregated the tasks by priority to ensure the most important ones were completed first.
我按优先级把任务分开，确保最重要的先完成。
Demarcate 划定界限
eg：I demarcated responsibilities clearly to avoid overlap and confusion in the project.
我把责任划分清楚，避免项目中出现重叠和混乱。
Phase out 逐步淘汰
eg：The outdated workflow system will be phased out next quarter.
过时的工作流系统将在下个季度逐步淘汰。
Schedule 安排时间（关联词汇：Pencil in 暂时安排时间）
eg：Let's pencil in a meeting for next Monday morning.
我们暂定下周一早上开会吧。
Timeline 时间线（关联词汇：Map out 规划）
eg：She mapped out her strategy for obtaining a promotion by year-end.
她规划了年底前获得晋升的策略。
Monitor 监督（关联词汇：Oversee 监督）
eg：I oversee project timelines to ensure we stay within deadlines and deliver high-quality results.
我监督项目时间，确保按时交付高质量成果。
Track 跟踪（关联词汇：Keep track of 跟踪）
eg：Project managers must keep track of task deadlines to avoid delays.
项目经理必须跟踪任务截止日期，以避免延误。
Follow up 跟进（关联词汇：Follow up on 跟进）
eg：I'll follow up on the team's progress and ensure all tasks are completed.
我会跟进团队进度，确保所有任务完成。
Catch up on 补上 / 赶上进度
eg：I need to catch up on the project reports before the meeting tomorrow.
明天开会前，我得补上项目报告的进度。
Pace 控制节奏
eg：I paced the project's milestones to ensure sustainable progress without overwhelming the team.
我控制项目里程碑的节奏，确保团队不过载，稳步推进。
三、调整与优化
Adjust（关联词汇：Amend 修改）
eg：I amended the team's schedule to reflect the new project requirements.
我修改了团队的时间表，以适应新的项目要求。
Revise 修改 / 调整
eg：I revised the schedule to accommodate the sudden change in project scope.
我调整了时间表，以适应项目范围的突然变化。
Modify（关联词汇：Modulate 调节）
eg：The manager modulated the team's workload to maintain a sustainable pace.
经理调节了团队的工作量，保持可持续的进度。
Transform（关联词汇：Restructure 重组）
eg：I restructured the team's schedule to better align with project priorities.
我重组了团队的时间表，使其更符合项目优先级。
Revamp 改造
eg：The board decided to revamp the project's framework to increase flexibility.
董事会决定改造项目框架，提高灵活性。
Overhaul 彻底改革
eg：They overhauled the scheduling process to eliminate inefficiencies.
他们彻底改革了排程流程，消除了低效问题。
Optimize 优化
eg：The manager optimized the team's workflow by introducing task automation.
经理通过引入任务自动化，优化了团队的工作流程。
Refine 改进
eg：I refined our employee development process to better match individual career goals with organizational needs.
我优化了员工发展流程，让个人职业目标和公司需求更匹配。
Maximize 最大化
eg：The company maximized its advertising budget for better reach.
公司充分利用广告预算，扩大了覆盖面。
Minimize（关联词汇：Curtail 缩减）
eg：To stay within budget, we'll need to curtail non-essential expenditures.
为了不超预算，我们得缩减非必要开支。
Pare down 精简
eg：We need to pare down unnecessary expenses to maintain profitability.
我们得精简不必要的开支，保持盈利。
Scale back 减少
eg：Due to recent budget cuts, we have to scale back on non-essential activities.
因为最近削减预算，我们得减少非必要活动。
Scale up 扩大规模
eg：Our new year goal is to scale up operations in emerging markets.
我们的新年目标是在新兴市场扩大业务规模。
Augment 增强
eg：The team augmented their capacity by integrating new tools into their workflow.
团队通过在工作流程中加入新工具，提升了工作能力。
Elevate 提升
eg：I elevated the career development program by incorporating leadership training for high-potential employees.
我为潜力员工加入了领导力培训，提升了职业发展计划的质量。
Strengthen（关联词汇：Fortify 强化）
eg：The new measures were implemented to fortify the organization's market position.
新措施的实施是为了巩固公司的市场地位。
Reinforce 加强
eg：We must carry forward the company's legacy by maintaining discipline.
我们必须通过保持纪律，传承公司传统。
四、沟通与协作
Articulate 清晰表达
eg：I articulated the project's strategic objectives to ensure that all stakeholders understood the long-term vision.
我把项目的战略目标说清楚了，确保所有相关方都明白长远规划。
Elaborate 详细说明
eg：Could you elaborate on the risk mitigation strategy for the new project?
你能详细说明新项目的风险缓解策略吗？
Expound 详细阐述
eg：I expounded on the benefits of the new approach to gain stakeholder support.
我详细说明了新方法的好处，争取到了利益相关方的支持。
Illustrate 说明
eg：The chart illustrates the correlation between customer satisfaction and repeat purchases.
图表说明了客户满意度与重复购买之间的关联。
Reiterate 重申
eg：I want to reiterate our commitment to delivering exceptional quality within the agreed timelines.
我想重申，我们承诺在约定时间内交付高质量成果。
Confirm（关联词汇：Authenticate 确认）
eg：I authenticated project outcomes to ensure they met the expected standards and benchmarks.
我确认了项目成果，确保它们达到了预期的标准和基准。
Notify（关联词汇：Update 更新）
eg：Please update the project timeline to reflect the latest changes.
请更新项目时间表以反映最新变化。
Negotiate 谈判
eg：The sales team negotiated favorable terms with the new distributor.
销售团队与新经销商谈判达成了有利条款。
Barter 议价
eg：The procurement team bartered with suppliers to get a 10% discount on raw materials.
采购团队与供应商议价，获得了原材料 10% 的折扣。
Engage with 与…… 互动
eg：It's important to engage with stakeholders regularly to maintain strong relationships.
定期和利益相关方互动很重要，能维持良好关系。
Collaborate（关联词汇：Foster a spirit of collaboration 培养协作精神）
eg：The holiday season is a great opportunity to foster a spirit of collaboration within the team.
假期是培养团队协作精神的好机会。
Liaise（关联词汇：Touch base with 联络）
eg：I'll touch base with the marketing team to ensure we're aligned on the campaign launch.
我会和市场团队联络，确保活动启动时我们步调一致。
Bounce ideas off 与…… 探讨想法
eg：I'd like to bounce some ideas off you for the upcoming campaign.
我想跟你聊聊关于即将开展的活动的一些想法，听听你的意见。
Circle back to 回过头来讨论
eg：Let's circle back to this topic once we have more data.
等我们有更多数据，再回头讨论这个话题。
Bring up 提起
eg：I will bring up the issue of resource allocation in the next team meeting.
下次团队会议上，我会提起资源分配的问题。
Lay out 阐述 / 规划
eg：The CEO laid out the company's vision for the next five years.
首席执行官阐述了公司未来五年的愿景。
Propose（关联词汇：Put forward 提出）
eg：I'll take your suggestions on board during the planning phase.
规划阶段，我会采纳你的建议。
Weigh in on 发表意见 / 参与决策
eg：I'd like you to weigh in on the timeline if you have concerns.
如果你对时间表有意见，来说说吧。
Strengthen team bonds 加强团队联系
eg：Hosting a holiday gathering is a great way to strengthen team bonds.
举办假日聚会是加强团队联系的好方法。
五、资源与执行
Utilize 利用
eg：We utilized our existing data analytics capabilities to identify new market opportunities.
我们利用现有的数据分析能力，发现了新的市场机会。
Harness 利用 / 驾驭
eg：The project successfully harnessed cross-departmental expertise to streamline operations.
这个项目成功利用了跨部门的专业知识，简化了流程。
Leverage 利用
eg：The company leveraged its brand reputation to attract new customers.
公司利用品牌声誉吸引新客户。
Tap into 开拓
eg：The startup tapped into a niche market that was previously underserved.
这家初创公司开拓了一个以前服务不足的小众市场。
Allocate（关联词汇：Deploy 部署）
eg：I deploy resources effectively to ensure we meet our time constraints without compromising quality.
我合理部署资源，确保在规定时间内完成任务，同时不降低质量。
Secure（关联词汇：Obtain 获得）
eg：The team worked hard to obtain the necessary certifications for the product.
团队努力为产品获得了必要的认证。
Garner 获得
eg：The new marketing campaign helped garner a 20% increase in sales.
新的营销活动帮助实现了 20% 的销售额增长。
Execute 执行
eg：I execute project plans with precision to ensure all tasks are completed on schedule.
我精确执行项目计划，保证所有任务按时完成。
Implement（关联词汇：Enact 实施）
eg：I enacted contingency plans to address potential risks and keep the project on track.
我实施了应急计划，应对潜在风险，保证项目按计划进行。
Operationalize 实施化
eg：I operationalized the strategic plans by creating detailed workflows and timelines.
我通过制定详细的工作流程和时间表，把战略计划落到实处。
Complete（关联词汇：Wrap up 完成）
eg：Let's wrap up the meeting with a summary of action items.
让我们以行动项总结结束会议。
六、问题处理与解决
Solve（关联词汇：Rectify 纠正）
eg：We need to rectify discrepancies in the timeline immediately to avoid further delays.
我们得赶紧纠正时间表中的错误，避免进一步延误。
Redress 纠正
eg：The company took steps to redress the issues raised in the customer survey.
公司采取措施纠正客户调查中提出的问题。
Troubleshoot 排查故障
eg：IT support is available 24/7 to troubleshoot system issues.
IT 支持全天候提供，以排查系统问题。
Circumvent 绕开
eg：I circumvented the problem by finding an alternative solution.
我找了个替代方案，绕开了这个问题。
Work around 设法避开
eg：We need to work around the delay caused by the system outage.
我们得想办法应对系统故障造成的延误。
Work through 解决问题
eg：When conflicts arose between departments, I facilitated discussions to work through the issues collaboratively.
部门间有冲突时，我推动大家讨论，一起解决问题。
Eliminate（关联词汇：Root out 彻底清除 / 查明）
eg：The audit team was brought in to root out any inefficiencies in the system.
审计团队被请来彻底找出系统中的低效问题。
Avoid（关联词汇：Refrain from 避免）
eg：Please refrain from discussing outside life during work hours.
工作时间请不要聊私事。
Halt 暂停
eg：We had to halt the project temporarily due to funding issues.
由于资金问题，我们不得不暂时暂停项目。
七、总结与评估
Conclude（关联词汇：Take stock of 总结 / 盘点）
eg：Before setting new goals, it's important to take stock of last year's achievements.
制定新目标前，总结去年的成就是很重要的。
Evaluate（关联词汇：Gauge 评估）
eg：I gauged the effectiveness of career development initiatives by tracking employee progress over time.
我通过长期跟踪员工进展，评估职业发展计划的效果。
Analyze（关联词汇：Drill down into 深入分析 / 细化）
eg：We need to drill down into the quarterly reports to identify key trends.
我们得深入分析季度报告，找出关键趋势。
Quantify 量化
eg：We need to quantify the impact of the new policy on employee productivity.
我们需要量化新政策对员工生产力的影响。
Benchmark 设立基准
eg：I benchmarked our project performance against industry standards to identify areas for improvement.
我把我们的项目表现和行业标准对比，找出需要改进的地方。
Validate 验证
eg：The testing phase will validate whether the product meets safety standards.
测试阶段将验证产品是否符合安全标准。
Audit（关联词汇：Scrutinize 仔细检查）
eg：Auditors will scrutinize the financial records for any irregularities.
审计员将仔细检查财务记录以寻找任何违规之处。
Reflect on achievements 回顾成就
eg：This holiday season is a perfect time to reflect on our achievements over the past year.
这个假期是回顾过去一年成就的好时机。
其他高频场景词汇

eg：I align my daily tasks with the project's long-term goals to ensure strategic focus.
我会把日常任务和项目的长期目标对齐，确保方向不跑偏。
Amplify 放大
eg：The campaign was designed to amplify the company's presence in the global market.
这个活动旨在扩大公司在全球市场的影响力。
Anticipate 预见
eg：The CEO anticipated market shifts and adjusted the company's strategy to maintain competitiveness.
首席执行官预见了市场变化，调整了公司战略以保持竞争力。
Aggregate into 汇总成
eg：The system aggregates raw data into a structured report for better analysis.
系统会把原始数据汇总成结构化报告，方便更好地分析。
Categorize 分类
eg：I categorized the files to streamline the retrieval process.
我把文件分了类，方便查找。
Cascade 逐级传递
eg：The project manager cascaded the new strategic goals across all teams to ensure alignment.
项目经理把新的战略目标逐级传达给所有团队，确保大家方向一致。

安保部门已开始严格管控员工之间的未经授权互动。

公司正在严厉打击违规行为。
Consolidate 合并 / 整合

我合并了项目任务，简化工作，减少重复劳动。
Coordinate 协调

我协调了我们和各部门的项目时间，确保合作顺畅。
Correlate 关联 / 相关
eg：I correlate employee performance metrics with training outcomes to assess effectiveness.
我把员工绩效数据和培训成果关联起来，评估培训效果。
Decentralize 去中心化
eg：The organization decentralized decision-making to empower regional teams.
公司推行决策去中心化，让区域团队更有自主权。
Decipher 解读 / 破译
eg：I decipher employee feedback to identify trends and areas for improvement in development programs.
我解读员工反馈，找出发展计划中的趋势和需要改进的地方。
Delineate 描绘 / 划定
eg：I delineated the project's scope to ensure we stayed within the time limits.
我划定了项目范围，确保我们在时间限制内完成。
Deconstruct 解构
eg：The team deconstructed complex workflows to identify inefficiencies.
团队拆解了复杂的工作流程，找出低效的环节。
Disband 解散
eg：I disbanded the task force after the project was successfully completed.
项目成功完成后，我解散了任务小组。
Diversify 多样化
eg：The company decided to diversify its product offerings to attract a broader customer base.
公司决定丰富产品种类，吸引更多客户。
Double down on 加倍努力 / 坚持到底
eg：Let's double down on stakeholder engagement this month.
这个月我们要加大力度和利益相关方沟通。
Embed 深植
eg：We embedded customer-centric values into every strategic initiative.
我们把以客户为中心的理念融入每一项战略计划中。
Empower 授权
eg：We must empower middle management to make quicker decisions to accelerate project delivery.
我们得给中层管理授权，让他们能更快做决定，加快项目交付。
Empower teams 赋能团队
eg：I aim to empower teams by delegating responsibilities and encouraging autonomy.
我的目标是通过分配责任和鼓励自主，给团队赋能。
Encapsulate 概括 / 总结
eg：I encapsulated the employee's progress in a development report to provide a clear path forward.
我在发展报告中总结了员工的进展，给他们指明了清晰的方向。
Encourage 鼓励
eg：I encouraged employees to set challenging career goals and provided the necessary resources for them to succeed.
我鼓励员工制定有挑战性的职业目标，并提供必要的资源帮他们成功。
Enforce upon 强制执行
eg：These policies are enforced upon all employees without exception.
这些政策无例外地对所有员工强制执行。
Envision 设想
eg：The CEO envisioned a future where the company leads in sustainable innovation.
CEO 设想了一个公司在可持续创新领域领先的未来。
Extend our gratitude to 向…… 表达感激之情
eg：We would like to extend our gratitude to all team members for their hard work this year.
我们想向所有团队成员表达感谢，感谢他们今年的辛勤付出。
Extrapolate 推断
eg：I extrapolated future challenges based on current data trends.
我根据当前数据趋势，推断了未来可能的挑战。
Extrapolate from 从…… 推断
eg：We can extrapolate from past trends to predict next quarter's performance.
我们可以从过去的趋势推断下一季度的业绩。
Elicit 引出
eg：The manager used open-ended questions to elicit feedback from the team.
经理用开放式问题引出团队的反馈。
Enlist 争取支持
eg：We need to enlist the IT department's support for the system upgrade.
我们需要争取 IT 部门对系统升级的支持。
Facilitate 促进 / 推动
eg：I facilitated time management workshops to improve the team's efficiency.
我组织了时间管理工作坊，提高团队效率。
Flesh out 具体化 / 丰富细节
