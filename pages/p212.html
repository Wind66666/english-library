商务沟通篇 (Communication)
1. Could you please clarify what you mean? 你能解释一下你的意思吗?
2. Let's schedule a meeting to discuss further. 我们安排个会议来进一步讨论。
3. I will get back to you as soon as possible. 我会尽快回复你。
4. Can we take this offline and discuss in more detail? 我们可以私下详细讨论这个问题吗?
5. I'm afraid I don't have the information right now. 恐怕我现在没有相关信息。
6. Let me know if you need anything else. 如果你需要其他帮助，告诉我。
7. Please find the attached document for your reference. 请查收附件文件供您参考。
8. I'll follow up with you tomorrow. 我明天会跟进。
9. Could you provide more context on this matter? 你能提供更多背景信息吗?
10. Thank you for bringing this to my attention. 感谢你让我注意到这一点。
会议场景篇 (Meetings)
11. Let's start the meeting. 让我们开始会议吧。
12. I'd like to add something to the agenda. 我想在议程中增加一点内容。
13. Could you please speak a little slower? 你可以说慢一点吗?
14. Let's summarize the key points from the meeting. 让我们总结一下会议的要点。
15. Does anyone have any questions or concerns? 有人有任何问题或疑虑吗?
16. Can we circle back to this later? 我们可以稍后再讨论这个问题吗?
17. Let's table this discussion for now. 我们暂时搁置这个讨论。
18. Could you share the minutes of the meeting afterward? 你能在会后分享会议记录吗?
19. I appreciate everyone's input today. 感谢大家今天的贡献。
20. The meeting is adjourned. 会议结束。

29. What's the next step in the process? 下一步流程是什么?
30. Could you provide a status update? 你能提供一下最新进展吗?

31. How can I assist you today? 我今天能为您提供什么帮助?

41. We need to find a mutually beneficial solution. 我们需要找到一个互惠互利的解决方案。
