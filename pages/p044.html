Workplace Small Talk 600 Essential Spoken English for Foreign Companies 职场Small Talk 外企闲聊必备口语600句 一、基础问候与开场 "How has your day been so far?"（你今天过得怎么样？） "How was your weekend?"（周末过得如何？） "Hi, [Name]! How are you?"（嗨，[名字]！你好吗？） "I am doing great! How about you?"（我挺好的！你呢？） "I am doing okay. Thank you! How have you been?"（我还不错。谢谢！你最近怎么样？） "Not so bad."（还不算差。） "How's it going?"（最近怎么样？） "How've you been?"（你近来可好？） "I don't think we've met before. I'm [Your Name]."（我们好像还没见过。我是 [你的名字]。） "Is this your first time here?"（你是第一次来这儿吗？） "What brings you here today?"（今天是什么风把你吹来的？） "Mind if I join you?"（我可以加入你们吗？） 二、短时偶遇场景 1. 电梯 / 走廊（3-5 秒速聊） "Finally Friday! Any fun plans to escape this heat?"（终于周五了！这么热的天有 “逃跑计划” 吗？） "Love your sneakers! Are they as comfy as they look?"（这双运动鞋太帅了！好穿吗？） "That meeting ran so long.. need coffee to reboot!"（会开太久了.. 急需咖啡续命！） "Hey! I think we've passed each other a few times - I'm [Your Name], by the way."（嘿！我们好像碰到过几次 —— 对了，我是 [你的名字]。） "Ah, cool. I'm over in [Your Department]. Small world in a big building!"（哇，不错。我在 [你的部门]。这么大的楼，世界真小！） 2. 会议开始前（避免冷场） "Looks like we're a few minutes early. Always better than rushing in late, right?"（看来我们早到了几分钟。总比慌慌张张迟到好，对吧？） "Same here. I was just thinking I should've grabbed a coffee on the way - do you have a favorite spot nearby?"（我也是。我刚才还在想，路上应该买杯咖啡的 —— 你有附近喜欢的咖啡店吗？） "Saw your slides - that chart on slide 5 was genius!"（刚看了你的 PPT—— 第 5 页的图表绝了！） "10AM meetings should be illegal.. thanks for the coffee!"（早 10 点的会简直 “反人类” 啊... 感恩咖啡救命！） "Your background is [Place]? Jealous! Best [Spot] there?"（你（视频）背景是 [地方] 吗？慕了！那儿最棒的 [地点，如海滩 / 餐厅] 是哪里？） 3. 远程会议早到 "Hi there! Looks like we're a bit early."（嗨！看来我们都早到了一会儿。） "Yeah, just trying to stay ahead of things today."（是啊，就是想今天把事情赶在前头。） "I know the feeling. Do you usually work from home or mix it up?"（我懂这种感觉。你通常居家办公，还是混合办公呀？） "Your plant's thriving! Is it low-maintenance?"（你的植物长得真好！是 “懒人款”（不用费心照顾）吗？） "If I freeze again, I'm blaming my cat on the router!"（等会卡了可别怪我 —— 我家猫在啃路由器呢！） "9PM for you? Appreciate you burning the midnight oil!"（你那边已经晚上 9 点了？感谢你挑灯夜战啊！） 三、中时交流场景 1. 茶水间 "Trying the new matcha powder? It's my secret focus booster!"（在试新抹茶粉？这是我的 “专注神器”！） "The water cooler's moody today.. fridge water is colder though!"（饮水机又闹脾气了.. 不过冰箱里的水更冰哦！） "Quick poll: Sugar in coffee - yes or no?"（紧急调研：咖啡加糖派还是无糖派？） "That smells amazing - homemade or takeout?"（这味道也太香了吧 —— 是自己做的还是外卖呀？） "Leftovers from last night - Thai curry."（昨晚的剩菜 —— 泰式咖喱。） "Delicious. I keep saying I'll learn to cook more but end up rotating three dishes every week."（太香了。我总说要多学几道家常菜，结果每周还是翻来覆去吃那三样。） 2. 商务社交活动 "Hi, I'm [Your Name]. Mind if I join you?"（嗨，我是 [你的名字]。我能加入你们吗？） "Not at all! I'm [Their Name]."（当然可以！我是 [对方名字]。） "Nice to meet you. What brings you to the event - are you in the same industry?"（很高兴认识你。你是为什么来参加这个活动呀 —— 咱们是同一个行业的吗？） "Yeah, I work in [Your Industry]! What about you—do you specialize in a specific area here?"（对呀，我在 [你的行业] 工作！你呢，在这一行有专门深耕的领域吗？） "Oh, [Their Industry] sounds interesting! Have you been part of this field for a long time?"（哇，[对方的行业] 听起来好有意思！你在这个领域已经做了很久了吗？） "I just started last year, still learning a lot. Did you come to this event for networking, or more for the talks?"（我去年才刚入行，还在学很多东西。你过来参加这个活动，主要是为了拓展人脉，还是更想听听讲座呀？） "Networking was my main goal too—so far, everyone here has been really friendly. Have you met anyone cool before me?"（拓展人脉也是我的主要目的 —— 目前来看，这儿的人都特别友好。在我之前，你有遇到什么有趣的人吗？） "That’s awesome! I haven’t run into many people from [Target Field] yet—do you know if there are more folks like us here?"（太厉害了！我到现在还没碰到几个 [目标领域] 的人 —— 你知道这儿还有更多和咱们同领域的人吗？） "I heard the keynote later is about [Keynote Topic]—are you planning to stick around for that?"（我听说待会儿的主旨演讲是关于 [演讲主题] 的 —— 你打算留下来听吗？） "Me too! Maybe we can grab a coffee before it starts, and chat more about what we’ve been working on lately?"（我也打算听！说不定咱们可以在开始前先去买杯咖啡，多聊聊最近在忙的项目呀？） 3. 社交聚会 "How do you know the host?"（你是怎么认识主办方 / 主人的呀？） "We used to work together a few years back."（几年前我们一起工作过。） "Nice! Always interesting how people stay connected through work. I'm a friend from [e.g., their yoga class]."（真好！人们通过工作保持联系，这点总很有意思。我是她 [比如：瑜伽课上] 认识的朋友。） "Yoga class connections are the best—such a relaxed way to meet people! Has she been doing yoga for as long as you have?"（瑜伽课上认识的朋友最棒了，这种认识人的方式特别轻松！她练瑜伽的时间和你一样久吗？） "I actually just started going with her last month—still getting the hang of it! Did you two work on any fun projects back when you were colleagues?"（其实我上个月才刚开始跟她一起去练，还在慢慢适应呢！你们以前当同事的时候，一起做过什么有意思的项目吗？） "Oh, that client project sounds intense but rewarding! Do you still collaborate with her on work stuff sometimes now?"（哇，那个客户项目听起来虽然有挑战，但肯定很有成就感！你们现在有时候还会在工作上合作吗？） "We only catch up over coffee now, no work stuff anymore. How did you hear about this event, since you know her through yoga?"（我们现在就偶尔约咖啡聊聊，不聊工作啦。你既然是通过瑜伽认识她的，那是怎么知道这个活动的呀？） "She mentioned it last week and said I’d love the vibe—turns out she was right! Have you been to any of her hosted events before?"（她上周提过这个活动，还说我肯定会喜欢这儿的氛围，结果真被她说中了！你以前参加过她主办的活动吗？） "This is my first one too—so far, I’m loving how casual and not too ‘network-y’ it is. Have you tried any of the snacks they’re serving over there?"（这也是我第一次来，目前感觉特别轻松，没有那种刻意 “搞人脉” 的压力。你尝过那边摆的点心了吗？） "I haven’t yet—let’s go check them out together! Do you have a favorite type of snack you’re hoping to find?"（我还没呢！咱们一起去看看吧！你有没有特别想吃到的点心呀？） 四、长时深度交流 午餐社交 "Hidden gem near office: The pasta place behind [Landmark, e.g., HSBC]!"（公司附近的宝藏小店：[地标，如汇丰银行] 后面那家意面店！） "You're from [Place, e.g., Mumbai]? I tried making [Local Food, e.g., butter chicken] - disaster!"（你是 [地方，如孟买] 来的？我试着做了 [当地菜，如奶油鸡]—— 大型翻车现场！） "Meal prepping too? Share your veggie hack please!"（你也带饭呀？求分享蔬菜保鲜秘籍！） "Have you tried the new coffee shop downstairs? Their cold-brew coffee is my favorite now!"（你试过楼下那家新咖啡店吗？他们的冷萃咖啡现在是我的最爱！） "Do you have any lunch spot recommendations? I am kind of tired of cafeteria food."（你有推荐的午餐地点吗？我已经有点吃腻食堂了。） "Oh, I totally get that—cafeteria food gets boring fast! There’s a tiny sandwich shop two blocks away that makes fresh paninis—you should try their turkey avocado one!"（太懂了，食堂的饭很快就吃腻了！两个街区外有家小三明治店，现做的帕尼尼超好吃，你一定要试试他们的火鸡牛油果口味！） "I made too much homemade soup this morning—want a container? It’s tomato basil, super easy to heat up in the office microwave."（我今早熬的家常汤太多了，要不要拿一份？是番茄罗勒味的，在公司微波炉里热一下就行。） "You like cold-brew? The convenience store next to the subway station has a cheaper version that’s just as good—way better than spending $5 every day!"（你喜欢冷萃呀？地铁站旁边的便利店有款平价的，味道一点不差，比每天花 5 美元买划算多了！） "I’m from [Place, e.g., Seoul]—butter chicken sounds tricky! If you want, I can send you a simple recipe for [Local Food, e.g., kimchi fried rice] that even beginners can’t mess up."（我是 [地方，如首尔] 来的，奶油鸡听起来确实难搞！要是你想试，我可以发你一个 [当地菜，如泡菜炒饭] 的简易食谱，新手都不会翻车。） "That pasta place behind HSBC? I went there last week—their truffle pasta is insane! Just a heads up, they get busy at noon, so you might want to go 15 minutes early."（汇丰银行后面那家意面店？我上周去过，他们的松露意面好吃到爆！提醒你一句，中午人特别多，最好提前 15 分钟去。） 五、通用话题库 1. 天气话题 "Beautiful day, isn't it?"（天气真好，对吧？） "Oh my god, it's freezing outside today, isn't it? Your coat looks really cozy!"（天呐，今天外面也太冷了吧，对吧？你的外套看起来好暖和！） "So humid today! Do you feel that?"（今天好潮湿啊！你感觉到了吗？） "I just came back for lunch. It's like 100 degrees outside - I was melting."（我刚吃完午饭回来。外面简直有 100 度 —— 我都要热融化了。） "It's a nice weather outside. Do you have any plans after work?"（外面天气真好。你下班后有安排吗？） "Can you believe this weather? It's so gorgeous outside!"（你能相信这天气吗？外面也太美了吧！） "I heard it's going to rain all week. Bring your umbrella!"（我听说这周都会下雨。记得带伞！） "This sunshine is making me feel so much better."（这阳光让我心情好多了。） "Looks like we're in for a heatwave this summer."（看来今年夏天会是热浪滚滚啊。） "The weather forecast says it might snow tomorrow. Exciting!"（天气预报说明天可能会下雪 —— 太令人兴奋了！） "I heard it might rain later. Did you bring an umbrella?"（听说待会儿可能会下雨，你带伞了吗？） "It's been so hot lately. How are you dealing with it?"（最近天气太热了，你是怎么应对的？） "The weekend was so sunny. Did you get to go outside?"（周末阳光这么好，你有出去玩儿吗？） 2. 假期与旅行 "Anything planned for the holidays?"（假期有什么安排吗？） "Any upcoming trips you're excited about?"（有什么即将到来、让你期待的旅行吗？） "How's your holiday going? Did you take any vacations?"（你假期过得怎么样？去旅行了吗？） "Have you been anywhere interesting recently?"（最近去过什么有意思的地方吗？） "Do you have any trips planned this year?"（今年有计划去哪里旅行吗？） "What's your favorite holiday destination?"（你最喜欢的度假胜地是哪里？） "I just got back from a trip. Have you been anywhere recently?"（我刚从旅行回来，你最近有去哪里吗？） "If you could travel anywhere right now, where would you go?"（如果现在能去任何地方旅行，你会选哪里？） "Do you prefer beach vacations or city trips?"（你更喜欢海边度假还是城市旅行？） "Any recommendations for a weekend getaway?"（有什么周末短途旅行的推荐吗？） "Best souvenir from your last trip? Mine was [e.g., spicy chocolate]!"（上次旅行最棒的纪念品是什么？我的是 [比如：辣味巧克力]！） 3. 周末计划 "Happy Friday! Any fun plans to escape this heat?"（周五快乐！这么热的天有 “逃跑计划” 吗？） "Is anything planned for the weekend?"（周末有什么安排吗？） "Do you have any exciting plans for the weekend?"（周末有什么令人兴奋的计划吗？） "Good morning, how was your weekend? Did you do anything fun?"（早上好，你周末过得怎么样？玩得开心吗？） "Oh, that sounds lovely. Which park did you go for camping?"（哇，听起来好棒。你去哪个公园露营了呀？） "So, what are you up to this weekend?"（那么，你这个周末打算干什么？） "Are you doing anything special for the holidays?"（假期你有什么特别的安排吗？） "I'm thinking of catching up on some reading. What about you?"（我想补读一些书。你呢？） "Planning any trips or just relaxing at home?"（打算出去旅行还是就在家放松？） "I'm looking forward to a lazy weekend. How's your schedule?"（我期待着一个慵懒的周末。你的安排呢？） "Have you checked out that new restaurant downtown? We're thinking of trying it."（你去过市中心那家新开的餐厅吗？我们打算去试试。） "I'm thinking of going to the beach. How about you?"（我打算去海边，你呢？） "I'm planning to catch up on some sleep. It's been a busy week!"（我打算补补觉，这周太忙了！） "I might go hiking if the weather is nice. Wanna join?"（如果天气好，我可能会去爬山，一起吗？） "Weekends are perfect for some Netflix binge watching, right?"（周末就是刷 Netflix 的好时光，对吧？） 4. 工作相关 "How's the project you're working on? Any progress?"（你正在做的项目怎么样了？有进展吗？） "I'm swamped with work today. How about you?"（我今天忙得不可开交。你呢？） "Did you catch the latest email from the boss? Thoughts?"（你看到老板最新的邮件了吗？有什么想法吗？） "I'm struggling with this task. Could you lend me a hand?"（这个任务我很难搞定。你能帮我一下吗？） "Have you finished your report? Mind sharing some tips?"（你完成报告了吗？能分享一些技巧吗？） "I'm excited for the team building event this Friday. Are you?"（我很期待周五的团队建设活动。你呢？） "What's the most challenging part of your job right now?"（你现在工作中最具挑战性的部分是什么？） "Any exciting projects you're working on?"（最近在做什么有趣的项目吗？） "Do you need any help with that?"（需要帮忙吗？） "I heard you did a great job on that presentation. Good work!"（听说你在那个汇报上做得很棒，干得不错！） "How do you usually handle tight deadlines?"（你通常怎么应对紧张的截止日期？） "What do you think of the new project we're starting?"（你觉得我们新开始的项目怎么样？） "Have you worked with this team before?"（你之前和这个团队合作过吗？） "Industry news"（行业新闻） "Conferences or trainings"（会议或培训） "Commute or work-from-home setups"（通勤方式或居家办公设备） 5. 生活与兴趣 "Have you seen any good shows or movies recently?"（最近看了什么好看的剧或电影吗？） "Do you have any hobbies or passions outside of work?"（你工作之外有什么爱好或热衷的事吗？） "I've recently started painting. Have you tried any new hobbies?"（我最近开始画画了。你尝试过什么新爱好吗？） "I'm a big fan of hiking. Do you enjoy outdoor activities?"（我很喜欢徒步旅行。你喜欢户外活动吗？） "Have you read any good books lately? Recommendations?"（你最近读过什么好书吗？有推荐吗？） "I love cooking. What's your favorite dish to make?"（我喜欢做饭。你最喜欢做什么菜？） "I'm always looking for new music. Any artists you'd suggest?"（我一直在寻找新音乐。有什么推荐的歌手吗？） "Do you follow any sports teams? Who's your favorite?"（你关注什么运动队吗？你最喜欢哪个？） "I'm into photography. Have you taken any great photos recently?"（我对摄影很感兴趣。你最近拍过什么好照片吗？） "What do you like to do in your free time?"（你平时喜欢做什么？） "I'm into hiking these days. What about you?"（我最近喜欢上了徒步，你呢？） "I heard you're a great cook. What's your favorite dish to make?"（听说你厨艺不错，你最喜欢做什么菜？） "Do you follow any sports?"（你平时关注什么运动吗？） "I've been reading this great book. Are you into reading?"（我最近在看一本很棒的书，你喜欢读书吗？） "Do you play any musical instruments?"（你会什么乐器吗？） "I just started learning yoga. It's tougher than thought!"（我刚开始学瑜伽，比我想象的难多了！） 6. 美食与饮品 "Food & restaurants"（美食与餐厅） "What's your go-to comfort food?"（你最喜欢的 “安慰食物”（难过时吃的食物）是什么？） "Have you tried any new restaurants lately?"（最近有尝试什么新餐厅吗？） "Do you prefer coffee or tea?"（你喜欢咖啡还是茶？） "I'm a big foodie. Are you into trying new cuisines?"（我是个吃货，你喜欢尝试新菜系吗？） "I made a great pasta dish last night. Do you like cooking?"（我昨晚做了道很棒的意大利面，你喜欢做饭吗？） "Any good coffee places around here?"（这附近有什么好喝的咖啡馆吗？） "What's your favorite dish to order when eating out?"（出去吃饭时你最爱点什么菜？） "I've been trying to eat healthier. Any tips?"（我最近在尝试健康饮食，有什么建议吗？） When you’re too tired to cook, what’s your go-to takeout order?（当你累得不想做饭时，最常点的外卖是什么？）​ I tried a new sushi spot downtown yesterday—their fatty tuna roll was amazing! Have you had good sushi lately?（我昨天去了市中心一家新开的寿司店，他们的金枪鱼腩寿司超赞！你最近吃过好吃的寿司吗？）​ Do you have a favorite type of tea, like green tea or chamomile?（你有特别喜欢的茶类吗，比如绿茶或者洋甘菊茶？）​ I’m terrible at baking, but I love eating pastries. Do you know any good bakeries nearby?（我烘焙超差，但超爱吃点心。你知道这附近有什么好的面包店吗？）​ When you’re feeling down, do you usually crave something sweet or savory as comfort food?（当你情绪低落时，作为安慰食物，你通常更想吃甜的还是咸的？）​ I’ve been wanting to try Thai food—do you have a favorite Thai restaurant to recommend?（我一直想试试泰国菜，你有推荐的泰国餐厅吗？）​ Do you prefer making breakfast at home or grabbing something on the way to work?（你更喜欢在家做早餐，还是上班路上随便买点？）​ I had a really spicy Sichuan hot pot last weekend—my mouth was on fire but I couldn’t stop eating! Are you a fan of spicy food?（我上周末吃了超辣的四川火锅，嘴巴都快烧起来了但还是停不下来！你喜欢吃辣吗？）​ Is there a coffee drink you always order, like a latte or an Americano?（你有没有每次都点的咖啡饮品，比如拿铁或者美式？）​ I started growing my own herbs on the windowsill—they make homemade salads taste so much better!
