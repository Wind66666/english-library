I hold a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science and have spent the last six years working in software development. I've had the opportunity to work on various projects, from developing mobile applications to leading a team of developers in my previous role at XYZ Company. I'm known for my problem-solving skills and my ability to work collaboratively with cross-functional teams. Outside of work, I'm passionate about volunteering for coding bootcamps, where I mentor aspiring developers." 答案中文： “当然，我很乐意。我拥有计算机科学学士学位，过去六年一直从事软件开发工作。我有机会参与了各种项目，从开发移动应用程序到在之前任职的 XYZ 公司带领开发团队。我以自己的问题解决能力以及与跨职能团队协作的能力而著称。在工作之余，我热衷于为编程训练营做志愿者，在那里我指导有抱负的开发人员。” 问题：Why should we hire you? 答案英文： "You should hire me because I bring a unique combination of technical expertise, leadership experience, and a proven track record of delivering results. In my previous role at ABC Inc., I not only led a team that completed a critical project ahead of schedule but also identified and implemented process improvements that saved the company 20% in operational costs. My ability to collaborate effectively and my commitment to continuous learning make me a valuable asset to any team." 答案中文： “你们应该雇佣我，因为我具备独特的技术专长、领导经验，并且有取得成果的可靠记录。在我之前任职于 ABC 公司的岗位上，我不仅带领团队提前完成了一个关键项目，还识别并实施了流程改进措施，为公司节省了 20% 的运营成本。我有效协作的能力以及对持续学习的承诺使我成为任何团队的宝贵资产。” 问题：What's your greatest strength? 答案英文： "One of my greatest strengths is my adaptability. I thrive in dynamic work environments and have demonstrated the ability to learn new technologies consistently and quickly. For example, at my previous job, I was asked to lead a project that involved using a programming language I had never worked with before. I immersed myself in learning it and successfully delivered the project on time." 答案中文： “我最大的优点之一是我的适应能力。我在动态的工作环境中表现出色，并且一直展示出能够快速学习新技术的能力。例如，在我之前的工作中，我被要求领导一个项目，该项目涉及使用一种我从未接触过的编程语言。我全身心投入学习该语言，并按时成功交付了项目。” 问题：What's your greatest weakness? 答案英文： "I used to struggle with delegating tasks, often taking on too much myself. However, I recognized this as a weakness and actively worked on improving my delegation skills. I now understand the importance of empowering team members and have seen how it enhances both productivity and team morale." 答案中文： “我过去在分配任务方面存在困难，常常自己承担太多工作。然而，我认识到这是一个缺点，并积极努力提高我的任务分配技能。我现在明白赋予团队成员权力的重要性，并且已经看到这如何提高了生产力和团队士气。” 问题：Can you describe a challenging situation you faced at work and how you handled it? 答案英文： "Certainly. In my previous role, we faced a tight deadline to complete a project with a high degree of complexity. The team was under immense pressure, and conflicts started to arise. I took the initiative to organize a team meeting, where we openly discussed our concerns and brainstormed solutions. By fostering open communication and reassigning tasks based on team members' strengths, we not only met the deadline but also improved team dynamics." 答案中文： “当然可以。在我之前的工作中，我们面临着在紧迫的期限内完成一个高度复杂项目的情况。团队承受着巨大的压力，并且开始出现冲突。我主动组织了一次团队会议，在会上我们公开讨论了我们的担忧并集思广益寻求解决方案。通过促进开放的沟通并根据团队成员的优势重新分配任务，我们不仅按时完成了任务，还改善了团队氛围。” 问题：Where do you see yourself in 5 years? 答案英文： "In five years, I see myself in a leadership role within the company, possibly in a senior project management position. I'm dedicated to continuous growth and would like to leverage my experience to mentor and lead teams to success while contributing to the company's long-term goals." 答案中文： “五年内，我希望自己在公司中担任领导职务，可能是高级项目管理职位。我致力于持续成长，并希望利用我的经验指导和带领团队取得成功，同时为公司的长期目标做出贡献。” 问题：Why did you leave your last job? 答案英文： "I left my last job because I felt that I had outgrown the opportunities it offered. I was seeking new challenges and a role that aligned better with my career goals. I wanted to contribute my skills and expertise to a company where I could make a more significant impact, which is why I'm excited about the opportunity with your organization." 答案中文： “我离开上一份工作是因为我觉得我已经超越了它所提供的机会。我在寻找新的挑战以及一个更符合我职业目标的职位。我想把我的技能和专业知识贡献给一家我能够产生更大影响的公司，这就是为什么我对贵公司提供的机会感到兴奋。” 问题：Tell me about a time you failed and what you learned from it. 答案英文： "One notable failure occurred when I was managing a project that ended up exceeding the budget due to unforeseen challenges. It was a difficult situation, but I took full responsibility, communicated transparently with stakeholders, and devised a cost-cutting plan. This experience taught me the importance of thorough risk assessment and proactive problem-solving, skills I've since honed to prevent similar issues in the future." 答案中文： “一个值得注意的失败经历是，我在管理一个项目时，由于一些意外的挑战，最终项目超出了预算。这是一个艰难的情况，但我承担了全部责任，与利益相关者进行了透明的沟通，并制定了一个削减成本的计划。这次经历让我明白了全面风险评估和积极主动解决问题的重要性，从那以后，我不断磨练这些技能，以防止未来出现类似的问题。” 问题：How do you handle stress or pressure? 答案英文： "I handle stress and pressure by first staying organized and breaking down tasks into manageable steps. I also prioritize effectively and maintain open communication with my team to ensure everyone is aligned and informed. Additionally, I make it a point to take short breaks to recharge and maintain a healthy work-life balance." 答案中文： “我应对压力和紧张情绪的方法是，首先保持条理清晰，将任务分解为可管理的步骤。我还会有效地确定任务优先级，并与我的团队保持开放的沟通，以确保每个人都达成一致并了解情况。此外，我会特意安排短暂的休息来恢复精力，保持健康的工作与生活平衡。” 问题：Describe a situation where you had to work with a difficult coworker. 答案英文： "In a previous project, I had a coworker who was very set in their ways and resistant to new ideas. We were working on a software development project and I proposed a new approach to solving a particular problem that could have saved us a lot of time. However, this coworker immediately dismissed my idea without even considering it. Instead of getting frustrated or arguing, I decided to take a different approach. I scheduled a one-on-one meeting with this coworker to have a calm and rational discussion. I started by acknowledging their expertise and experience and then presented my idea again, but this time with more data and examples to support it. I also asked for their input and suggestions on how we could improve the idea. During the conversation, I listened carefully to their concerns and tried to understand their perspective. It turned out that they were worried about the potential risks associated with the new approach. I addressed their concerns by showing them how we could mitigate those risks and also pointed out the potential benefits of the new approach. By the end of the meeting, my coworker was more open to the idea and we were able to work together to develop a plan that incorporated both of our ideas. We presented the plan to the rest of the team and it was well-received. Through this experience, I learned the importance of being patient, respectful, and flexible when working with difficult coworkers. I also learned the value of effective communication and collaboration in overcoming challenges and achieving common goals." 答案中文： “在之前的一个项目中，我有一位同事非常固执己见，对新想法持抵触态度。我们当时在进行一个软件开发项目，我提出了一种新的方法来解决一个特定问题，这种方法本可以为我们节省很多时间。然而，这位同事甚至都没有考虑就直接否定了我的想法。 我没有感到沮丧或与之争论，而是决定采取不同的方法。我安排了与这位同事的一对一会议，进行冷静而理性的讨论。我首先认可了他们的专业知识和经验，然后再次提出我的想法，但这次提供了更多的数据和例子来支持它。我还询问了他们对于如何改进这个想法的意见和建议。 在谈话过程中，我仔细倾听了他们的担忧，并试图理解他们的观点。结果发现，他们担心新方法带来的潜在风险。我通过向他们展示我们如何减轻这些风险来解决他们的担忧，同时也指出了新方法的潜在好处。 会议结束时，我的同事对这个想法更加开放了，我们能够一起合作制定一个融合了我们双方想法的计划。我们将这个计划展示给团队的其他成员，并且得到了好评。通过这次经历，我明白了在与难相处的同事合作时，耐心、尊重和灵活的重要性。我也认识到了有效沟通和协作在克服挑战和实现共同目标方面的价值。 So, tell me a little about yourself. 说说你自己的情况吧。 You don’t need to explain everything from birth to present day. Relevant facts about education, your career and your current life situation are fine. 你不用把从出生到现在的所有事情都讲一遍，说说和教育、职业以及你目前生活状况相关的情况就行。 Why are you looking (or why did you leave you last job)? 你为什么在找工作呢（或者你为什么从上一份工作离职）？ This should be a straightforward question to answer, but it can trip you up. Presumably you are looking for a new job (or any job) because you want to advance your career and get a position that allows you to grow as a person and an employee. It’s not a good idea to mention money here, it can make you sound mercenary. And if you are in the unfortunate situation of having been downsized, If you were fired, you’ll need a good explanation. But once again, stay positive. 这个问题看似简单，却很容易让你栽跟头。一般来说，你找新工作（或者任何工作）是为了在职业上有所发展，获得一个能让自己作为个人和员工都有所成长的职位。这里最好别提到钱，不然会显得你很功利。要是你很不幸被裁员了，或者是被解雇了，那你得好好解释一下。但不管怎样，回答时都要保持积极的态度。 Tell me what you know about this company. 跟我讲讲你对这家公司的了解。 Do your homework before you go to any interview. you should know about the company or business you’re going to work for. Has this company been in the news lately? Who are the people in the company you should know about? Do the background work, it will make you stand out as someone who comes prepared, and is genuinely interested in the company and the job. 去面试前一定要做好功课。你得了解自己即将入职的公司或企业。这家公司最近有上新闻吗？公司里有哪些重要人物是你应该知道的？做好背景调查，这会让你显得有备而来，并且表明你是真的对这家公司和这份工作感兴趣。 Why do you want to work at X Company? 你为什么想在 X 公司工作？ This should be directly related to the last question. Any research you’ve done on the company should have led you to the conclusion that you’d want to work there. After all, you’re at the interview, right? Put some thought into this answer before you have your interview, mention your career goals and highlight forward-thinking goals and career plans. 这个问题和上一个问题密切相关。你对这家公司做的任何调查都应该让你得出自己想在这儿工作的结论。毕竟，你都来面试了，不是吗？面试前好好想想这个问题的答案，谈谈你的职业目标，突出具有前瞻性的目标和职业规划。 What relevant experience do you have? 你有哪些相关经验？ Hopefully if you’re applying for this position you have bags of related experience, and if that’s the case you should mention it all. But if you’re switching careers or trying something a little different, your experience may initially not look like it’s matching up. That’s when you need a little honest creativity to match the experiences required with the ones you have. People skills are people skills after all, you just need to show how customer service skills can apply to internal management positions, and so on. 如果你申请这个职位，希望你有丰富的相关经验，如果是这样，那就把这些经验都讲出来。但要是你正在转行，或者尝试一些不太一样的工作，你的经验乍一看可能不太匹配。这时候就需要你发挥点实事求是的创造力，把你现有的经验和岗位要求的经验联系起来。人际交往能力总归是人际交往能力，你只要展示出客户服务技能如何能应用到内部管理岗位上，诸如此类就行。 If your previous co-workers were here, what would they say about you? 要是你之前的同事在这儿，他们会怎么评价你？ Ok, this is not the time for full disclosure. If some people from your past are going to say you’re a boring A-hole, you don’t need to bring that up. Stay positive, always, and maybe have a few specific quotes in mind. “They’d say I was a hard worker” or even better “John Doe has always said I was the most reliable, creative problem-solver he’d ever met.” 这个问题可不能啥都如实说。要是以前有人会说你是个无趣的讨厌鬼，这种话就别说了。永远要保持积极的态度，心里可以想一些具体的好评。比如 “他们会说我工作很努力”，或者更好一点 “约翰・多伊一直说我是他见过最靠谱、最有创意的问题解决者”。 Have you done anything to further your experience? 你有没有做过什么来提升自己的经验？ This could include anything from night classes to hobbies and sports. If it’s related, it’s worth mentioning. Obviously anything to do with further education is great, but maybe you’re spending time on a home improvement project to work on skills such as self-sufficiency, time management and motivation. 这可以包括从夜校课程到兴趣爱好、体育运动等任何事情。只要是相关的，都值得一提。显然，任何和继续教育有关的事情都很不错，但也可能你花时间做家庭装修项目，锻炼了自给自足、时间管理和自我激励等技能。 Where else have you applied? 你还申请了其他哪些公司？ This is a good way to hint that you’re in demand, without sounding like you’re whoring yourself all over town. So, be honest and mention a few other companies. don’t go into detail. The fact that you’re seriously looking and keeping your options open is what the interviewer is driving at. 这是个暗示自己很抢手的好办法，但又不会显得你在到处找工作。所以，诚实说出几家其他公司就行，别讲太细。面试官想知道的是你确实在认真找工作，并且没有把自己局限在一处。 How are you when you’re working under pressure? 你在压力下工作表现如何？ Once again, there are a few ways to answer and should all be positive. You may work well under pressure, you may thrive under pressure, and you may actually PREFER working under pressure. If you say you crumble like aged blue cheese, this is not going to help you get your foot in the door. 这个问题有好几种回答方式，但都得往积极的方向说。你可以说自己在压力下工作得很好，或者在压力下能超常发挥，甚至可以说自己实际上更喜欢在压力下工作。要是你说自己一有压力就像过期的蓝纹奶酪一样崩溃，那可不利于你获得这份工作。 What motivates you to do a good job? 是什么激励你把工作做好？ The answer to this one is not money, even if it is. You should be motivated by life’s noble pursuits. You want recognition for a job well done. You want to become better at your job. You want to help others or be a leader in your field. 这个问题的答案可别说是钱，哪怕真是钱也别这么说。你应该说是生活中的崇高追求激励着你。你希望自己出色完成工作后能得到认可，想要在工作中不断进步，想要帮助他人，或者成为所在领域的领导者。 What’s your greatest strength? 你最大的优点是什么？ This is your chance to shine. You’re being asked to explain why you are a great employee, so don’t hold back and stay do stay positive. You could be someone who thrives under pressure, a great motivator, an amazing problem solver or someone with extraordinary attention to detail. If your greatest strength, however, is to drink anyone under the table or get a top score on Mario Kart, keep it to yourself. The interviewer is looking for work-related strengths. 这是你展示自己的好机会。面试官是在问你为什么是一名优秀的员工，所以别谦虚，一定要保持积极的态度。你可以说自己在压力下表现出色、很擅长激励他人、是个厉害的问题解决者，或者是个特别注重细节的人。但要是你最大的优点是喝酒千杯不醉，或者玩《马里奥赛车》总能拿高分，这种话还是别说了。面试官想了解的是和工作相关的优点。 What’s your biggest weakness? 你最大的缺点是什么？ If you’re completely honest, you may be kicking yourself in the butt. If you say you don’t have one, you’re obviously lying. This is a horrible question and one that politicians have become masters at answering. They say things like “I’m perhaps too committed to my work and don’t spend enough time with my family.” Oh, there’s a fireable offense. I’ve even heard “I think I’m too good at my job, it can often make people jealous.” Please, let’s keep our feet on the ground. If you’re asked this question, give a small, work-related flaw that you’re working hard to improve. Example: “I’ve been told I occasionally focus on details and miss the bigger picture, so I’ve been spending time laying out the complete project every day to see my overall progress.” 要是你太实诚，可能会后患无穷。要是你说自己没有缺点，那显然是在撒谎。这是个很棘手的问题，而政客们最擅长回答这类问题。他们会说 “我可能太专注于工作，都没什么时间陪家人” 之类的话。这简直就是给自己挖坑。我甚至还听过 “我觉得我工作能力太强了，这经常招人嫉妒”。拜托，咱还是实际点吧。要是被问到这个问题，就说一个和工作相关的小缺点，并且表明自己正在努力改进。比如：“有人说我有时候太关注细节，忽略了大局，所以我现在每天都会花时间梳理整个项目，好了解整体进展。” Let’s talk about salary. What are you looking for? 咱们谈谈薪资吧。你期望的薪资是多少？ Run for cover! This is one tricky game to play in an interview. Even if you know the salary range for the job, if you answer first you’re already showing all your cards. You want as much as possible, the employer wants you for as little as you’re willing to take. Before you apply, take a look at salary.com for a good idea of what someone with your specific experience should be paid. You may want to say, “well, that’s something I’ve thought long and hard about and I think someone with my experience should get between X & Y.” Or, you could be sly and say, “right now, I’m more interested in talking more about what the position can offer my career.” That could at least buy you a little time to scope out the situation. But if you do have a specific figure in mind and you are confident that you can get it, I’d say go for it. I have on many occasions, and every time I got very close to that figure (both below and sometimes above). 这可真是个难题！在面试中谈薪资是个棘手的事。即便你知道这个岗位的薪资范围，要是你先回答，就等于暴露了自己的底线。你想拿尽可能多的钱，而雇主则想以你能接受的最低薪资聘用你。在申请工作前，可以去salary.com网站看看，了解一下像你这种经验水平的人该拿多少工资。你可以说：“这个问题我想了很久，我觉得以我的经验，薪资应该在 X 到 Y 之间。” 或者，你也可以耍点小聪明，说：“目前，我更想多了解一下这个岗位能给我的职业发展带来什么。” 这样至少能为你争取点时间，好摸清情况。但要是你心里已经有了一个具体的数字，并且有信心能拿到，那我觉得不妨直接说出来。我就这么做过很多次，每次都能拿到和预期很接近的薪资（有时比预期低一点，有时还能高一点）。 Are you good at working in a team? 你擅长团队合作吗？ Unless you have the I.Q. of a houseplant, you’ll always answer YES to this one. It’s the only answer. How can anyone function inside an organization if they are a loner? You may want to mention what part you like to play in a team though; it’s a great chance to explain that you’re a natural leader. 除非你智商低得像盆植物，不然这个问题肯定得回答 “是”。这是唯一正确的答案。要是一个人不合群，在公司里怎么能好好工作呢？不过，你可以说说自己在团队中喜欢扮演什么角色，这可是个表明自己天生具有领导才能的好机会。 Tell me a suggestion you have made that was implemented. 说说你提出过的一个被采纳的建议。 It’s important here to focus on the word “implemented.” There’s nothing wrong with having a thousand great ideas, but if the only place they live is on your notepad what’s the point? Better still, you need a good ending. If your previous company took your advice and ended up going bankrupt, that’s not such a great example either. Be prepared with a story about an idea of yours that was taken from idea to implementation, and considered successful. 这里要重点突出 “被采纳” 这个词。有一千个好点子固然不错，但要是它们只停留在你的笔记本上，那有什么用呢？更重要的是，你得讲一个好的结局。要是你之前的公司采纳了你的建议，结果却破产了，这也不是个好例子。准备好一个故事，讲讲你提出的某个建议是如何从想法变成实际行动，并且取得成功的。 Has anything ever irritated you about people you’ve worked with? 和你一起工作的人有没有什么地方让你很恼火？ Of course, you have a list as long as your arm. Employers can always give you experience but few want to help you overcome shortcomings in your personality! So avoid telling interviewers that you "get bored" or "too involved" or "frustrated"! 最后要注意，把缺乏经验或知识当作缺点来谈，比暴露性格上的缺陷要稳妥得多。雇主通常可以给你提供积累经验的机会，但很少有人愿意帮你克服性格上的不足！所以不要告诉面试官你 “容易感到无聊”、“过于投入” 或者 “容易沮丧”！ 你为什么认为自己适合这份工作？ What they are really asking is "You know what we are looking for so can you tell us what you have done or what you have, that is relevant." 他们真正想问的是 “你知道我们在寻找什么样的人，那么你能告诉我们你做过什么，或者你具备哪些与之相关的能力和经验吗？” 我们为什么要录用你而不是其他应聘者？ This is often our closing question. Don't be cocky, but don't be shy. 这通常是我们面试的最后一个问题。简而言之，我们想知道为什么你是这份工作的最佳人选。这是一个列出你与该职位相关的最佳特质的机会。不要过于自大，但也不要过于害羞。 你在压力下工作表现如何？ “I know that all jobs involve some sort of pressure at some time. Just expand on this in your job interview answers. List the skills you think are required, giving a little explanation as to why each is needed. 你能进入面试环节，是因为你的求职申请表、简历或履历符合面试官在招聘广告中列出的标准。在面试回答中，你只需对此进行扩展。列出你认为所需的技能，并简要解释为什么需要这些技能。 You might then be asked to give examples where you've used one or more of these skills. When you answer, you want to sound ambitious enough to be motivated to do a good job, but not too ambitious in case your interviewers think you're only using this job as a stepping - stone to something better. 这可能是一个很难回答的面试问题，尤其是如果你除了得到这份工作之外，没有更多的职业规划！回答时，你要听起来有足够的雄心壮志，有动力把工作做好，但又不能显得过于野心勃勃，以免面试官认为你只是把这份工作当作通往更好职位的跳板。 I can be successful in this job and taken on additional responsibilities and be considered suitable for promotion. 我能在这份工作中取得成功，承担更多的责任，并获得晋升机会。 这份工作如何契合你的职业规划？ This is a similar job interview question to the one above really, except that it looks beyond five years.
