Point（重申观点）：Therefore, extending the deadline by 7 days will help the team catch up without compromising quality.（因此，延长 7 天截止日期能帮助团队赶上进度，且不影响质量。）

Point（观点）：Therefore, data visualization dashboards will 增强 user motivation, reduce early churn, and highlight the product’s value through tangible progress metrics.（因此，数据可视化看板将增强用户动力，减少早期流失，并通过可量化的进度指标凸显产品价值。）
场景：主张优化员工报销流程
Point（观点）：We should digitize the entire expense reimbursement process with a mobile app that allows photo uploads of receipts and automatic approval routing, replacing the current paper-based system.（我们应通过移动应用将整个费用报销流程数字化，支持收据照片上传和自动审批流转，取代当前的纸质系统。）
Reason（理由）：Admin efficiency data shows that the current process takes 7-10 days, with 30% of claims rejected due to missing information—employees spend 2+ hours per month on paperwork, and finance teams waste 15 hours/week processing physical receipts.（行政效率数据显示，当前流程需 7-10 天，30% 的报销因信息缺失被驳回 —— 员工每月花 2 + 小时处理 paperwork，财务团队每周浪费 15 小时处理纸质收据。）
Example（事例）：A case in point is the sales team—after testing the app, reimbursement time dropped to 2-3 days, rejection rates fell from 30% to 5%, and sales reps reported saving 5 hours/month; finance team processing time decreased by 80%.（销售团队的典型事例：测试应用后，报销时间缩短至 2-3 天，驳回率从 30% 降至 5%，销售代表报告每月节省 5 小时；财务团队处理时间减少 80%。）
Point（观点）：Thus, a digital 报销 system will reduce administrative burdens, speed up reimbursements, and improve employee satisfaction with internal processes.（因此，数字化报销系统将减轻行政负担，加快报销速度，并提升员工对内部流程的满意度。）
场景：建议调整产品试用版功能限制
Point（观点）：We should limit only advanced features in the free trial version, keeping core functions fully accessible, instead of restricting 50% of key features as we do now.（我们应仅在免费试用版中限制高级功能，保持核心功能完全可用，而非像现在这样限制 50% 的关键功能。）
Reason（理由）：Conversion data shows that trials with limited core features have a 40% lower conversion rate—users can’t experience the product’s full value, leading 60% of trial users to say “the trial didn’t show what the product can do.”（转化数据显示，限制核心功能的试用版转化率低 40%—— 用户无法体验产品的全部价值，60% 的试用用户表示 “试用版没展示产品的能力”。）

Point（观点）：Therefore, 合理的 trial restrictions will let users experience core value, increase conversion rates, and reduce the need for sales demos to prove basic functionality.（因此，合理的试用限制将让用户体验核心价值，提高转化率，并减少通过销售演示证明基本功能的需求。）
场景：主张开展客户成功案例视频拍摄

场景：建议优化供应商沟通频率
Point（观点）：We should schedule monthly check-ins with key suppliers (top 10 by spend) to review performance, address issues, and align on future needs, instead of communicating only when problems arise.（我们应每月与核心供应商（按支出前 10 名）召开跟进会议，回顾表现、解决问题并协调未来需求，而非仅在出现问题时沟通。）
Reason（理由）：Supplier relationship data shows that proactive communication reduces delivery delays by 40%—our current reactive approach has led to 3 last-minute shortages this quarter due to unforeseen supplier capacity issues.（供应商关系数据显示，主动沟通可减少 40% 的交货延误 —— 我们当前的被动方式导致本季度因供应商产能问题出现 3 次临时短缺。）
Example（事例）：For example, after starting monthly calls with Supplier Q (our largest raw material provider), we learned about their planned maintenance in advance, adjusted orders to avoid delays, and even negotiated a 5% discount for bulk pre-orders; delivery reliability improved from 85% to 98%.（例如，开始与最大原材料供应商 Q 每月通话后，我们提前得知其计划维护时间，调整订单避免延误，甚至通过批量预购谈判获得 5% 折扣；交货可靠性从 85% 提升至 98%。）
Point（观点）：Therefore, regular supplier check-ins will strengthen partnerships, reduce supply chain risks, and create opportunities for cost savings and better alignment.（因此，定期供应商跟进将加强合作关系，减少供应链风险，并创造成本节约和更好协作的机会。）
